## Register your number

1. Open **Favor Brain**, then **Text Favor Brain** at the bottom of the left column.
2. Enter your mobile number and press **Send code**.
3. Type the 6 digit code from the text and press **Confirm**. The code lasts 10 minutes.

Each person has one number, and one number belongs to one person. **Change number** replaces it. **Remove** stops answers to it.

## Ask by text

Text a question to the Favor number. The answer comes back as the first line, the key numbers (six rows at most) and a link that opens the full answer in FavorOS. Questions sent within 30 minutes of each other share one thread, so "and in October?" works as a follow-up.

For a file, say what you want: "Send me the Chad wells one-pager". The reply names the file and gives its Drive link, for files your Google account can open.

## What a text never includes

Your access is the same as on the Favor Brain page. Partner answers come only with the partner package, a text never lists individual gifts, and a text number that is not registered gets one reply pointing here.

Every question and answer is kept with your name. You can send up to 8 questions in 10 minutes and 80 a day.
