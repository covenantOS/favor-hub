Your first-day page opens from the link in your welcome email, or from **Welcome** under Help.

## Your steps

The top of the page lists the steps that are yours, such as finishing the HR paperwork on onboard.favorintl.org, reading this page and meeting your teammates. Each has a due date. Press the circle to mark a step done. The steps for the two Learn paths close by themselves when you finish the path.

## Your first week

Day by day, what you do, what your supervisor does and what training covers.

## People to meet

Your supervisor comes first, then the people who set up your accounts and training, then your team. Each shows a face, a title and why you will talk to them.

## The tour

Press **Take the FavorOS tour** for a guided walk through Today, requests, Chat, meetings and search. You can run it again from Help at any time.
