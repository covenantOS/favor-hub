Favor Brain answers questions about Favor in plain words. Ask "How many active partners do we have in North Carolina?" and the answer comes from Blackbaud, the KPI dashboard and Favor's manuals, counted with Favor's own definitions. **Brain** in the FavorOS menu opens it.

## Where you can ask

- **The Brain page.** A full-window chat. Type your question and press Enter, or press one of the starter cards. **What you can ask** under the cards asks "What can you do?". The box takes up to 600 characters. **Shift Enter** adds a line. While an answer is being built the send button becomes a stop button, and **Esc** stops it too.
- **Any page.** Select text in an email, a Chat message, a task, a request, meeting notes or a partner page and **Ask Favor Brain** appears beside the selection. It opens the Brain over the page you are on, with your selection as context. The round Chat button at the bottom right of every page also has a Favor Brain tab.
- **Chat.** Type **@Favor Brain** and a question in a channel or thread. The answer is private to you until you share it.
- **Your own Claude or ChatGPT.** Connect once (see below). Your app can compare two lists, combine answers and follow up on anything you ask.
- **By text.** See [Text Favor Brain](/help/text-favor-brain/).

## The Brain page

**Your chats** are on the left. The list keeps every conversation for 30 days. Search them with **Search your chats**. Point at a chat for three buttons: pin, rename and delete. Press the chat's title at the top to rename the open chat. **New chat** (**Ctrl Shift O**) starts a fresh one. The panel icon at the top left of the chat hides or shows the list. On a phone, **Ask** and **History** at the top switch between the chat and the list.

The icon at the top right hides the FavorOS menu so the chat gets the whole window. The three dots beside it hold **What you can ask about**, **Connect Claude or ChatGPT**, **Help: asking well**, **Keyboard shortcuts** and **Sounds**. Admins also see **Brain admin**. Keys: **Enter** sends, **Esc** stops an answer or closes what is open, **/** jumps to the question box.

**Your sheets** at the bottom of the list shows the Google Sheets you made from answers. **Connect Claude or ChatGPT** and **Text Favor Brain** sit below it.

## Reading an answer

Answers come back as cards, not only text: a partner card with giving by year, a team's year with its quarters and months, charts, tables, steps from the manuals with a link to the section, and your own day as lists (today's meetings with **Join**, unread mail, files that changed this week and your open tasks, each with an Open link). A count shows the partners behind it, and a total shows the gifts behind it. The line above the answer ("Read your question, checked the numbers") opens to show the steps it took.

Under each answer:

- **How I read it** shows the year, state, team or status it used. Press one to change it and the answer redraws.
- **Follow-up buttons** under the newest answer ask the next question for you. You can also type a few words, such as "show me them", "what about Texas?" or "only major partners".
- When a question could mean two things, you pick from buttons. **Something else** puts the cursor in the box.
- **Copy the answer**, **Copy link to this chat** (only you can open it), **Pin to My dashboard** (the answer appears on Highlights, under My dashboard, and Refresh there asks it again), **Right**, **Not right**, **Ask again**.
- **Send to email** puts the answer in a new email. Partner data goes only to people allowed to share it. You can also drag an answer into a Chat message, a task or an email.
- **Copy the question** and **Edit the question** sit beside your own question. Editing and pressing Enter replaces everything after it.

When the Brain cannot answer a question reliably, the card says **Let's take this one to Claude**. **Continue in Claude** opens Claude with your question and what the Brain found. **Rephrase** puts your question back in the box.

Tables have **Copy**, **CSV** and **Open in Google Sheets**, a filter box, sortable columns, and **Open large** or **Open full table** to read all rows in a side panel. The first time you press Open in Google Sheets, Google asks once for permission. The sheet lands in your own Drive and only you can open it until you share it. It holds partner information, so keep it inside Favor.

## What you can ask about

- **Favor-wide numbers** from the Executive tab, for everyone.
- **Your team's numbers**, the way your dashboard tab shows them.
- **Definitions and the manuals**: "What counts as a LYBUNT partner?", "How do I approve a gift batch?"
- **Your own day**: today's meetings, unread mail, recent files and your tasks.
- **Files in Favor's shared Drive**: "Find the 2026 budget spreadsheet" or "Do we have photos from the Uganda trip?" Each match is a card with the owner, the date, an Open in Drive button and the line that matched. **Indexed** with a date shows when the Brain last read the file. You see only files your own Google account can open.
- **Partner records and giving, lists and totals, grant opportunities and iWave ratings**, for the people who have those packages.

Press the three dots, then **What you can ask about**, to see your packages: Partners and giving, Contact details, iWave ratings, Every team's numbers, Google Sheets, and The manuals and your team. Each shows **Yours**, **Waiting for Will** or **Ask Will**. Press Ask Will, say why your work needs it and for how long, and press **Send the request**. Will decides.

## Connect Claude or ChatGPT

Until you connect, a banner at the top of the Brain page offers **Connect**, **Not now** (hidden for 24 hours) and **ChatGPT instead**. **Connect** opens the steps in a panel with the address to **Copy**; the same panel opens from `/brain/?connect=1`. The full steps are in [Connect Claude or ChatGPT](/help/connect-your-ai/). Every tenth answer ends with a short "Ask this in Claude next time" card until you connect.

## It is in beta

Most answers are right and some are off. When an answer is wrong, press **Not right** under it and say what you expected, or tell your app "that number is wrong because...". Your note goes to Will with the question and how it was read.

## For the Brain's admin

**Brain admin** (three dots menu, or Brain requests in Admin) has five tabs. **Requests** holds access requests waiting for a decision, the ones decided lately, and a form to give or remove a package for a person with an end date. **People** shows what each person can see. **Activity** lists every question, newest first. **Names** lists questions it could not place and names that missed, and teaches it what a phrase like "PBS Chad" means. **iWave** shows the credits, the screenings waiting for approval and the log. Each list can open as a Google Sheet.

## Keep partner data inside Favor

Don't download Blackbaud exports and paste them into another app. Ask Favor Brain instead; it hands lists back as a download or a Google Sheet in your own Drive. Don't share a list outside Favor without your director's okay.
