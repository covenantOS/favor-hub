The biggest changes have a walkthrough. FavorOS opens **New in FavorOS** for you when one ships, and **Help**, then **What's new and tour**, plays any of them again. Every change is listed here.

## October 12, 2026

- **What changed this week.** Pressing the year's raised number opens This week first: gifts and dollars against last week, the largest gifts and the new partners. A name opens the partner card, and an amount opens the gift on the partner page. See [Your Today page](/help/today/).
- **How a first-time partner found Favor.** Cadence shows Came in through on each first-time partner: the first gift's appeal, the website form for an online gift, and the source code once Blackbaud's copy has it. The First-time givers report has a Show: Each partner view with the same column. See [Lists](/help/lists/).
- **What a gift is for, from the record.** In Gift entry, a check that matches a partner is checked against that partner's calls, meetings, notes and opportunities for a gift someone said was coming. The fund the note describes and the caller's personal contact appeal are suggested with the reason, for example "Sep 28 call: She said a gift for the Chad wells is on its way". A person still confirms both. See [Gift entry](/help/gift-entry/).
- **Chat in the new FavorOS app.** With the new app on, Chat opens inside the app without a page load, keeps your place when you come back to it, and the Chat button on every page shows the same messages and message box as the Chat page, with reactions, cards, voice notes, threads and Favor Brain answers. Use classic FavorOS in the account menu goes back to the old page. See [Chat](/help/chat/).
- **Online gifts.** Under Work, Online gifts lists each gift from the website, the Blackbaud donation forms and international giving with the partner's comment and the designation read from it. Change the designation if it is wrong and press Approve, or Approve all. FavorOS records who approved each one. Batches still waiting in Blackbaud show at the top with a link, since they are approved in Blackbaud. See [Online gifts](/help/online-gifts/).
- **The 24-hour thank-you clock.** To thank on Today, the Work Overview and Gifts to thank list the newest gifts by the time left to thank them: 24 hours from entry in Blackbaud, weekdays counted. Each row reads Due in 6 h, Due today or Overdue in red, the soonest due first with the largest gift leading each day, and gifts more than a day past it fold under Older.
- **Receipts and thank-you letters are named apart.** The printed mailing every partner who gives gets is a receipt everywhere in FavorOS: the Receipts page, its messages, Learn quizzes and Today. A thank-you letter is the one from headquarters for a gift of $5,000 or more, on the HQTY letters tab of the Work Center.
- **Credit and assignments.** Reporting, Lists, Credit shows each gift's fundraiser credit and each partner's assignments with dates, proposes fixes by Favor's rules, and takes a second person's approval with a log of every step. Nothing changes in Blackbaud until an admin turns Apply on. See [Lists](/help/lists/).
- **Standard reports and mail-ready lists.** Reporting, Lists now has Standard reports with nine reports, a period picker, a check against the KPI dashboard and a drill to the rows. The Columns menu has a Mail house set, and the lists data refreshes itself within the hour after each Blackbaud sync. See [Lists](/help/lists/).
- **Lists.** Under Reporting, build a list of partners or gifts from menus (status, major, came in through, who holds the partner, place, giving, fund, appeal, codes, events), see the count change as you go, save it for yourself or a team, and download a CSV or open it in Google Sheets. See [Lists](/help/lists/).
- **Gift entry from a photo.** Photograph a check and its reply slip, or drop scans. FavorOS reads the amount, check number, date and name, matches the partner, and shows each gift beside its photo with the appeal and the reason for it (a reply slip, a letter, or White Mail when nothing came with the check). Press Approve, Hold or Reject (A, H and R on a keyboard). An admin can require a second person above an amount. Sending the batch to Blackbaud is off until an admin switches a person on, and a batch can be taken back the same day.
- **Text Favor Brain.** Under Favor Brain, **Text Favor Brain** registers your mobile number with a 6 digit code. Once the Favor text number opens, a question texted from that phone gets the first line of the answer, the key numbers and a link to the full answer here. A file request returns the file's name and its Drive link. Partner answers follow your access. See [Text Favor Brain](/help/text-favor-brain/).
- **Cleaner screens on a phone.** Rows of chips scroll sideways and no longer wrap, lists use small buttons so the name reads first, the partner page shows a Giving pattern card with dates in place of a paragraph, and the trip planner lists each stop in short lines with 12 hour times.
- **Work and Receipts open faster.** The Work Overview and the Work Center draw the last list you saw while the fresh one loads, with anything you finished since left off it, and tabs you have not opened read in the background one at a time. Receipts shows the last list from Blackbaud at once and refreshes it behind the page.
- **Undo is never refused.** Press Undo while a change is still going to Blackbaud and FavorOS accepts it, puts the row back in the list at once and sends the reversal as soon as the first change lands. The message at the bottom stays for 10 seconds with a large Undo, and Z presses it. Recent changes on the Work Overview lists the last 24 hours with an Undo on each change. See [Work Center](/help/work-center/).
- **FavorOS on a phone, and field staff.** Open dash.favorintl.org/m/ on a phone and add it to the Home Screen. A direct message or a mention sends a notification, and Data saver shrinks pictures, opens meetings as audio-only calls and holds messages written without a connection until it returns. Admins can let people on a partner domain such as favourafrica.org ask for access. Each new person waits under Admin, People and roles until an admin approves them, and an approved field person has Chat, Meetings and Favor Brain only, with no partner records, dashboards or Work Center. See [FavorOS on your phone and field staff](/help/phone-app/).
- **Highlights, pace to year end and My dashboard.** Press the year total or a team total on Today and the panel now says why it changed since last month, with this month's gifts by where they came from and the largest gifts, tied to the KPI dashboard's own figure for the month. Highlights, under Reporting, projects where each goal ends the year and says if it is ahead, close or behind, and writes the week in plain words for all of Favor and for each team you can open. Under any Favor Brain answer, the new pin button adds it to My dashboard, where Refresh asks it again with the latest numbers. See [Highlights, pace and My dashboard](/help/reporting/).
- **A first week built for each role, a practice record and evidence for evaluations.** The first-day page now lays out five days for the new hire's role, with each course ticking as it is finished in Learn, and Add to FavorOS assigns the role's Learn path. Practice, in the Learn menu, is a pretend partner where you add a gift in a form laid out like Blackbaud's, with Favor's real funds and appeals, and log a contact and a task as the Work Center does, with every field checked and nothing sent to Blackbaud. Evaluations has a tab for the year's records (tasks finished, partners contacted, courses finished) for the person and the reviewer. When people have nobody under Reports to, Evaluations asks an admin to fill it in. See [Learn](/help/learn/), [Onboarding](/help/onboarding/) and [Evaluations](/help/evaluations/).
- **A morning briefing on Today.** The top of Today lists gifts since the last workday from your partners, the gifts waiting for a thank-you with a Thank button, what is late, and today's meetings with one line of prep on each partner in the room. After a day or more away, Since you were away lists what changed in your partners, requests, projects and Chat. Moments suggest a call for a first gift, a largest gift, a giving anniversary or a partner who went quiet, and Not now hides one for two weeks. Focus shows only your next three things.
- **The story of a partner.** The partner page opens with what the story is in a few sentences, the partner's health (Growing, Steady, Slipping, Lapsing, At risk or New) and the next best action. The timeline now carries calls, emails, meetings, notes, events, Chat mentions, FavorOS meetings and your own clips beside the gifts.
- **Call mode.** Call mode on a partner shows the script, the giving, the last notes and a large Log it. Say or type what happened, FavorOS writes the summary and notes, and Log it saves a completed phone call on the partner with Undo.
- **Portfolio health and the trip planner.** My partners marks every partner by how they give, shows the next best action in each row and plans calls for a whole group. The trip planner takes a line such as "I'm in Dallas Thursday" and lists the partners nearby, a route and meeting slots, then adds the visits as tasks.
- **Meetings prepare you and finish the work.** A Prep button before each meeting lists who is coming, the partners among them with their giving, open tasks between you and each person, what is open from past meetings and what the last one decided. The Notes tab in a room catches action items and decisions as they are said. After the call, action items become tasks for their owners, the summary posts to the channel the host chose with each owner mentioned, and a meeting with a partner on the invite waits for the host to press Log in Blackbaud before anything is written. Ask your meetings answers a question from every past meeting you can open. See [Meetings](/help/meetings/).
- **Requests fill themselves out.** Make a request reads your words and answers the form: the kind, where it lives, a summary, who sees it and when it is needed, with the reason beside each. After you send it, four stops show where it is, who has it, the due date and what moved last, and they update while the page is open. A FavorOS admin sees a suggested owner and due date on the card with one button to use them. See [Make a request](/help/make-a-request/).
- **Receipts are checked before printing.** The check flags a ZIP or state that will not deliver, a street with no number, a blank or placeholder name after "Dear" and two records that look like one partner. **Needs a look** lists them, each with what to fix and a link to the record. Nothing is changed or marked. See [Receipts](/help/thank-you-receipts/).

- **Names open cards, and one bell holds everything.** Point at a colleague, partner, task code or request on any page to see its key facts and what you can do with it: message, make a task, ask Favor Brain, or Send to. Send to puts the thing in Chat as a card, a Projects task, a comment on a request, or a link. The bell now lists meetings starting soon, courses, evaluations, expense and receipt work, feedback answers and unread mail with your mentions and request notices, and Mark all read clears the ones the bell owns. Search, Favor Brain and Chat take ask links.
- **A first week built for each role, a practice record and evidence for evaluations.** The first-day page now lays out five days for the new hire's role, with each course ticking as it is finished in Learn, and Add to FavorOS assigns the role's Learn path. Practice, in the Learn menu, is a pretend partner where you enter a gift from a check and log a call, with every field checked and nothing sent to Blackbaud. Evaluations has a tab for the year's records (tasks finished, partners contacted, courses finished) for the person and the reviewer. When people have nobody under Reports to, Evaluations asks an admin to fill it in. See [Learn](/help/learn/), [Onboarding](/help/onboarding/) and [Evaluations](/help/evaluations/).
- **Plan a project from a sentence.** On Projects, **Plan a project** turns a sentence into a draft with sections, tasks, owners from the staff directory and dates, built from the way Favor runs the weekly newsletter, appeal letters, banquets, grant applications, print orders and church newsletters. You edit it before it is created. **Team workload** shows every person by week and marks weeks well above their usual one. A project lead can turn on a **Friday update** that posts what moved to the project channel.
- **Chat reads in your language.** The globe in a channel's header sets the language you read Chat in, including French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, Swahili and Luganda. A message written in another language shows in yours, with Show original under it. Your own messages are never changed, and each translation is kept so a channel is translated once. **Catch me up** (the spark in the header, offered on its own after five unread messages) summarizes what you missed and lists what is yours to do. **Turn into** on a message's menu makes a task, a request, a reminder in the bell, or a Blackbaud action that saves only after you review it. Type @ and a name to mention a partner or a fund, with a card on hover. The microphone records a voice note and the transcript appears under it. **Ask Favor Brain about this** reads the thread. The Chat button on every page translates and shows transcripts and partner cards too. See [Chat](/help/chat/).
- **Chat and Favor Brain on every page.** A round button at the bottom right of every page opens a compact panel over the page you are on. Chat lists your direct messages and channels, opens a conversation or a thread and lets you reply. Favor Brain asks a question and shows the answer, with the same chats as the Brain page. A direct message or a mention puts a number on the button and shows a small notice with a Reply box. Ctrl and a period opens and closes the panel. It stays open as you move between pages, and it does not show on Chat, on Favor Brain or on a phone. See [Chat](/help/chat/).
- **Change the screen while you record.** The floating recording window, the recorder window and the pill have a new Change what I'm sharing button. It swaps to another screen, window or tab and keeps recording into the same clip. With the camera on, a new button hides or shows the round camera. Alt+Shift+P pauses and resumes, and Alt+Shift+S stops and saves. See [Clips](/help/clips/).
- **Every number beside a menu entry matches its page.** Evaluations showed the Projects number and Projects showed the Learn number; each entry now shows its own. Requests counts requests with something new, not notices. Meetings counts live rooms you can open. Chat counts mentions you can still open. Learn counts what My learning lists. Projects adds up the Projects cards on Today. A section such as Work shows the sum of its pages. The Work Overview reads the same live numbers as the menu, and the Notes page lists the same action items as Today.
- **Mail knows Favor.** A morning briefing names where to start, and each email shows who the sender is to Favor, with giving, who holds the partner, a timeline and a one-press Log as an action. Appeal codes, Lookup IDs and fund names in a message open cards. Gift mentions offer an expected-gift note and a Partner Care notice, each confirmed, and promises in your sent mail become tasks. You can describe a category in words, ask a question about an email, find a free time and book it, and start a search with ? to ask in plain words. Before a message goes, Mail checks for the word donor, a wrong name or amount, a missing file and no subject. Send later, templates with the partner's details, snooze until a reply or the next gift, Z to undo, and Unsubscribe are in.
- **More than one mailbox in Mail.** Add a mailbox under Mailboxes in Mail to bring in another Favor Google account, such as an accounting or admin inbox. All mailboxes shows them in one list with a colored dot and a count for each, replies go out from the mailbox the conversation came to, and a From line in each message picks the mailbox or one of its addresses. Only you can see a mailbox you add, and Manage mailboxes removes it.
- **Mail sorts itself.** Tabs above the inbox sort it into Needs you, Partners, Staff, People, Receipts and gifts, Newsletters, Notifications and Calendar, with a count on each, and Waiting on reply lists sent mail nobody has answered in three days. Unread, Starred, Has attachments and From a partner narrow any list, and Newest first switches to Oldest first. Press the circle beside a sender to select a conversation, Shift click for a range, then archive, delete, mark read, label, snooze or move a whole group to a category. A conversation from a partner shows their lifetime giving, last gift and who holds them with a button to log the email in Blackbaud, a one line summary when the thread is long, and three short replies to edit before you send. Mail offers to archive newsletters and notices you never open. The number beside Inbox now matches Gmail's own Inbox number and follows reading done anywhere.

## October 11, 2026

- **A shorter tour, and New in FavorOS.** The tour of FavorOS is now 8 to 10 stops for your role and opens on a new person's first page. New features come with a walkthrough of one to four steps on the real screen, grouped by the day they shipped, with the help article and the training video beside each. Closing asks once, and Skip for now brings it back the next working day. Admins see who has finished under Admin, then Tours. See [The tour and New in FavorOS](/help/guided-tour/).
- **Mail, for admins.** Mail in the menu opens your own Gmail inside FavorOS: inbox, labels, conversations, files in the viewer, search with Gmail words, drafts that save and Gmail's keyboard keys. Reply, reply all, forward, archive, delete, star, label and snooze. **More** on a conversation makes a task, sends it to Chat, opens the partner and logs an action in Blackbaud. Google asks once before FavorOS reads your mail and once more before it changes or sends anything. An email on Today's Unread mail card now opens here.
- **Learn, for admins and HR.** Learn in the menu holds courses and learning paths built from the help videos, the help articles, Google Docs, clips and written pages, with quizzes that have a pass mark and retakes. My learning shows what is assigned to you with due dates, where you left off and a certificate for each course you finish. Assigned learning shows on Today, on the bell, in Projects as a task and by email. Starter courses cover FavorOS basics, Favor Brain, Blackbaud by role and Favor: who we are. Manage builds courses and paths, assigns them to people, a team or everyone, and reports who is done, late and how they scored.
- **Today opens FavorOS, not outside sites.** A Blackbaud action opens in the Work Center, with Open in Blackbaud inside the panel. Every event on your Google Calendar shows in Today's meetings, with its source, Join, and Move to FavorOS for meetings held in Zoom, Meet, Teams, by phone or with no link. Shared with you files open in the FavorOS file viewer. A feedback card opens the note, and a request opens its panel.
- **Onboarding and offboarding.** FavorOS admins and HR start a new hire or an exit from Onboarding in the Admin menu. FavorOS builds the checklist from the HR list, gives every step an owner and a due date, and makes each step a task in Projects. One button adds the new hire to FavorOS, assigns the New hire paths in Learn and emails a link to a first-day page with their steps, their first week and the people to meet. An exit hands over open work, closes FavorOS access and keeps a record of each step.
- **Evaluations.** Evaluations in the Work menu runs the semiannual cycle from Rachel Cox's questionnaire. In week 1 you complete the self-assessment (Form A) and name 3 to 5 people for feedback. In week 2 each of them writes Form B and your supervisor sees both sides, with self against others for each competency and no overall score. In week 3 you meet, read the summary and your six-month plan, and acknowledge, and HR signs. The signed evaluation is filed as a Google Doc in your folder and downloads as a PDF. Admin, People and roles now has a Reports to drop-down, a start date and an HR switch for each person.
- **The new name is FavorOS.** The address, the sign-in and the tools stay the same. The name changes in the header, the tab title, the home screen icon and the emails.
- **Chat works more like Slack.** Favor Brain answers in Chat sort, filter, open full screen with **Show all**, copy, download and open in Google Sheets, and their option buttons and cards work as on the Brain page. A thread opens beside the channel or full width, the panel between them drags wider, **Also send to the channel** posts a reply in both places, and **Threads** lists every thread you are in with what is unread. Each message has a menu with Copy link, Pin, Mark unread and Create a request. Channels get a pinned and bookmarks row, drafts stay with their conversation, Ctrl K jumps to a channel or a person, and the message box has formatting, slash commands (/brain, /request, /huddle, /clip) and an emoji picker with search and skin tones. A request card in a message opens in the request panel without leaving Chat.
- **Work Center tabs in groups.** The 16 tabs now sit in five groups, My work, Gifts, Grants, Reports and Review, with the tabs of the group you are in on a second row and their counts beside them. Work Center remembers the last tab you used. The page draws once and then fills counts in place, and the toolbar under Find a partner (New action, Views, Columns, Export) sits in one row. A link like /work/?tab=open&action=123 opens Open actions with that action selected.
- **Projects in FavorOS.** Projects hold task lists, a board, a calendar and a timeline. Each project can have a Chat channel and a folder in US Team Files. Type `/task` in Chat, or press Make a task from this message, and the task opens in place from its card. Meeting action items with a named owner become tasks, with the moment in the transcript. Clips attach as the brief or as proof, due dates can go on your Google Calendar, and a task can carry its expense request and show the approval. Project notices in the bell sets how email comes.
- **Requests: mentions that hold, email, and a panel that opens anywhere.** A picked @mention is now a solid tag in the comment box, in the comment, in Chat and in email. Request notices also go by email, with a choice of Immediately, Daily summary or None under **Request email**. A request opens in a side panel from Chat, Today, the bell and search, and the panel says when a person has not been told because notices are off.
- **Clip controls that follow you.** While a clip records, a small window with Stop, the time, Pause, Mute, Start over and Discard floats above every app in Chrome and Edge, and you can drag it anywhere. In Safari and Firefox the same controls show as a pill on every FavorOS page. The Record a clip card shows a preview of what will be recorded, with your camera and a microphone level. On the watch page the big play button leaves as soon as the video plays.
- **Appeal mailing list and Annual tax receipt lists are live in Reports.** Each compares its partners and dollars with the saved Blackbaud query it replaces (795 and 1066), and each has a Mail file button that writes the same columns the Blackbaud export had, as a CSV or a Google Sheet in US Team Files. A tax receipt still goes to a partner whose address is marked do not mail; only Blackbaud's no valid address mark holds a partner back.
- **Giving statements.** Giving statements in the Work menu makes one partner's statement on request and the January year-end statements without opening Blackbaud. Find a partner, pick a year or two dates, read every gift that belongs on it and what was left off, then make the PDF on Favor's letterhead layout. The Year-end run tab takes the mail, email and other tax receipt lists and makes print files of 100 statements, one at a time, with the totals checked against the Blackbaud copy and the KPI dashboard. No partner is ever put in two files. Emailing a statement is built and switched off in Admin, Giving statements, where the wording lives too.
- **Declines, Grants and Gift images in the Work Center.** Declines lists declined monthly payments and zeroed gifts with the card processor's reason, and Log saves the follow-up on the partner in Blackbaud. Grants logs a request, LOI, report or task on a foundation and attaches the PDF in the same pass. Gift images lists the gifts of $5,000 or more and the giving-fund gifts that still need a check or letter image, and attaches a photo or PDF to the gift. Admin has a new Appeal codes page that builds a code from the scheme and creates the appeal in Blackbaud.
- **Four more Blackbaud lists in Reports.** Yesterday's gifts (with who holds each partner and their last action), Top partners by total giving, RDD and church engagement actions, and Gifts with no fundraiser credit. The Blackbaud query map now shows the query number and what replaced every query run in the last 120 days.
- **Send a request to someone, and talk about it.** Open a card on the request board and press **Send to...**: pick anyone on staff, add context, and they get it on Today, the bell and as a card in a Chat direct message. Every request has comments with @mentions, a due date, Waiting on and Follow. A reply under the card in Chat shows up on the request, and the other way round. The board filters to Mine, Sent by me, Waiting on others and By person.
- **Huddles in Chat.** **Huddle** at the top of a channel opens a sheet with who is in it, your camera preview, a microphone level and device choices before anything starts. A live huddle shows as a green bar under the channel name with faces, the time and **Join**, and **Leave** brings you back to the channel. **Join** on the Meetings page opens the same sheet. Live and in the room now count only people who are there: a closed tab or a locked phone drops off within about 20 seconds.
- **Files open inside Chat.** Clips and videos play in the message. Pictures, PDFs, Word and Excel files and Drive files show a first page on the card and open in a viewer without leaving the conversation. On a phone, tap a message for its actions.
- **Work Center fits a phone better.** My partners has a sort menu, Asks keeps its filters behind one **Filters** button, every stat tile scrolls into view, and late actions older than 60 days read in weeks or months. The Work Overview team table sorts by any column.
- **Favor Brain answers in Chat are private.** An answer in a channel or group is visible only to the person who asked, with **Share to channel** and **Dismiss** under it.
- **Chat, for admins.** Chat in the menu opens channels, direct messages, groups and threads. Messages arrive live, with reactions, @mentions, typing and who is online. Share a Drive file, a clip or a meeting from the plus in the message box, and type @Favor Brain in any thread to get an answer back as cards and tables. Mentions show on the bell and the Chat badge. Ctrl K finds messages. Every admin joins #general and #hub-feedback.
- **Mine shows one number.** The Mine chip on Open actions and Mine on the Overview count the same open actions, the ones that list you as a fundraiser. "What each number counts" in the Work Center article has the definition.
- **Add a partner, in full.** Add partner in the Work Center's tool row and on Find a partner opens one form for an individual, a household or an organization. A household joins two people as spouses, each with their own phone and email. An organization takes a type and a main contact who gets their own record. Add a relationship links the new partner to someone already in Blackbaud. Matches show beside the form with the reason, and Add stays locked until you tick that none is the same person. The holder follows the state on the address. Admins and the Support Team only.
- **Blackbaud users in Admin.** Admin has a new page, Blackbaud users, that lists every Blackbaud login with its status and access. The desktop reads it each weekday morning, and the page shows when. Add user, Change access and Deactivate save a request that the desktop runs in Blackbaud, and the Requests list shows Waiting, Running, Checked, Done or Failed.
- **A deceased mark reaches GoHighLevel and the giving portal.** Marking a partner deceased puts Do Not Disturb on every channel of the GoHighLevel contact, adds the tag deceased and removes the contact from published workflows. The giving portal stops emailing the record and refuses its sign-in. Open reminders close and the partner's asks leave Asks. Mark active reverses the Do Not Disturb, the tag and the portal block. Every record change now refreshes the FavorOS copy of that partner the same day.

- **Contact, Codes and Record tabs on every partner.** Open a partner and four tabs sit under the name. Contact lists every address with its dates and seasonal window, every phone and every email, and Edit, Add an address, Add a phone and Add an email change them in Blackbaud with the preferred mark moving cleanly from the old row to the new one. Codes lists constituent codes and solicit codes with Add and End. Record marks a partner deceased or inactive with the effects listed first, and marks active again. Each save reads Blackbaud back and shows what it kept, and Undo stays for 24 hours.

## October 10, 2026

- **Work counts agree.** The Work Center badge in the menu, the Work Center tile on the Overview, the Open actions tab and its Open tile all show the same number of open actions, and each Overview link opens exactly the actions its number counts. "What each number counts" is in the Work Center article.
- **Admin settings.** Admin has a tab for People and roles, Work Center, Meetings, Favor Brain, Clips, Reports, Expenses and Receipts. Each setting saves on its own and writes a row to the Audit log. People and roles lists everyone who signs in and lets an admin change a role or block and unblock a person. Expenses holds the approvers, substitutes and mileage rate. FavorOS admins only.
- **Meetings round two.** Hosts invite a Favor name, an email or a phone number from People inside a call, and each invitation shows Sent or Failed. Recordings are named by the Eastern date, a Google Doc of the notes is saved beside them, and the notes page plays sound or video in FavorOS. The notes start when the last person leaves. Guest lines show the guest's name and a repeated line shows once.
- **Admin overview.** Admin opens on a health strip with the last run of the Blackbaud copy, the morning jobs, the overnight sender, the Drive library job, the SKY call meter, iWave credits, Workers AI and the unsubscribe write-back, each marked OK, Late or Failed. The Audit log lists every settings change with who made it and the before and after values. FavorOS admins only.
- **Drive files in Ctrl K.** Type a word and the palette adds a Drive group after the rest: up to six files you can open, with type, owner, date and folder. Each row opens the file in Drive in a new tab. A slow Drive search never holds back the other rows.
- **Feedback on a phone.** Feedback is in **More** at the bottom of the screen and in your account menu. It opens the same form, with the picture of the page and Blur an area.
- **Ctrl K searches all of FavorOS.** Type two letters and it finds partners, reports, your Favor Brain chats, meetings and their notes, and clips, grouped by kind. Each row is one you can already open. Local pages and help articles still show as you type, and the server rows follow a moment later. Drive files are not in it yet.
- **Work opens as your day.** The Work Overview page lists what is due today and overdue from your Work Center, with a circle to complete each action, Snooze to move it and a link to the partner. Gifts to thank shows the oldest five for your role with a Thank button. Your week shows a director's goals, the Support Team's entry backlog or Partner Care's cadence counts. Requests lists what waits on you and your own open ones, Recent activity covers the last seven days, and admins get a team table with open and overdue counts per person. Every number opens the page it comes from.
- **A ? button on every page.** The top bar has a ? button. It opens the help article for the page you are on, with its videos, in a panel on the right, so you can learn a tool without leaving it.
