## My partners

My partners is a tab on the Work Center for regional directors and church engagement. It lists every partner you hold as Regional Development Director, Prospect Steward or Church Engagement Director, with the biggest givers of the last 12 months first.

Each row shows the days since your last contact and what it was, the last gift, the last 12 months against the 12 before, lifetime giving and the next step. The next step is an open task, or a + Task button that adds a call for the next weekday. The phone button dials, and it is crossed out when no number is on file or every number is marked do not call. Prep opens the partner, and Log adds a contact.

A contact is a completed call, visit or email. A printed receipt is not one, so a partner who only gets mail still shows as quiet.

The five numbers at the top are filters. Press Quiet 90 days or more to see who needs a call. Gave this year, no contact in 30 days shows partners who gave recently and heard nothing from you. Gave last year, not this year shows partners who may have lapsed. The buttons above the list do the same, and the search box finds a name, a city or a lookup id.

Support and admins pick any director from the list at the top. Open in Google Sheets sends the rows on the screen to a sheet only you can open.

## Portfolio health and the next best action

The Portfolio health bar above the list sorts every partner into Growing, Steady, Slipping, Lapsing, At risk or New. Press a group to filter the list. Under a partner's name, a line gives the next best action: thank a gift, welcome a new partner, reconnect when a regular gift is late, or check in when a partner has gone quiet for longer than their giving level calls for. Pick Slipping, Lapsing or At risk and press Plan calls for these to plan a call for every partner in the group.

The phone button on a row opens Call mode, which has the script, the giving, the last notes and a Log it button.

## Trip planner

Press Trip planner and type where you will be, for example "I'm in Dallas Thursday". Choose how far you will drive. FavorOS lists the partners you hold within that distance, picks the ones most worth a visit, builds a route and suggests a meeting slot for each with the drive between them. Open the route in Google Maps, untick anyone you will skip and press Add visit tasks to put each visit on the day as a task. Partners are placed by the ZIP code on their address, and a ZIP that is not placed yet is placed the next time you ask.

## Plan calls

Tick the partners you want to call and press Plan calls. Choose This week, Two weeks or A month. The window shows the day each call falls on, weekdays only, with the highest giving first. Tick Remind me at 9 AM each day to be reminded each morning. Press Add tasks. Each partner gets one phone call task in Blackbaud, and Undo sits on the message that appears for 24 hours.

## Reminders

The bell at the top of FavorOS groups what waits on you by type. Reminders due today sit in their own group. Press it to see them. Prep opens the partner. Done clears a reminder. Later moves it to in an hour, this afternoon, tomorrow morning or next week.

Open a partner and press the small bell on any open task to set a reminder for it. Pick a quick choice or a day and time. Press the bell again to change or remove it. Reminders are yours alone and stay in FavorOS, so nothing about them goes to Blackbaud.

## The morning email

Press Morning email on the Work Center. The window shows the email as you would get it today. Switch off any section you do not want: Gifts to thank, Due today and late, Quiet partners, or Reminders. Pick 7:30 AM, 8:00 AM or Off, and choose whether a day with nothing due is skipped. Send me a test mails it to you now.

The email comes on weekdays and each line opens that item in the Work Center. It starts with the regional directors. Support and Partner Care follow a week later. An admin sets the day for each group in Work Center settings, and you can still turn your own off.
