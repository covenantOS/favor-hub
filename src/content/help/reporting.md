## What is in a number, and why it changed

Press the year total or a team total on Today, or a card on Highlights. A panel opens on the right, or from the bottom on a phone. It says how much the number moved since the end of last month and which gifts made up this month, sorted by where they came from, with the largest gifts. A line under it compares this month's gifts with the KPI dashboard's own figure for the month. When the two match, it says so. When they differ, it gives the gap.

The gifts behind a total are listed under it, with Open in Google Sheets and Download CSV.

## Pace to year end

Each card on **Highlights** projects where the year ends. Favor's giving is not even across the months, so the projection uses last year. The low end adds what came in after this date last year to what is in so far. The high end takes what is in so far and divides it by the share of last year's giving that had arrived by this date. A team with no usable last year, and Grants, which counts awards, get a straight line from the amount so far.

The card also shows where the line sits today, the goal, what is left and the amount needed each week. **How this was worked out** in the panel shows the figures behind each projection. A projection is an estimate. It does not change any number the KPI dashboard shows.

## The week in plain words

Highlights has a short summary for all of Favor and one for each team whose tab you can open: gifts in the last 7 days against the 7 days before, the largest gift, the month so far against last month, and what is left to the goal. Grants adds awards. **Copy as text** puts the whole week on the clipboard for an email or a Chat message.

## My dashboard

Under any Favor Brain answer, the pin button adds the answer to **My dashboard**. Only you see your pins. Each pin shows the question and when it was asked. **Refresh** asks the question again with today's numbers and replaces the answer. Rename, move up, move down and unpin sit beside it. A dashboard holds 24 pins.

## Where the numbers come from

The KPI dashboard and Raiser's Edge, rebuilt at 5 a.m. and 5 p.m. Eastern. The time of the last rebuild shows at the top right of Highlights.
