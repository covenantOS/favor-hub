## The columns

- **Inbox**: new and waiting for review.
- **Approved**: accepted and waiting its turn.
- **In motion**: being worked on.
- **Done**: finished. The person who asked gets an email when a card lands here.

Press any card to read the whole request, its files and its history. Your own requests and their status also show on Today.

## For the hub admin

Drag a card from one column to the next as the work moves. Moving a card to Done emails the person who asked, so move it only when the work is live. The number beside **Request board** in the menu, and the Requests card on Today, count what is still in the Inbox.
