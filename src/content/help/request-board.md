## The columns

- **Inbox**: new and waiting for review.
- **Approved**: next in line.
- **In motion**: being worked on.
- **Done**: finished. The person who asked gets an email when a card lands here.

Press any card to read the whole request, its files, its history and the conversation under it. Your own requests and their status also show on Today.

## Send a request to someone

Open the card and press **Send to...** beside Owner. Search the staff list by name or team, pick the person, and write what they need to know in **Context**. Press **Send**.

- The person becomes the owner. The card shows their face, and the history reads who sent it to whom.
- They see it on Today under **Sent to you**, on the bell, and in Chat as a direct message from you with the title, your note and **Open**.
- The owner can send it on to someone else the same way.

## Comments and @mentions

Every request has a conversation under it. Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter (on a phone, press Send). Type **@** and a name to bring someone in. The people you name follow the request and hear about it on Today, the bell and in Chat.

A reply typed under the request's card in Chat lands in the request's conversation, and a comment typed on the board lands under that card in Chat. It is one conversation in two places.

## Owner, due date, Waiting on and Follow

- **Due** sets the day it should be done. A card past its day reads Late.
- **Waiting on** names the person the work is stuck on. They hear about it.
- **Follow** puts you on the list of people told about new comments, without sending it to you. **Following** turns that off.

## Who can do what

- The hub admin approves, moves and declines cards, and can do everything below.
- The owner can send the request on, set the due date and Waiting on, comment and mark it done.
- Anyone sent it, named in it or waited on can comment and mark it done.
- Everyone else reads the board as before.

Marking a request done emails the person who asked, the same email the board has always sent.

## Filters

The row above the board shows **All**, **Mine** (you are the owner), **Sent by me**, **Waiting on others** (yours, waiting on someone else) and **New for you** when something new waits on a request you are on. **By person** shows the requests one person owns, sent, follows or is waited on.

## For the hub admin

Drag a card from one column to the next as the work moves. Moving a card to Done emails the person who asked, so move it only when the work is live. The number beside **Request board** in the menu, and the Requests card on Today, count what is still in the Inbox.
