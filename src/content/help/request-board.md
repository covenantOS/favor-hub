Requests live inside Projects. Everyone has a **My requests** tab. The people who triage a team's requests also have an **Intake** tab.

## My requests

My requests lists what you asked for, newest first, with a question waiting on you at the top. Each request shows four stops: **Sent**, **Approved**, **In progress** and **Done**. Under them you see who has it, when it is due, when you sent it and what moved last.

- **A question for you** shows in a yellow box with an answer line under it. Type your answer and press **Send**. The person who asked is told.
- **Declined** shows the reason the team gave.
- **Open** shows your words, your files, how it was sorted, the conversation and the history. Add a note there with an @mention to bring someone in.

You get an email when your request is done. Links to a request from Chat, Today, the bell and email open it here.

## Intake

Tech and Marketing each have a board with five columns: **Intake**, **Triage**, **In progress**, **Review** and **Done**. A new request lands in Intake. The Intake tab shows for the owners of a team's board and for FavorOS admins, and its number counts what waits in Intake. A person who triages both teams switches between them at the top.

Press a card to open it. Three buttons sit under the details:

- **Accept** puts the card in a project and a stage, with an owner and a due date. The person who asked is told once. The team's own Triage column is the default.
- **Ask a question** sends it to the person who asked. The card stays in Intake and reads **Waiting on** their name until they answer.
- **Decline** needs a reason, and the person who asked reads it.

Drag a card from one column to the next as the work moves. Moving it to **Done** ticks the card and emails the person who asked, so move it only when the work is live. A card in Review reads In progress to the person who asked. Owners are added to a team's board under Project > Members.

The owner of an open request can press **Hold focus time** in its panel. Pick how long, then one of three open times, and Google puts a busy block on the owner's own calendar with no guests.

## Done in either place

Ticking the card closes the request and sends the done email. Moving the request to Done ticks the card. A task ticked in Asana closes the request on the next pass.

## Who can see what

You see your own requests. The team's board is open to the owners of that board and to FavorOS admins. A request accepted into another project shows there as a task, and the people on that project see its title and description.
