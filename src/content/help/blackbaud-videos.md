Every video below has captions, and the words spoken in it are searchable from the help docs. The names, gifts and numbers in them are made up.

## For everyone

- **[Your first week in Blackbaud](/help/videos/first-week-in-blackbaud/)**: the menu, searching before you add anyone, and reading a partner's record.
- **[Queries in Raiser's Edge NXT](/help/videos/queries-in-raisers-edge/)**: building a query from one filter up, and when a query should be dynamic or static.

## RDDs

- **[Entering an RDD action](/help/videos/entering-an-rdd-action/)**: every call, text, email and meeting goes in as an RDD Action with its custom fields. Actions are due every Wednesday morning.

## Partner Care and the front desk

- **[Taking a gift by phone](/help/videos/taking-a-gift-by-phone/)**: a card gift by phone goes in with Add gift on the partner's record.

## Grants

- **[Recording a grant award](/help/videos/recording-a-grant-award/)**: an award goes in as an opportunity on the foundation's record so the dashboard counts it.
- **[Logging a grants action](/help/videos/logging-a-grants-action/)**: log your own conversations with a funder so the follow-up is tracked.
