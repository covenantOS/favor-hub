Today gathers what came to you from every part of FavorOS, so you do not have to open Chat, Mail, Projects and the request board one by one.

## Inbox

The Inbox lists, newest first:

- Chat messages that mention you, direct messages and thread replies
- Tasks given to you, and comments or mentions on your tasks
- News on requests you are on
- Mentions in meeting notes
- Hand-offs from a teammate
- Expense requests waiting for your signature, and new requests for admins to review
- Email that needs an answer from you, and email you starred (when you use Mail in FavorOS)

Press a row to open it. The button on the right clears it where it came from: **Mark read** marks the message or notice read, **Done** archives the email, **Unflag** removes the star, **Got it** picks up a hand-off. **Undo** in the message at the bottom puts it back. An expense request or a new request leaves the list once you decide it.

## Waiting on

Waiting on shows what you are waiting for from other people, and the questions other people are waiting on you to answer (those come first):

- Email you sent with no reply after three days
- A question you asked in Chat with no answer after two hours
- Tasks and requests you gave someone that have not moved in three days
- Action items from meetings you ran that are due

**Nudge** sends a reminder the same way the thing started: a reply on the email, a message in the same Chat conversation, or a comment on the task or request that names the person. You can change the words before it goes. You can nudge each line once a day. The **x** takes a line off your list without changing anything else.

## Hand off

**Hand off** gives a task, a request, an email or a partner to a teammate, with a note:

- **A task**: they become the owner and your note goes on the task.
- **A request** you own: they become the owner and your note opens its conversation.
- **An email**: they get your note and the email, and Mail lists it under Assigned for you. Leave **Forward the email** ticked so they can answer from their own inbox.
- **A partner**: they get the partner page, your note and the last gift. Blackbaud assignments stay as they are.

They hear about it in Chat, or by email if they do not use Chat. When they open it, your Today shows that they picked it up.

## New gifts

If you thank partners, **New gifts** under Your work lists gifts that came in during the last three days for your partners. Each one shows the partner's lifetime giving, your last contact and where the thank-you stands. For a gift of $5,000 or more it also shows where the handwritten letter stands. **Thank** opens the thank-you form. Printed receipts are handled by Support and do not show here.

## Ask Favor Brain about anything you select

Select some words anywhere in FavorOS (an email, a Chat message, a task, a request, meeting notes or a partner's page) and press **Ask Favor Brain** beside them. Favor Brain opens on the side with what you selected, so you can ask about it. Every answer has **Drag**: drag it into a Chat message, a task or an email you are writing.

## Mentions on a partner's page

A partner's timeline shows where they were mentioned: Chat, tasks, requests, meeting notes and your own email. You see a mention only if you can open the place it was said. A mention is not a contact. **Add to contact log** opens Log a contact filled in for you, and nothing goes in the contact log until you press **Save**.
