Chat is where the team talks inside the hub. It is open to hub admins for now, and Admin > Chat changes who can use it.

## Channels and messages

The left column lists channels, then direct messages and groups. Bold means unread, and a red number means someone mentioned you or sent you a direct message. Every admin starts in **#general** and **#hub-feedback**. Press **+** beside Channels to make a channel, public or private. A private channel and its messages show only to the people in it. **Browse channels** lists the public ones.

Press the pencil or the **+** beside Direct messages and pick one person for a direct message or several for a group. The same people always open the same conversation.

Type in the box and press Enter. Shift and Enter start a new line. Hold the pointer over your message for the toolbar: react with an emoji, reply in a thread, edit or delete. Press the up arrow in an empty box to edit your last message.

## Threads, mentions and files

**Reply in a thread** opens the thread beside the channel, so side talks stay out of the main line. Type @ and a name to mention someone. They see it on the bell and on the Chat badge in the menu. @channel notifies everyone in the channel.

Paste or drag a file into the box to upload it, up to 25 MB. A pasted web address becomes a card with the page title.

## Clips, Drive and meetings

Press the **plus** in the message box.

- **Share from Drive** searches the Drive library. It lists only files your own Google account can open, and posts the pick as a card with an Open button.
- **Share a clip** lists your clips. A pasted clip link becomes a clip card too.
- **Record a clip** opens the recorder. The clip posts to the channel when you finish it.
- **Start a meeting** makes a Favor Meetings room and posts a card with a Join button. The card shows how many people are in the room. When the meeting ends the card says so, and when its notes are ready they post in the card's thread with the action items.

## Ask Favor Brain

Type **@Favor Brain** and a question in a channel or a thread. Favor Brain answers in the thread as cards, tiles and tables, using the records the person who asked can see. Everyone in the conversation can read the answer, so ask in a direct message when the question is private.

## Search

Press **Ctrl K** in Chat, or the search box at the top of the channel list, to search messages. Filter to this conversation, to messages with files, or to messages that mention you. Ctrl K elsewhere in the hub lists chat messages in its Chat group.

## On a phone

The channel list, a channel and a thread are three screens. The back arrow at the top left goes up one. The message box sits above the tab bar.
