Chat is where the team talks inside FavorOS. Admin > Chat sets who can use it.

## Channels and messages

The left column lists channels, then direct messages and groups. Bold means unread, and a red number means someone mentioned you or sent you a direct message. Everyone starts in **#general** and **#hub-feedback**. Press **+** beside Channels to make a channel, public or private. A private channel and its messages show only to the people in it. **Browse channels** lists the public ones.

Press the pencil or the **+** beside Direct messages and pick one person for a direct message or several for a group. The same people always open the same conversation.

Type in the box and press Enter. Shift and Enter start a new line, and the next item when you are in a list. The row above the box makes text bold, italic or struck through, adds a link, a list, a quote or code; Ctrl B and Ctrl I work too, and typing - or 1. or > at the start of a line does the same. Press the up arrow in an empty box to edit your last message, in the channel or in a thread.

Hold the pointer over a message for its toolbar: react, reply in a thread, and **More** for Edit, Copy text, Copy link, Pin, Mark unread from here, Create a request from this message and Delete. The emoji picker has a search box, groups and a skin tone that it remembers.

What you type and do not send stays with that conversation as a draft, marked with a pencil in the list and gathered under **Drafts**.

## Threads, mentions and files

**Reply in a thread** opens the thread beside the channel, so side talks stay out of the main line. Drag the line between them to make the thread wider; Chat remembers the width. The square at the top of the thread opens it full width, and the channel name at its top left goes back. Tick **Also send to** under the reply box to post the reply in the channel as well. Escape closes the thread.

A thread with replies you have not read shows a red count beside its replies. **Threads**, above the channels, lists every thread you started, replied in or were named in, newest first, with the same counts. **Mentions and reactions** lists where you were named, replies in your threads and the reactions people left on your messages.

A pinned message shows on a yellow band, and the row under the channel name counts the pins and holds bookmarks: press **+** to add a web address or a FavorOS page. **Latest** appears at the bottom when you have scrolled up, with a count of new messages. Type @ and a name to mention someone. They see it on the bell and on the Chat badge in the menu. @channel notifies everyone in the channel.

Paste or drag a file into the box to upload it, up to 25 MB. A pasted web address becomes a card with the page title, and a pasted Google Docs, Sheets, Slides or Drive address becomes a Drive card.

Files open where they were posted:

- A clip or a video plays in the message. Press the play button in the middle; the player has full screen. **Open in Clips** under a clip opens its page.
- A picture opens full screen. Swipe or use the arrow keys for the other pictures in the conversation, pinch or double tap to zoom, and swipe down or press Escape to close.
- A PDF, a Word file, an Excel file and a Drive file show their first page on the card and open in a viewer with every page. **Download** and **Open in Google Drive** sit at the top right. When Drive will not hand over a file, the viewer shows its first page and the Drive button.
- A voice note or other audio file plays in its card.

## Your language, Catch me up and voice notes

The globe in a channel's header sets the language you read Chat in. Pick French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, Swahili or Luganda and every message written in another language shows in yours, with a line under it that names the original language. **Show original** brings back what was typed, and the same row in a message's menu does it too. What you type is never changed: each person reads the conversation in their own language. Choose **Original, no translation** to turn it off. The setting follows you to every page, including the Chat button.

The spark in the header is **Catch me up**. It summarizes the messages you have not read in four to six lines, adds a **For you** list when someone asked you something or gave you work, and each line has a **See it** link to the message. A channel with five or more unread messages offers it at the top when you open it.

A message's menu has **Turn into a task, reminder or action**. A task goes on your Projects list, a request goes on the request board, and a reminder shows in the bell at the time you pick. **A Blackbaud action** asks for the partner (one named in the message is filled in), whose contact it is, how it happened, the date and one line about what happened. The next step shows exactly what will be saved. Nothing goes to Blackbaud until you press **Save to Blackbaud**. It is for people who have the Work Center.

Type @ and at least three letters of a partner's or a fund's name to mention them. The mention shows as a chip, and holding the pointer over it, or tapping it, shows a card with giving to date and the last gift. Partner cards are for people who have the Work Center.

The microphone beside the message box records a voice note of up to five minutes. **Stop and attach** adds it to the message, and Send posts it. A line under the note reads Writing the transcript, then shows the words, and they translate like any message. **Ask Favor Brain about this** in a message's menu opens the thread with Favor Brain addressed. When you ask Favor Brain inside a thread, it reads that thread first, so "summarize this" works.

## Clips, Drive and meetings

Press the **plus** in the message box.

- **Share from Drive** searches the Drive library. It lists only files your own Google account can open, and posts the pick as a card with an Open button.
- **Share a clip** lists your clips. A pasted clip link becomes a clip card too.
- **Record a clip** opens the recorder. The clip posts to the channel when you finish it.
- **Huddle** works like the **Huddle** button at the top.

## Huddles

In a direct message or a group, press **Huddle** at the top to call the others. You go straight into the room with your microphone on and your camera off, and they hear a ring on every FavorOS tab they have open, plus a notice on their phone. They press **Join**, **Decline**, or **Message** to decline with a quick reply such as "I'll call you back in a few minutes", which lands in your conversation. A ring lasts 30 seconds. If nobody answers, or the person is in quiet hours, they get a **Missed huddle** note in your direct message instead, and the room shows who missed it with **Ring again**.

You can also call someone from their card: hover a name or an @mention and press **Huddle**, or press the headset next to a person in a conversation's member list or in the dock. Inside a meeting, type a colleague's name under **Add people** in the People panel to ring them in.

In a channel, **Huddle** starts the channel's huddle, or joins it when one is going, and nobody is rung. Your camera, microphone and speaker settings are inside the room under the arrow next to Mute and Camera.

What a huddle records follows your setting in the Notifications gear under **Meeting recording**: Record video, Transcribe only or Neither. The host can change it during the call from the recording button at the top of the room, and everyone in the room sees what is recorded.

A channel has one huddle at a time. While one is going, a green bar under the channel name shows the faces, how long it has run and a **Join** button, and the Huddle button turns green. A new huddle posts a card in the channel. The bar and the card count only people who are in the room now: a person who closes the tab, locks their phone or loses the connection drops off within about 20 seconds, and a huddle everyone has left reads Ended. **Leave** takes you back to the channel. When the notes are ready they post in the card's thread with the action items.

## Ask Favor Brain

Type **@Favor Brain** and a question in a channel or a thread, or start the message with **/brain**. Favor Brain answers as cards, tiles and tables, using the records the person who asked can see. Everything in an answer works as on the Brain page: sort a table by its headings, filter it, **Copy**, **CSV** and **Open in Google Sheets**, and **Show all** or the arrows icon to open the whole list on top of the chat. Option buttons, the questions under an answer and a name in a list ask the next question for you. In a channel or a conversation with other people in it, the answer is marked **Only visible to you** and no one else sees it. Press **Share to channel** to post it for everyone, labelled with your name, or **Dismiss** to delete it. In a direct message with only you in it, the answer stays as a normal message.

## Search

Press **Ctrl K** in Chat, or **Jump to or search** at the top of the list, to jump to a channel or a person; the last line searches messages for what you typed. The search icon in a channel searches messages, filtered to this conversation, to messages with files, to messages that mention you, or **From** one person.

## Slash commands and requests

Start a message with **/** for FavorOS's own commands: **/brain** and a question, **/request** and a summary for a request board card, **/huddle**, and **/clip** to record a clip. **Make a request** in the plus menu and **Create a request from this message** in a message's menu do the same. The card posts in the conversation, a reply under it is a comment on the request, and pressing the card opens the request on top of the chat with its comments.

## Reminders, scheduled messages, checklists and polls

Type these at the start of a message. Times are Eastern.

- **/remind** me tomorrow 9am to call Grace. The reminder comes to your bell. **/remind #channel friday 3pm send the photos** or **/remind @person** posts it there instead.
- **/schedule** monday 8am and the message. It posts then, as you. Only you see the note that it is waiting.
- **/scheduled** lists what you have waiting, with Cancel on each.
- **/todo** Event prep: book the venue @Grace fri; print programs; call the church. Each line becomes a Projects task, and anyone in the conversation can tick it off.
- **/poll** Which day for the staff lunch? | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday. Put **multi** after /poll to allow more than one pick.
- **/meet** tomorrow 10am Budget review. Everyone in the conversation is invited and the notes come back to it. For a call right now, use **/huddle**.
- **/status** In Uganda until friday sets the line under your name. **/away** until monday marks you out of office, and a direct message to you gets a note saying so. **/back** clears both.

## Quiet hours

From 8 PM to 7 AM in your own time zone, FavorOS holds phone notices and email about chat, requests and project tasks. The bell still lists everything as it comes in. When your quiet hours end you get one email and one phone notice that sum up what came in. To change the hours, add whole weekends, or turn quiet hours off, open the bell and use **Quiet hours** (on the older pages, the bell's **Settings**). An admin can type **@here** for something urgent, and that message reaches everyone in the channel at once, quiet hours or not.

## Notices

The list offers **Notices for messages and mentions** once. **Turn on** asks the browser, and Chat then shows a notice for a direct message or a mention when it is not in front. Ctrl K elsewhere in FavorOS lists chat messages in its Chat group.

## Email about chat

The first time Chat has something for you, FavorOS emails a welcome with the message and an **Open the chat** button. After that, a direct message, group message, @mention or thread reply you have not read 10 minutes later arrives as one email per conversation with the last three messages. FavorOS sends nothing more for that conversation until you read it. A muted conversation, and one you read or wrote in during those 10 minutes, sends nothing.

To stop it, use **Turn off email about chat** at the bottom of any of these emails, or open **Request email** on the request board and switch off **Email me about chat**.

## The Chat button on every page

On a computer, a round button at the bottom right of each FavorOS page opens Chat and Favor Brain in a panel over the page. The Chat tab lists your direct messages and channels with Threads first. Press a conversation to read it and reply, press a reply count to open its thread, and press the arrow at the top left to go back. In the new FavorOS app the panel draws the same messages and message box as the Chat page: reactions, cards, voice notes, Favor Brain answers, formatting, @ mentions, emoji and files, with Catch me up and the globe in the conversation's header. The Favor Brain tab asks a question and shows the answer the way the Brain page does, with the same list of chats under **Chats**.

A number on the button counts direct messages, mentions and threads waiting for you. A direct message or a mention also shows a small notice above the button with **Reply** and **Open**. A muted channel stays quiet. The panel stays open and on the same conversation as you move between pages. Drag its top left corner to resize it, or press the arrows for a larger panel. Ctrl and a period (Command and a period on a Mac) opens and closes it, and Esc closes it. The button is not on the Chat page, the Favor Brain page or a phone, and it moves up when a page has a button in its corner.

## On a phone

The channel list, a channel and a thread are three screens. The back arrow at the top left goes up one. The message box sits above the tab bar. Tap a message for its actions: reactions, reply in thread and the same menu as on a computer. Threads, Mentions and reactions and Drafts sit at the top of the Channels list.
