Data care is a tab under **Partners**. It has two lists that keep the Blackbaud records clean: partners who gave and cannot be mailed, and records that may belong to the same person. The numbers across the top show the duplicates waiting, the partners with no mailing address and how many of those have been fixed since the list started.

## Partners with no mailing address

The list holds every partner who gave in the last 12 months and has no usable mailing address in Blackbaud. A receipt or statement cannot go out by mail to them. Each row shows what the partner gave, the last gift, the email on file and the reason: Do not mail, No street, Address ended, None on file or Name is not a person.

1. Open the **Waiting** tab and press **Add address** on a row. The partner page opens.
2. Add the address on the Contact tab. If the partner asked not to be mailed, leave the record as it is.
3. Come back later. When an address shows up in Blackbaud, the partner moves to **Fixed** on its own, with the date.

Type a name in the search box to find one partner. The **Name check** tab holds records flagged because the name is not a person's name. Each partner on the list also carries a flag on the partner page.

An admin can send the whole review to the person who reviews it. Assign the review sends one email with a link and puts a task and a tag in FavorOS. The page then says who it went to and when.

## Possible duplicates

Every pair on this list shares an email. A score from 0 to 100 sits beside each pair. Matching names and the same ZIP code raise it, and a low score means look closely. The reason is under the names, such as "Same email, names match, same ZIP". The list rebuilds every hour from the Blackbaud copy.

1. Press a pair. Record A and Record B line up side by side with the address, phone, email, codes, who holds the partner, gifts, actions and the date each was added.
2. Under **Keep**, pick the record that stays. The other one merges into it.
3. Pick where the name comes from and where the contact details come from.
4. Press **Merge into record A** (or B). FavorOS repeats what will move and asks you to confirm with **Yes, merge**.

The merge runs in Blackbaud for you. The pair shows Merge waiting to run, and the page updates in a few minutes with the result. Every gift, action, note and code moves to the record that stays. If Blackbaud turns the merge down, the pair says it did not merge and nothing changes in either record.

If the two are different people, press **Not the same person**. The pair leaves the list and sits under the Not the same tab. **Put back in review** brings it back. Press J for the next pair.

## Is there an easier way?

Instead of asking the Support Team to merge two records in Blackbaud, line them up here and merge them yourself.

## Notes

You can add a note to a partner record from the partner page. The Partner article explains Notes. The note saves to Blackbaud.
