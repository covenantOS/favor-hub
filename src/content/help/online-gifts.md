Online gifts is for Partner Care and the admin desk. It lists every gift that came in online: the website's giving form, the Blackbaud donation forms and international giving.

## Each gift

A row shows the partner, the amount, where the gift came from and its date. An international gift also shows what the partner gave in their own currency. Under it is what the partner wrote with the gift.

The designation under the comment is read from the partner's words. A partner who picked Where Needed Most on the form and wrote "for the trauma care ministry" is read as Trauma Counseling, and the row is marked Changes fund. With no comment, the designation is the one the partner picked on the form.

## Approve

Change the designation if it is wrong, then press Approve. Approve all approves every gift in the list with the designation shown. FavorOS keeps who approved each gift, when, and the fund it had before.

When an approved designation differs from the fund on the gift, the change goes to Blackbaud while writes are on. An admin turns writes on and off at the bottom of the page. A gift split across two funds is changed in Blackbaud.

An international gift shows Not in Blackbaud yet until the admin desk enters it. The approved designation stays on the row for whoever enters it.

## Flags

Three checks run each morning against Blackbaud and Fundraise Up. Whatever they find stays on this page, whatever the gift's date, with the count at the top.

- **Changes fund.** A website gift whose note names a fund, place or program other than the gift's fund. The fund the note names is already chosen in the row.
- **Fund differs.** A Fundraise Up gift entered on a different fund than the partner picked in Fundraise Up.
- **Not in Blackbaud.** A Fundraise Up gift with no entry in Blackbaud two business days after it came in. The row shows the days since.
- **Batches.** A Blackbaud batch unapproved for more than two business days appears under Approve in Blackbaud with its age, gift count and a link to the batch.

A flag closes itself once Blackbaud shows the new fund. To leave a gift on the fund it has, press **Keep fund** on its row. FavorOS records who kept it.

The line at the bottom shows when Website notes, Fundraise Up and Batches last ran well. It turns amber when a check last ran well more than two days ago or its latest run failed.

## Fundraise Up gifts

A Fundraise Up gift is matched to the gift the admin desk entered by the gift Blackbaud's sync matched it to, or else by the amount (what the partner paid or what Favor received, to the cent) and a date from three days before to 21 days after, with the partner's last name preferred.

## Batches in Blackbaud

Monthly gifts charged overnight and gifts from the Blackbaud donation forms arrive in Blackbaud as batches. Those batches are approved in Blackbaud, under Fundraising, Gift management. When any wait, the top of the page shows how many, with Open Blackbaud.
