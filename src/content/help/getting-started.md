FavorOS at **dash.favorintl.org** is where Favor staff do their everyday work outside Blackbaud: asking for work, expense requests, receipts, foundation prospects, the KPI dashboard and Favor Brain. You sign in once with your Favor Google account and every tool is one press away.

## The fastest way to learn it

The tour of FavorOS opens on your first page. It has 8 to 10 stops set up for your work, shows each one on the real screen, and takes about two minutes. Nothing gets sent or changed. To see it again, press **Help**, then **Tours and what is new**.

## Help for the page you are on

The ? button in the top bar opens the help article for the page you are on, in a panel on the right. Press Read the full article to open the whole article. A page with no article opens the help home.

## Getting around

1. **Today** is your home page. It shows what is waiting on you, your Blackbaud actions for the week, and once Google is connected, your meetings, new files and who emailed you.
2. **The menu on the left** has an area for each kind of work: Partners, Gifts, Projects, Meetings, Chat, Mail, Favor Brain, Reporting and Staff. The areas for your job come first. **More** holds the others, and every page in them is open to you. A number on an area shows how much is waiting there for you.
3. **The tabs under the page name** are the pages of the area you are in. Your job's pages come first; **More** at the end of the row holds the rest, with whose job each one is.
4. **Search** is the magnifier at the top of every page. Press Ctrl and K together from any page (Command and K on a Mac), type a few letters, and press Enter. Press G and then a letter to jump to an area: G P for Partners, G G for Gifts, G J for Projects, G S for Staff.
5. **Make a request** is at the top right of every page.
6. Your name is at the bottom of the menu. Its menu has Blackbaud and Paycom, Data saver, sounds and Sign out.

## On a phone

The bar at the bottom holds Today and the next three areas for your job. **More** opens every other area. The pages of an area sit in a row of buttons under the page name; swipe it sideways for the rest.

## Where things live

Work was one area until October 2026. Its pages moved to the area for each job, and search shows where a page used to be ("Was Work > Statements") for a month.

| You need to | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Finish your Blackbaud actions, thank gifts, enter the week | Partners > Actions |
| Check a foundation before you call | Partners > Foundations |
| Enter the mail, approve online gifts, print receipts, make a statement | Gifts |
| See your tasks and projects, or follow a request | Projects (Requests is a tab there) |
| Ask someone for work, a list or a design | Make a request |
| Get approval for a purchase or a trip | Staff > Expenses |
| Your evaluation, your courses, a new hire's checklist | Staff |
| See how the year is going | Reporting |
| Ask a question about partners, giving or the manuals | Favor Brain |
| Learn how something works | Help docs |
