## Put FavorOS on a phone

Open **dash.favorintl.org/m/** on the phone and sign in.

- **iPhone.** Press Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, and open FavorOS from the new icon. iPhone shows notifications only for an app opened from the Home Screen.
- **Android.** Press the browser menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The page lists Chat, Meetings and Favor Brain, and holds the settings for that phone.

## Notifications

On the phone page, press **Turn notifications on** and allow them. A direct message or a mention then reaches the phone when FavorOS is closed, and a tap opens that conversation. A muted conversation sends nothing. **Send me a test** sends one notice to that phone. Turn them off from the same button.

## Data saver

Under **Data saver**, pick **Automatic**, **On** or **Off**. Automatic turns it on when the phone's own Data Saver is on or the connection is slow (3G or slower). While it is on:

- Pictures shrink before they upload.
- A meeting opens as an **audio-only call**. No camera is asked for and nobody's video or screen share is downloaded.
- A message written without a connection waits on the phone and sends once, in order, when the connection returns. **Waiting to send** on the phone page lists them.

## Field staff

Field staff are people on an allowed partner domain, such as favourafrica.org, who work with Favor from outside the US team. They sign in with the Google account Favor gave them at **dash.favorintl.org**. The first time, FavorOS tells them an admin must approve them. An admin opens **Admin**, **People and roles**, finds the person under **Waiting for approval** and presses **Approve** or **Decline**.

An approved field person has **Chat**, **Meetings** and **Favor Brain**, and nothing else: no partner records or giving numbers, no dashboards, no Work Center, no mail, no Drive. In Chat they see the conversations they belong to, plus the **field-staff** channel. They see only the meetings they host or are invited to. Favor Brain answers from the manuals and the program descriptions, and it stays closed to field staff until an admin sets **Favor Brain for field staff** to On.

Admins set the allowed domains on the same page, under **Other domains that may ask for access**. Taking a domain off the list signs out everyone on it. @favorintl.org accounts never wait for approval.
