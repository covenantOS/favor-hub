Partners is the area for partner relationship work. Its Actions page (the Work Center) lists every open action in Blackbaud, the week's contacts to enter and the thank-yous still owed. Blackbaud finishes one action at a time. The Work Center finishes as many as you pick in one pass. Foundations sits beside it. Partners is in the menu for regional directors, Partner Care, Church Engagement, Grants, the Support Team and leadership; everyone else finds it under More.

## Your work on Today

The Work Overview moved onto Today in October 2026; /work-home/ opens Today. Under Needs you, **Your work** lists your actions due today and overdue, oldest first. Press the circle to complete one, Snooze to move it to tomorrow, in three days, next week or a date you pick, or the partner name to open the partner. Each change is saved, sent to Blackbaud and undone from the message at the bottom for 24 hours, and Your recent changes lists your own changes with Undo.

Gifts to thank lists five gifts waiting for your role, the soonest due on the 24-hour clock first. Thank opens the same form as the Gifts to thank tab. Your week shows a director's goals, the Support Team's entry backlog, or Partner Care's cadence counts. The Gift day card gives the gift day's six numbers to the people who work it. Log a contact and New task in Start something work on any partner you find. Admins also see a team table with open and overdue actions per person. Every number opens the Work Center page it comes from.

## What each number counts

Open actions lists every open Blackbaud action you may see: all of them for an admin, your own and your partners' for a director. The tile and the Open actions tab count that whole list. The badge on Partners in the left rail and the Work Center tile on the Overview count only your own work (see below).

On Today, Today's work counts your own open actions due today or earlier. Overdue counts those due before today and Due today counts those due today. Open all shows the open actions the link opens, filtered to your name. An admin or a Support Team member with none of their own sees an empty card; the Everyone chip on Open actions lists the whole board.

Mine is the open actions that list you as a fundraiser. The Mine chip on Open actions and Mine on Today show the same number.

Press the number on Today's work counts, the Open actions tiles or the Gifts to thank tiles to open a side panel with that number's Favor definition and the actions or gifts it counts. The panel says whether the rows add up to the number on the page, and exports them to Google Sheets or CSV. On the tiles, the label under the number still filters the list.

The badge on Partners in the left rail counts the open actions that list you as a fundraiser, plus the gifts waiting for your thank-you on your own partners. Partner Care counts the gifts on its team's partners. The Support Team also counts actions that have no owner. Being an admin adds nothing to it, so it can read 0 even though the board holds hundreds of open actions. The other badges follow the same rule: Receipts counts for the person who made the print file, Expenses for the approvers named on the request, and Meetings for the live rooms you host or are invited to. Requests keeps its own badge on its own tab. The Entry tab counts the week's contacts still waiting to go into Blackbaud, a separate list.

## Finish many actions at once

Open the Open actions tab. Pick a fundraiser, then narrow the list by type, category, due window or partner. Tick the boxes you want, press Shift and click to pick a range, or drag down the checkbox column. When every row on the screen is ticked, a line offers Select all with the full count that match.

The bar at the bottom shows how many you picked. Press Mark complete, choose the date, and add one shared line if you want one. Each action keeps its own summary. The window shows how many Blackbaud calls the batch uses before you confirm.

Reassign changes who an action belongs to. The partner and the summary stay as they are. Reschedule sets a new due date, and the action stays open.

## Undo

Every batch appears under Recent with an Undo button. Undo works for 24 hours. An action that did not save stays on its row with the reason, and you can try it again.

Press Undo once. The button turns off while the change goes back, and a second press is refused with a note that the change is already being undone. An undo cannot be undone, so make the change again if you want it back.

Undo is never refused. If you press it while the change is still on its way to Blackbaud, FavorOS saves your press, the row returns to the list at once, and the reversal goes to Blackbaud as soon as the first change lands. The message at the bottom stays for 10 seconds with a large Undo, and **Z** presses it. If the message is gone, **Recent changes** on the Overview page and the Recent tab list every change from the last 24 hours with an Undo button on each.

## Entry

Entry shows the week's contacts for each regional director, how many are already in Blackbaud and how many are still waiting. Rows already in Blackbaud are blocked so nothing enters twice.

Work Center reads each director's tracking sheet tabs for this month and last month by itself. Opening Entry checks the sheet, at most once every ten minutes, and the Read the sheet button checks it right away. Rows it has already seen are skipped, so reading twice never enters a contact twice. Paste rows is still there for rows from any other list. Ticking a row on the sheet after you enter it stays a manual step.

### Add a partner

Some sheet rows match no record. A row like that has an Add partner button, for the Support Team. The form fills in from the row: Individual, Household or Organization, the names, phone, email, address, the code (Prospect, Partner or Church) and who holds the partner.

While you type, the right side lists records already in Blackbaud with the reason: the same phone, the same email, the same web address for an organization, the same name and city, or the same name. Blackbaud's own duplicate search runs a moment after you stop typing, so a record added in the last 12 hours shows too. An organization is never matched on its name alone. Use this record matches the row to a record that is already there and adds nothing.

Add as new partner stays locked until you tick None of these is the same person. Changing a name, phone, email or city clears the tick. After the add, the row is ready to enter like any matched row. A household makes two records joined as spouses. If one step after the record fails, the message says which one to finish in Blackbaud.

## Thank-yous

Thank-yous shows one row per gift. Thanked already, Probably done and Owed sit in three lanes. Pick the gifts you thanked and press Mark thanked.

Marking a gift thanked closes the fundraiser's task and records one contact for that fundraiser, so the thank-you counts once on the regional director KPI page. A task that is already the fundraiser's own contact (an RDD, Partner Care or Church Engagement action) is completed in place with the way it went out. A Follow Up task closes and one completed contact is added in its place. Two open tasks about the same gift produce one thank-you.

## Gifts to thank

Gifts to thank lists every gift on the partners you hold that nobody has thanked yet, soft credits included. Every gift is thanked within 24 hours of the time it was entered in Blackbaud, counting weekdays only. The gifts on that clock come first under **On the 24-hour clock**, the soonest due first and the largest gift first within a day, and each row shows the time left (Due in 6 h, Due today, Due Mon) or Overdue in red. Gifts more than a day past it fold under **Older**; press Older to open them. The Overdue tile counts every gift past its 24 hours. The row shows the amount, the partner with lifetime giving and last contact, the fund, how it was paid, and notes such as First gift, First monthly gift, Largest gift, $1,000 and up, Soft credit and Task open. Directors see their own partners. Partner Care sees the gifts on partners it holds under a Partner Care assignment that are first gifts or under $1,000, and thanks them by call, card, letter or email. A partner a director also holds stays on the director's list. The Support Team picks a director from the list at the top.

Press Call to ring the partner, Prep to open the call brief, or Thank. Thank opens a short form. Pick how the thank-you went out (call, text, email, letter, card or visit). For a call, choose Talked or Left a message. Write what was said and press Save thank-you. Tick Follow up in 2 weeks and a follow-up task goes on the partner.

A saved thank-you is one completed contact in your name with the Thanked tag, so it counts once on the KPI dashboard. If Blackbaud has an open thank-you task for that gift, the task closes with it and nothing is added twice. Left a message writes the attempt without the Thanked tag. The gift stays on the list and the row says when you left the message.

Tick several gifts and press Thank by letter or Thank by email to record them together with one shared line. The message at the bottom has Undo for each thank-you, and Recent lists them for 24 hours.

A gift counts as thanked when the partner has a completed contact after the gift with the Thanked tag or a completed call or mailing, so a thank-you made in Blackbaud itself clears the list after the next refresh at 5 AM and 5 PM. A recurring pledge shows its first payment only.

## Thank-you letters (HQTY)

The handwritten letters moved to **Gifts > Thank-you letters** in October 2026. The Support Team and admins use it. It lists every gift of $5,000 and up, newest first, grouped by month. Each row shows the amount, the partner the letter goes to (the partner a gift is credited to when there is one, otherwise the giver), the fund, the mailing address and where the letter stands: To write, Printed, Signed, Mailed or Cannot send. A gift counts as written when Blackbaud already holds an HQTY Letter on its partner dated on or after the gift. These are separate from the printed receipt every partner who gives gets from Receipts.

Press **Print** and the page opens one PDF with a page per letter and marks the gift Printed. Press **Signed** once the letters are signed. Press **Mark mailed** when they go out and FavorOS records one HQTY Letter action on the partner in Blackbaud, and Undo in Recent takes it back. Tick several rows to print, sign or mail them together. Each row has a **Signed by** picker. It can be Terry Goodman, Carole Ward, Rachel Cox, Michael Hinton, or the partner's Regional Development Director. With no pick, the letter takes the partner's RDD when one is assigned, and otherwise the last signer you chose. The signer prints in the signature block and goes into the summary of the HQTY Letter action in Blackbaud. Select letters and use **Set for all selected** in the bar to set one signer for the batch. A letter that is printed, signed or mailed keeps its signer, so put it back to To write to change it.

The month button, for example **October letter**, edits the letter text once for every gift that month. Use {amount}, {fund} and {date} where the gift's details belong. Preview shows the letter for a partner before anything prints. A gift with no mailing address or no partner credited cannot print. The three-dot menu holds **Cannot send** (pass-through for another ministry, no partner credited, returned mail, sent outside FavorOS), **Print again** and **Put back to To write**. **Earlier gifts, no letter logged** lists gifts older than last month that have no letter in Blackbaud. Google Sheets exports the list.

## Letters from Gifts to thank

The Support Team and admins see **Letter** next to Prep on every row of Gifts to thank. It opens a page with the letter on the right. Pick who the letter is from (the partner's director by default), paste what the director said, and switch the Designation, Director's words and Invitation paragraphs on or off. **Print PDF and log** prints the letter and records a Mailing action with the Thanked tag, which clears the row. **Copy for email** copies the text and records an Email action instead. Tick several gifts and press **Letters, one PDF** to print them together. Directors keep Thank on their own list and do not see Letter.
## My week and the weekly report

Regional directors see a strip above the Work Center tabs. It shows this week's connections, meetings attended or hosted, event scheduled and one-on-ones against the weekly goals of 20, 3, 1 and 3, and the year's giving credited to the director against the annual goal, with a mark for where pace puts it. Press the strip to open My week.

My week lists every action that counted and what counts toward each goal. A connection is a two-way phone call or an in-person visit or meeting with a partner or prospect in the week, counted once per partner; letters, voicemails, emails and texts do not count. A meeting is an action tagged Attended Event or Hosted Event. A one-on-one is a meeting with no event tag. The event goal counts an action tagged Hosted Event, or one whose summary says you planned or scheduled an event. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Last week shows the week before.

The report draft has two kinds. Goals for the week lists the goals, what is on your calendar and the partners you plan to reach. Update for the week lists the goals met, what happened, and the sections you tick: Giving, Asks, Number of Referrals and Next. Edit the text in the box, then press Copy for WhatsApp, Copy for email, or Open in my email, which starts a new message with no recipient. Nothing is sent from FavorOS and nothing is saved to Blackbaud.

The numbers come from the Blackbaud copy that refreshes at 5 AM and 5 PM, so an action you enter shows after the next refresh. The Support Team and admins open the same tab as Weekly reports and pick a director.

## Cadence

Cadence is the Partner Care tab. It applies the contact rules from the Partner Care manual to the Blackbaud copy and lists the partners who are due. First-time partners get a call at once when they gave a number, and a card when they did not. Monthly partners under $1,000 get a text, an email or a call every three months, and a handwritten card twice a year. Any one completed text, email or call meets the three-month contact, so it is one Reach out step shown as Text, Email and Call in that order, and finishing one clears the other two. A call logged as a voicemail plus a text counts as the text. Admins set the cards a year (none, two or four) in Admin settings under Work, Cadence. Quarterly partners are thanked each time they give. Twice-a-year partners get a card and a call twice a year. Annual partners get a call, card, email and text. A partner a director holds stays off this list.

Communication, under each row and on the partner panel, lists every contact in the last 12 months: Blackbaud actions (calls, texts, emails, cards and mailings, meetings, HQ letters), steps saved in FavorOS and thank-you receipts printed on the Receipts page. The count above it is thank-yous in the calendar year against the target of seven, with the kinds that counted: an action tagged Thanked, a New Gift Received follow-up, an HQ letter, an action whose summary says TY or thank, and each printed receipt. A call that left a message shows but does not count. Mark no texts sets a flag in FavorOS only, because FavorOS has no way to write Blackbaud solicit codes; the text step then shows the reason, for example Replied STOP. Clear removes the flag.

A first-time partner's row also says how they found Favor: **Came in through** and the first gift's appeal, for example Digital Newsletter · Email Newsletter, Wells for Chad, or a director's name and event. A gift from the website adds the giving form it came through, and the source code shows where the partner came in. Open in Google Sheets has the same column.

Each row shows the partner, the giving pattern, the rule, the last contact with its date and kind (Last contact Jun 17 (call and email)), who holds the partner and the steps in the manual's order: call, text, email, card. The next step is green. Every step on a repeating row has its own due date (Reach out counts as one), counted from the last time that step was done. The red headline names the step that went due most recently and says how many days over it is, for example Email 39 days over. Any other late step is listed under it (Card 339 days late), and the list is ordered by the oldest late step. A call whose summary or description says text, texted or txt also counts as the text step on that date, the same as the Texted tag. A step with no phone number, no email or mailing address, or a do-not flag is switched off and says why. Press a step, pick Talked or Left a message for a call, write a line and save. The step becomes one completed contact in your name, and a text carries the Texted tag. Left a message logs the call as unsuccessful, keeps the step due and puts a reminder in your bell for the next workday. A row leaves when every step it needs is done. Undo on the message at the bottom puts the row back.

The five numbers across the top filter the list: Due now, First-time partners, Monthly, twice a year and annual, Quarterly gifts to thank, and Done this week. Mine and All of Partner Care show when the two lists differ. **Print the three lists** splits the partners who are due into Friday, Saturday and Sunday, longest wait first, with Print and Open in Google Sheets. The pattern comes from the partner's gifts in the last 24 months, because Blackbaud's copy holds no schedule for a recurring gift.

## Declines

Declines moved to **Gifts > Declines** in October 2026. The admin desk, the Support Team and Partner Care use it. It lists every declined monthly payment and every gift set to zero in the last 90 days, newest first. Blackbaud records a failed charge as a $0 payment with the processor's reason, so a **Hard reject** (the card or account will not work again) sits apart from a **Soft reject** (the bank said try again). **Recorded at full amount** and **Zeroed gift** hold the gifts a person has to look at one by one, such as a returned check.

Each row shows the partner, the card or bank, the amount that failed, the reason, how many times the same schedule has declined, who holds the partner and the last follow-up. Press **Log**, choose Call, Text, Email, Card or Note, say what happened and pick where it stands: Still open, Card or bank fixed, Partner ended the gift, Cannot reach or Nothing to do. FavorOS saves one Information Update action on the partner in Blackbaud. That type counts as no portfolio contact, so a billing call never changes who is credited. Left a message keeps the row open and sets a reminder for the next workday.

A decline clears by itself when a later payment on the same schedule goes through, and moves to **Closed or paid** when the schedule has ended. Nothing on this tab charges a card or changes a schedule. Changing a monthly amount, how often it runs or its status is done on **Gifts > Recurring**. Updating a card stays in Blackbaud's secure form.

## Recurring gifts

**Gifts > Recurring** lists every recurring gift schedule: the partner, the amount, card or bank draft, the last twelve months of payments as dots, the last payment, who holds the partner and where the schedule stands. The five numbers on top are the active schedules, what they schedule each cycle, schedules paid in the last 30 days, declines since July and schedules stopped in the last 90 days. The chips under the numbers narrow the list to Active, Declined, Stopped, Held, Giving 2+ years, Card, Bank, and, when there is something in them, Past due and Past end date. Declines come first, then the largest.

Press a row to open the schedule. You can change the amount, change how often it runs, skip one charge (the next charge moves one cycle later and Undo moves it back), hold it, resume it or stop it. FavorOS sends the change to Blackbaud, reads the schedule back and only then says it saved. If Blackbaud does not take the change, the page says so and nothing changes. Each change files one Information Update on the partner, with how the partner asked, and shows under Changes made in FavorOS. **Undo** puts the old value back for 24 hours, for the person who made the change and for admins. Stopping a schedule is final because Blackbaud does not restart a stopped one, so Stop asks you to confirm and has no Undo. **Open in Google Sheets** sends every row of the chip and search you have chosen to a sheet only you can open. Hover a chip or a number to see what it counts: Giving 2+ years counts from the first paid installment, and Paid in the last 30 days counts paid installments by Eastern dates. The admin desk, the Support Team and Partner Care use this page. The partner page shows the same facts for one partner: how often each recurring gift runs, the next charge, and any declined or missed charges.

## Grants

The grants team, the Support Team and admins see a **Grants** tab. It lists the Grant Request, Grant LOI, Grant Report, General Task and Cultivation actions on foundations from the last 24 months. **Log** records one of them and attaches the PDF in the same pass: pick the foundation, what it is, Sent or To do, the date, how it went (funder portal, email or mail), a short summary, notes such as the amount asked, and the file. The file can be a PDF, Word, Excel or image file up to 10 MB. If the file does not reach Blackbaud, nothing is logged. Undo in Recent takes back the action and its attachment together.

**File** on a row adds a document to an action that is already there, and **Open** opens the action in the usual panel. The Blackbaud copy holds no attachments, so a row shows a file name only when FavorOS attached it. Nothing here contacts a funder.

**Conversation**, beside Log, records a talk with a funder in two fields: who it was with and what was said. Pick the foundation, how it happened (call, email, meeting or mail) and press Log it. It saves a completed Grants Action on the foundation in your name, dated today, so the next person who opens the record sees it. Conversations have their own filter.

A report shows the award it belongs to under its summary: the foundation's awarded opportunity closest before the report's date, with the amount. Press it to open the foundation's page at that opportunity. A report on a foundation with no award on record says so.

## Gift images

The Support Team and admins see a **Gift images** tab. It lists gifts from the last 45 days that need a check or letter image in Blackbaud: gifts of $5,000 or more, gifts from a giving fund, foundation or donor advised fund, and, when you tick **Include designated funds**, gifts to a designated fund. Gifts paid online are left out.

The Blackbaud copy holds no attachments, so a gift reads **Not checked** until you ask. **Check** asks Blackbaud whether that gift has an attachment (one call), and **Check 20 in Blackbaud** does the oldest twenty. A gift that already has one leaves the list. **Attach** puts a photo or PDF on the gift (three calls) and keeps the original in FavorOS's private storage. The footer shows how many calls are left in the day's allowance for gift entry, so checking never uses up the room the mail-day deposit needs.

## Call prep

Prep on a gift row, Call prep on the partner panel, and the same button wherever a partner appears opens the brief. The left side is the call in order: Thank, Pray, Report, Ask, Thank, Pray. Thank names the gift waiting for a thank-you. Pray quotes the last note or contact that mentions prayer. Report names the fund the partner gives to most. Ask gives the last Amount of Ask on record with its date. Each line says so when the records hold nothing for it.

The right side has the do-not-call, do-not-email and do-not-mail flags, lifetime giving, the last 12 months against the year before, the largest gift, monthly giving, the iWave rating, the last three contacts with what was said, and the open tasks. Call and Text appear only when the partner may be called. Log this call opens the contact form on the partner. Print gives a clean page to hold during the call.

## Asks

Asks lists every action tagged Amount of Ask, one card per ask, listed under the partner's current director. It opens on open asks tagged in the last 12 months; Gave, Check amount, From former staff and All time are filters. Asks on partners no current director holds sit under From former staff, and nothing moves in Blackbaud. The four columns are Asked with no close date, Closing, Past the close date and Gave, each with its count and dollar total. The top row shows open asks, asks closing in 90 days, asks past the close date and the gifts that came since an ask. Board and List show the same asks. Find a partner narrows both. Directors see their own asks. The Support Team picks a director or sees everyone.

A card shows the amount, the partner, the first line the director wrote on the action, when it was asked and how many days ago. The chip on the card sets the expected close date. Pick In 30 days, In 60 days, By year end or After Easter, or choose a date, and press Save. The card moves at once and the message at the bottom has Undo. Clear removes the date. The close date lives in FavorOS and does not write to Blackbaud.

A gift moves an ask to Gave when it is credited to the same partner, as the giver or as a soft credit, is dated on or after the day of the ask, and is at least the amount asked. One gift settles one ask, the oldest it covers. The card then reads Gave with the gift amount and date. Prep opens the call brief. Google Sheets exports the board with each ask's status.

## Change one action

Press a partner name in the list (or press e on a row) to open the action. Every field Blackbaud keeps is there: summary, category, type, status, date and start and end time, priority, direction, location, outcome, fundraisers, the linked opportunity and the description. Notes, Tags and Attachments each have their own tab. The partner sits on the right with giving, the last contact and who holds them, so you never leave the page. Press Save, or Ctrl and Enter. If someone changed the same field in Blackbaud after you opened it, the panel shows their value and asks which one to keep.

From the panel you can also press Complete, Complete and next (it closes this one and adds the next with the same partner and fundraiser), Duplicate (to this partner or others), Move (to another partner) and Delete.

## Change it in the list

Press a due date to move it, the small Open label to complete or cancel the action, or the fundraiser name to give it to someone else (Shift and click adds a person alongside). The row changes at once and saves to Blackbaud in the background. Undo sits on the message that appears.

## Tags

The Tags tab on an action shows nine tags one press away: Thanked, Texted, Stewardship, Scheduling, Favor Presentation, Attended Event, Hosted Event, Amount of Ask and Number of Referrals. A tag that is on shows filled. Press it to take it off, press a plain one to put it on. Amount of Ask and Number of Referrals have a box for the number, with Add or Change beside it. Every press saves to Blackbaud at once and shows Undo. The tags on an action also show as small green labels on its row in Open actions. Other tag categories stay in the list under the nine.

## Change many at once

Tick the actions, then press Edit in the bar at the bottom. Tick each field to change and pick its new value; everything else on each action stays as it is. Delete in the same bar removes them after you confirm.

## New action, follow-ups and repeats

Press New action, or n. Pick the partner (or several), what kind it was, and whether it is done already or to do. The date starts at today for a contact made and a few days out for a task. The fundraiser starts as the partner's current holder. Add a follow-up and it is created with it. Tick Repeat this follow-up and a new one is added each time it is completed, here or in Blackbaud.

## Opportunities

The Opportunities tab lists every opportunity with its status, ask, expected and funded amounts and dates. The band at the top counts each status; press one to see only those. Press a row to edit it and see the actions linked to it. New opportunity adds one for a partner. Inside an action, pick the opportunity it belongs to.

## Views, columns, export and keys

Views saves your filters, sort and columns under a name, and one can open first. Columns hides what you do not need. Export downloads the list or opens it in Google Sheets. Press ? for every keyboard shortcut: j and k move, e edits, c completes, n adds, / searches and u undoes your last change.

## When your list updates

The lists read a copy of Blackbaud that refreshes at 5 AM and 5 PM. Between those times FavorOS checks Blackbaud for changed actions about every hour, and again when you open Work Center, and has them read in. A task created at 6 AM shows within about 15 minutes of your opening the page. If you change an action twice in a row, the second change waits until FavorOS has read the first one back, usually a few minutes.

## Overnight sending

A batch too large for today's share of Blackbaud goes out after 8:00 PM Eastern, when the daily count starts again. A small background job sends held batches through the night, so nobody has to keep the page open. Work Center uses up to 2,400 of the 3,000 Blackbaud calls Favor allows itself each day. The giving form shares Blackbaud's account, so the rest stays free for gifts.

## Stale

Stale groups old tasks into lanes. Select all in a lane leaves out tasks tied to a gift of $1,000 or more, so you pick those by hand.

## Who can use it, and what each role sees

Work Center is open to the people whose job touches it. What you see depends on your role, and the page only offers what your role may do.

- **Regional directors, church engagement and grants writers** see the actions they work and the actions on partners they hold. They can change, complete and reschedule those one at a time or many at once, add contacts and tasks, and hand an action to someone on their own team. Entry shows their own contacts.
- **The Support Team** sees the directors they support. They enter contacts for those directors, mark thank-yous, change and delete actions in that portfolio, and move an action to another partner.
- **Partner Care** sees the Partner Care portfolio. They can change and complete those actions and add contacts and tasks.
- **Admins** see everything and can hand work to any team.

Only admins and the Support Team delete an action or move it to another partner. Only an admin hands an action to someone on another team. Recent shows your own changes. Any partner opens for every role from the partner page, since Blackbaud already shows them to everyone. If you need more than your role shows, press Feedback and say why.

## Where the Work Center opens

The Work Center's tabs are the tabs of Partners, across the top of the page: **Actions**, **Gifts to thank**, **My partners**, **Asks**, **Cadence**, **My week**, **Entry**, **Grants** and **Foundations**. The tabs for your job come first and **More** at the end of the row holds the rest. A tab switches in place, and the address keeps it, so a link or a refresh opens the same tab. Actions has one row of views over the table: **Open actions**, **Thank-yous**, **Opportunities**, **Stale**, **Recent changes** and **Gift images**, each with its count. The Work Center opens on the tab you used last, unless you set one with **Opens on**.

The **Opens on** button in the top row sets the tab you land on. Pick a tab, pick what Open actions shows (Mine, On my partners or Everyone) and press Save. **Use the default** puts it back. Until you pick one, directors open on Gifts to thank, the Support Team on Entry and Partner Care on Cadence, and a tab that is not open for you yet falls back to Open actions.

Open actions has the same three chips with a count on each. Mine lists actions you work. On my partners lists other people's open actions on partners you hold. Everyone lists all of them. **Start here** on a chip saves it as the scope that opens first.
