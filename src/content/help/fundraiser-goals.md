Fundraiser goals is in **Admin**. It holds every fundraiser's goal for the year: the annual goal, the four quarters and the goal for last year's fourth quarter. FavorOS reads goals from this page.

## Where staff see a goal

A regional director sees the goal on Today, in the week figure, and on My week. Both read the annual goal saved here for that director.

## Change a goal

1. Pick the year. Only the current year can be edited.
2. Find the fundraiser. Type the new amounts in Annual and in Q1 to Q4, and press **Save** at the end of the row.
3. The Saved column shows who saved the row and when.

Press **Show ended fundraisers** to list people whose Blackbaud fundraiser record has ended. **Copy goals from Blackbaud where none are saved** fills the rows that are empty from the numbers Blackbaud holds and leaves saved rows alone.

## The check against the KPI dashboard

The table at the top lists the team goals (the U.S. year goal, the RDD team, Grants, Partner Care, Church Engagement and Marketing) from the KPI dashboard next to the same goal in FavorOS. Each line says Matches or Different. The page shows the check for the current year.

A switch under the table sets where FavorOS reads goals. **Read goals from FavorOS** is available once every line matches. **Go back to the KPI dashboard goals** reverses it.

## Is there an easier way?

FavorOS used to read goals from the KPI dashboard. Once the switch is on, change a goal on this page and Today and My week follow it.
