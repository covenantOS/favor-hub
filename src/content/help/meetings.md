Meetings runs video calls inside FavorOS. Nobody installs anything. You join from the browser on a computer or a phone, and a call can hold the whole staff.

## Start or join

Press **Start now** on the Meetings page for a room you can use at once, then **Copy meeting link** in the People tab and send it to the people you want. Anyone signed in to FavorOS can open the link. **Join** and **Start now** open a sheet first: who is in the room now, a preview of your camera, a microphone level, buttons for the microphone, the camera and your devices, and the Join button. The room opens with those choices. A link opened on its own shows the same preview on the room page, where you can also change the name people see.

Live and the count of people in the room mean people whose browser checked in during the last 20 seconds. A person who closes the tab, locks an iPhone or loses the connection drops off on their own.

## In the room

The bar at the bottom holds Mute, Camera, Share, Record (hosts), Raise hand, Chat, People and Leave. Press **M** to mute and **V** to turn the camera off. Double-click a person's picture to pin them for yourself. When someone shares a screen it takes the big space and everyone else moves to the side. With more than nine people the pictures page, with arrows under the grid.

If your connection is weak the room shows fewer pictures at lower quality and says Connection weak. If it drops, the top of the room says it is reconnecting, and you rejoin on your own when it is back.

## Host controls

Open **People** and press the dots beside a person: Mute, Spotlight for everyone, Turn camera off, Stop sharing rights, Make a host, Remove from meeting. **Mute everyone**, **Lock room** (nobody new can join) and **Only hosts share** sit at the top of the list. **End for everyone** closes the meeting. If every host leaves, the person who has been there longest becomes a host after 30 seconds.

## Invite someone during the meeting

Open **People** and use the Invite box at the bottom. Type a Favor name, an email address or a phone number and press Send. A Favor person gets an email with a Join now link. An email address outside Favor gets the guest link by email. A phone number gets the guest link by text. Each invitation shows Sent or Failed under the box.

## Recording and notes

A meeting set to record shows a Recording or Notes on badge to everyone. If the host's browser closes, another person in the room takes over the recording within a few seconds, and the meeting keeps one recording. When the room empties, FavorOS saves the video to the Meetings folder in US Team Files, reads the sound, and writes a summary, decisions and action items. The notes start the moment the last person leaves and appear under **Meeting notes** a few minutes later. The recording plays on that page. A Google Doc of the notes, with the summary, action items, chapters and the transcript with speaker names, is saved next to the recording and linked as Notes in Google Docs. The transcript is searchable on the notes page. The notes are open to the people invited, to the leader of each invited person's team, and to all staff when the meeting was open to all staff.

## Prep before a meeting

On the Meetings page, a meeting that has not started has a **Prep** button, and the lobby has a **Prep sheet** link. The sheet lists who is coming, with their team and title. A partner on the invite shows their giving, last gift, last contact and who holds them. Under each person you work with, it lists the open tasks between you in both directions. It also shows what is still open from past meetings with these people, and the last meeting with the same group, with its decisions. Partner giving shows only to people who have the Work Center.

To open the sheet for your next meeting, use **Next meeting** in the briefing on Today, or open Prep from the Meetings menu: with no meeting picked, it opens the next one on your list. A task made from a meeting's action item shows the meeting's name on its row and card in Projects, and the name opens that meeting's notes.

## Live notes

In a room, the **Notes** tab lists action items and decisions as people say them, a few seconds behind the talk. Each shows who owns it when someone was named, and the time in the meeting. Anyone in the room can add an item by hand. The host can dismiss an item or pick its owner from the people in the meeting. When the meeting ends, the final notes keep what stays on the list.

## After the call

Each action item with a named owner becomes a task for that person in Projects. When you book a meeting, **Post the summary to** picks a channel in Chat. When the notes are ready, the summary, decisions and action items post there once, and each owner is mentioned.

If a person on the invite is a partner, the notes page shows a **Log on a partner record** card to the host. Nothing goes to Blackbaud on its own. The host presses **Log in Blackbaud**, confirms, and one completed Meeting action goes on that partner's record with the date, who from Favor was there, the summary, the decisions and the follow-ups. The host can also find a partner by hand or press **Not a partner meeting**. Logging needs the Work Center.

## Ask your meetings

On **Meeting notes**, **Ask your meetings** answers a question from the notes and transcripts of every meeting you can open, and lists the moments it used. Press a time to jump to it in that meeting's notes.

## Two minutes before

Two minutes before a meeting on your calendar starts, a box opens in the corner of any FavorOS page with the meeting's name and a **Join** button. It opens once for each meeting in each browser session. If you allow system notifications, your computer shows the same alert too. On the Meetings page, a small line at the bottom asks for that permission once. Nothing pops up on its own to ask.

## Book the follow-up

Open a meeting's notes and press **Book the follow-up**. The booking opens with the same people already added and the title Follow-up, so you only pick a time.

## Moving a meeting from Zoom, Meet or Teams

On Today, **Move to FavorOS** makes a FavorOS meeting at the same time with the same people. The sheet has a box, checked at the start, that emails each invitee the join link, the time in Eastern and a calendar file, with a line telling them to use it instead of the Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or phone link on the calendar invite. You are not emailed, and no one gets it twice.

## Limits while it is tested

Only FavorOS admins see Meetings. Guests from outside Favor join from an invite link (see Invite above). If a room fails to load, tell the technology team through Feedback.
