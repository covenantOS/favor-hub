## Before you call

1. Open **Foundation prospects**.
2. Type the foundation's name in **Check a foundation before you call**. One search covers this list and every organization in Blackbaud.
3. Open the foundation to see who contacted them, when, and how.

If someone on the team already has the relationship, talk to them first.

## After you call

1. Open the foundation and press **Log a contact**.
2. Choose **Whose contact** it is, the date, and **How** you reached them.
3. Pick the **Outcome**, write **What happened** (who you reached and what they said), and tick anything under **Mark what applies**.
4. Press **Save and post**. The contact posts to Blackbaud as an action on the foundation's record, so the whole team sees it.

Signed in with Google, your name goes on the contact for you. Otherwise put your name in **Entered by** at the top of the page; FavorOS keeps it for next time.

## The list

Foundations is a Partners page, under **More** in the Partners tabs. **Prospects** lists every foundation with its contacts, the last contact, the fundraisers who spoke to it and its status: Working, Contacted once, Two fundraisers or In Blackbaud. The buttons above the list (All, Working, Contacted once, Two fundraisers, In Blackbaud, Dead ends) each show a count and filter the list. Sort by Most contacts first, Latest contact first or By name. **Open in Google Sheets** sends the list to a sheet.

**To clean up** lists contacts that sit on the shared Unsolicited Foundations record although the foundation has its own Blackbaud record, with the reason FavorOS matched them (the same phone number, the same name). Moving them is not switched on, and the tab says so. New contacts for those foundations already post to the right record.

**Sent to Blackbaud** shows whether posting is on, the state of the Blackbaud connection and everything the page has done. Admins can switch posting off and on.

## New foundation

Press **Add a foundation**, enter its name and its city and state, and add it to the list. The page shows whether Blackbaud already has the organization.

## Dead ends

If a foundation only funds elsewhere, the number is disconnected, or they are not interested, open it and press **Mark dead end** with the reason. Nothing goes to Blackbaud. The foundation stays on the list with its history, so nobody calls it cold again.
