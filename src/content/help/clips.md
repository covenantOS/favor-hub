Clips records your screen, a window, a browser tab or your camera and gives you a link. It suits a short walkthrough of something you built or a question you want to show instead of type. Admins can record for now. Any signed-in staff member can watch a clip you send them.

## Record a clip

Press the camera at the top right of any page. A card opens over the page you are on.

Pick Full screen, Window, Tab or Camera only. Turn the camera and the microphone on or off. With the camera on, a round bubble of your face shows up, and S, M and L set its size. Tick Include computer sound to record the sound a tab or the computer plays. Press Start recording.

Your browser asks which screen, window or tab to share. A 3, 2, 1 countdown runs, and then the timer starts. Keep the hub tab open while you record, and switch to what you want to show.

A small bar holds the timer, a microphone meter, Mute, Pause, Stop and save, and Discard. In Chrome and Edge the bar can float above your other windows. With the camera on, the round bubble floats the same way, and you can drag it anywhere on the screen. When you record a window or a tab, the bubble goes into the video at the spot you set. When you record the whole screen, what you see is what the video holds.

A clip stops by itself at 45 minutes. If the tab closes in the middle, the next hub page you open finishes the recording as a shorter clip.

Upload a video at the bottom of the card takes an MP4, MOV or WebM file from your computer and treats it like any other clip.

## What you get when you stop

The clip opens for watching at once. A moment later it has a title, a summary, chapters and a transcript, all written from what you said. Rename the title, edit the summary and chapters, or press Write the title again in the menu if you want a fresh one.

## Watch a clip

The watch page has a player with captions, speeds from 1x to 2x, and the keys Space, K, J, L, the arrow keys, M, F and C. Chapters show as marks on the scrubber and as a list. The transcript sits on the right. Search it, click any word to jump there, copy it, or download it as text or subtitles.

Signed-in staff can react with an emoji and write comments tied to a moment in the video. Each comment shows as a small circle on the scrubber. The Activity tab shows who watched and how far they got.

## Share it

Share turns on the link and copies it. By default only people signed in with a Favor account can open a clip. Anyone with the link adds people outside Favor. Someone outside Favor sees the video, the summary, the chapters and the transcript, and nothing else. Turn the switch off in the menu and the link stops working for them at once.

## Edit a clip

Edit the clip in the menu opens the editor. The original file never changes. Edits are a list, and Download edited version makes a new file from it.

Drag a handle on the timeline to trim, click a part and press Delete selection to cut it, or press S to split at the playhead. Select words in the transcript and press Delete to cut them. Remove filler words takes out the ums and uhs. Remove silences takes out long pauses, at Gentle, Normal or Tight. Undo, Redo and the history list step back, and every change saves by itself. Finish returns to the clip.

## Draft a help article

On the watch page, the Help article tab writes a draft in the shape the hub's help articles use, from what you said. Nothing is published. Copy it or download it as a Markdown file, change it, and send it to the team that keeps the help docs.

## Manage your clips

My clips on the recorder card opens the quiet list of every clip, and so does Clips in the hub search. Search the list by title, summary or what was said. Hover a clip to preview it. You can rename a clip, copy its link, switch sharing, or delete it. Delete removes the video, the transcript and every comment for good, and the link stops working.
