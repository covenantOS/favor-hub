Clips records your screen, a window, a browser tab or your camera and gives you a link. It suits a short walkthrough of something you do every week, or a question you want to show instead of type. Every staff member with a hub sign-in can record. Your clips are your own: other people see a clip only when you send them its link.

## Record a clip

Press the camera at the top right of any page, or choose **Record a clip** from the menu under your name. A small recorder window opens next to the hub.

Pick Full screen, Window, Tab or Camera only. Turn the camera and the microphone on or off. With the camera on, a round bubble of your face shows up, and S, M and L set its size. Tick Include computer sound to record the sound a tab or the computer plays. Press Start recording. Your browser asks which screen, window or tab to share, a 3, 2, 1 countdown runs, and then the timer starts.

The recorder window holds the recording, so you can go to any page in the hub, reload a page, or switch to another app, and the recording keeps going. Leave the recorder window open. Its small bar holds the timer, a microphone meter, Mute, Pause, Stop and save, and Discard. The camera button in the hub shows a red dot while a recording runs, and pressing it brings the recorder window forward.

If your browser blocks pop-up windows, the hub says so and records in the page instead. In that case keep the tab open.

Everything you record is saved as it goes, every few seconds. If the recorder window closes, the computer goes to sleep or the connection drops, the clip is finished from what was saved and lands in your clips with everything up to the last few seconds.

A clip stops by itself at 45 minutes. Upload a video at the bottom of the card takes an MP4, MOV or WebM file from your computer and treats it like any other clip.

## What you get when you stop

The clip opens for watching at once. A moment later it has a title, a summary, chapters and a transcript. The title and summary come from what you said and from the app and page on your screen, for example the Request board or a Blackbaud screen. Rename the title, edit the summary and chapters, or press Write the title again in the menu if you want a fresh one.

Text that was on your screen is searchable in your own clips, so you can find a clip by a word you saw and never said. The screen text stays on your clip. Nobody else sees it, and it is deleted with the clip.

## Watch a clip

The watch page has a player with captions, speeds from 1x to 2x, and the keys Space, K, J, L, the arrow keys, M, F and C. Chapters show as marks on the scrubber and as a list. The transcript sits on the right. Search it, click any word to jump there, copy it, or download it as text or subtitles. Your own clips also have an On screen tab that lists the pages you showed, in order.

Signed-in staff can react with an emoji and write comments tied to a moment in the video. Each comment shows as a small circle on the scrubber. The Activity tab shows who watched and how far they got.

## Share it

Share turns on the link and copies it. By default only people signed in with a Favor account can open a clip. Anyone with the link adds people outside Favor. Someone outside Favor sees the video, the summary, the chapters and the transcript, and nothing else. Turn the switch off in the menu and the link stops working for them at once.

A clip can show partner details on screen. Check what is showing before you turn on Anyone with the link.

## Edit a clip

Edit the clip in the menu opens the editor. The original file never changes. Edits are a list, and Download edited version makes a new file from it.

Drag a handle on the timeline to trim, click a part and press Delete selection to cut it, or press S to split at the playhead. Select words in the transcript and press Delete to cut them. Remove filler words takes out the ums and uhs. Remove silences takes out long pauses, at Gentle, Normal or Tight. Undo, Redo and the history list step back, and every change saves by itself. Finish returns to the clip.

## Draft a help article

On the watch page, the Help article tab writes a draft in the shape the hub's help articles use, from what you said. Nothing is published. Copy it or download it as a Markdown file, change it, and send it to the team that keeps the help docs.

## Your clips and your space

**Clips** in the menu, **My clips** under your name and the small clips button beside the camera all open your list. Search it by title, summary, what was said or what was on screen. Hover a clip to preview it. You can rename a clip, copy its link, switch sharing, or delete it. Delete removes the video, the transcript, the screen text and every comment for good, and the link stops working.

Each person has 10 GB for clips. The top of your list shows how much you have used. Past 80%, it lists your oldest clips that nobody has watched, with a Delete button on each. At 10 GB a new recording waits until you delete something.

Hub admins can open an All clips view that lists every person's clips and how much space each person uses. An admin can open a clip and delete it to free space, and cannot edit it. What was said and what was on screen are not searched there.
