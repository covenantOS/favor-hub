Clips records your screen and your microphone and gives you a link. It suits a short walkthrough of something you built. Your camera stays off. Admins can record for now. Any signed-in staff member can watch a clip you send them.

## Record a clip

Open Clips and press Start recording. Your browser asks for the microphone, then asks which screen, window or tab to share. Pick one. To include sound from a browser tab or the computer, tick the sound box in the browser's picker when it shows one.

A 3, 2, 1 countdown runs, and then the timer starts. Pause holds the recording and Resume continues it. Stop and save ends it and uploads it. Discard throws it away. A clip stops by itself at 15 minutes.

Keep the tab open while the bar fills. A part that fails is tried again on its own, and the page tells you if the clip could not be saved.

## Share it

When the clip is ready you get a link that starts with dash.favorintl.org/c/. The link opens a page with the clip, the title, your name, the date and a speed control at 1x, 1.5x and 2x. Copy link sits under the player.

By default only people signed in with a Favor account can open it. Anyone else lands on the sign-in page. Turn on Anyone with the link on a clip to let people outside Favor watch without signing in. The switch starts off for every clip, and you can turn it off again at any time.

## Manage your clips

My clips lists every clip with its length and how many times others opened it. You can rename a clip, copy its link, switch sharing on or off, or delete it. Delete removes the video for good and the link stops working.
