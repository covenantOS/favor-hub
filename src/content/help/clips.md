Clips records your screen, a window, a browser tab or your camera and gives you a link. It suits a short walkthrough of something you do every week, or a question you want to show instead of type. Every staff member with a FavorOS sign-in can record. Your clips are your own: other people see a clip only when you send them its link.

## Record a clip

Press the camera at the top right of any page, or choose **Record a clip** from the menu under your name. A small recorder window opens next to FavorOS.

The card shows what will be recorded. Pick Full screen, Window, Tab or Camera only. Turn the camera, the microphone and computer sound on or off. With the camera on, your face shows as a round bubble on the preview: S, M and L set its size, and dragging it sets where it sits in the video. The bars next to Microphone move when it hears you. Press Start recording. Your browser asks which screen, window or tab to share, a 3, 2, 1 countdown runs, and then the timer starts.

## The controls follow you

In Chrome and Edge a small window with the controls floats above everything on your computer, other apps included. Drag it anywhere by its title bar. It holds Stop and save, the time, Pause, Mute, Change what I'm sharing, the camera switch, Start over and Discard, and the round camera when the camera is on. Closing that window never ends the recording. The Float button in the recorder window brings it back. A full screen recording shows the floating window in the video. Turn Floating controls off on the card to leave it out. With the camera on, the round camera still floats.

In Safari and Firefox, and in Chrome or Edge with the floating window closed, the same controls show as a pill on every FavorOS page. Drag it by the dots at its left end, or move it with the arrow keys. It stays where you put it as you go from page to page.

While you record, Change what I'm sharing in the floating window, the recorder window or the pill opens the browser's picker again. Pick another screen, window or tab and the recording carries on in the same clip, with one steady picture. In the pill the picker opens in the recorder window, so the pill asks you to press the button there. With the camera on, the camera button hides or shows the round camera in the video. Alt+Shift+P pauses and resumes, and Alt+Shift+S stops and saves. A browser passes these keys to FavorOS only while the recorder window, the floating window or a FavorOS page has the focus.

Start over deletes what you recorded so far and begins again after a new countdown, with the same screen, camera and microphone. Discard deletes the recording. Both ask first.

The recorder window holds the recording, so you can go to any page in FavorOS, reload a page, or switch to another app, and the recording keeps going. Leave the recorder window open. The camera button in FavorOS shows a red dot while a recording runs, and pressing it brings the recorder window forward. When you stop, the controls say Clip saved and link to the clip.

If your browser blocks pop-up windows, FavorOS says so and records in the page instead. In that case keep the tab open.

Everything you record is saved as it goes, every few seconds. If the recorder window closes, the computer goes to sleep or the connection drops, the clip is finished from what was saved and lands in your clips with everything up to the last few seconds.

A clip stops by itself at 45 minutes. Upload a video at the bottom of the card takes an MP4, MOV or WebM file from your computer and treats it like any other clip.

## What you get when you stop

The clip opens for watching at once. A moment later it has a title, a summary, chapters and a transcript. The title and summary come from what you said and from the app and page on your screen, for example the Request board or a Blackbaud screen. Rename the title, edit the summary and chapters, or press Write the title again in the menu if you want a fresh one.

Text that was on your screen is searchable in your own clips, so you can find a clip by a word you saw and never said. The screen text stays on your clip. Nobody else sees it, and it is deleted with the clip.

## Watch a clip

The watch page has a player with captions, speeds from 1x to 2x, and the keys Space, K, J, L, the arrow keys, M, F and C. Chapters show as marks on the scrubber and as a list. The transcript sits on the right. Search it, click any word to jump there, copy it, or download it as text or subtitles. Your own clips also have an On screen tab that lists the pages you showed, in order.

Signed-in staff can react with an emoji and write comments tied to a moment in the video. Each comment shows as a small circle on the scrubber. The Activity tab shows who watched and how far they got.

## Share it

Share opens a window where you choose who can watch the clip. There are four levels.

- **Private.** Only you and FavorOS admins.
- **Specific people.** You, admins, the people you add by name, and the members of any Chat conversation where you post the clip. This is where a new clip starts.
- **Anyone at Favor.** Any Favor staff member who is signed in and has the link.
- **Everybody.** Anyone with the link, including people outside Favor, with no sign-in. A Public mark shows on the clip for everyone who opens it.

Type a name or email under People to add a Favor staff member. To share with someone outside Favor, choose Everybody. A change takes effect the moment you make it. Copy link in the same window copies the link. A person outside Favor sees the video, the summary, the chapters and the transcript, and nothing else.

A clip can show partner details on screen. Check what is showing before you choose Everybody.

## Edit a clip

Edit the clip in the menu opens the editor. The original file never changes. Edits are a list, and Download edited version makes a new file from it.

Drag a handle on the timeline to trim, click a part and press Delete selection to cut it, or press S to split at the playhead. Select words in the transcript and press Delete to cut them. Remove filler words takes out the ums and uhs. Remove silences takes out long pauses, at Gentle, Normal or Tight. Undo, Redo and the history list step back, and every change saves by itself. Finish returns to the clip.

## Highlights and chapters

A clip with a transcript always gets chapters. If one has none when it is opened, or shows in My clips, FavorOS writes them. **Highlights** under the summary lists up to four moments worth jumping to: lines where the speaker stressed something, chapter starts and spots people reacted to. Each shows why it was picked.

## Make a help article or a Learn lesson

On your own clip, **Make a help article** writes the draft and opens the Article tab. **Make a Learn lesson** makes a draft course in Learn with the clip and a written page of its steps, and opens it in Learn, Manage. The course stays a draft until you publish it. Making a lesson is for FavorOS admins and HR. In My clips, **Help article** and **Learn lesson** do the same from the card.

## Draft a help article

On the watch page, the Help article tab writes a draft in the shape FavorOS's help articles use, from what you said. Nothing is published. Copy it or download it as a Markdown file, change it, and send it to the team that keeps the help docs.

## Your clips and your space

**Clips** in the menu, **My clips** under your name and the small clips button beside the camera all open your list. Search it by title, summary, what was said or what was on screen. **All**, **Shared** and **Private** filter the list. Each card shows who can watch it, and pressing that label opens the share window. **Select** lets you tick several clips and set them to Private, Specific people or Anyone at Favor, or delete them together. On a card, **Send to email** adds the clip to a Mail draft, and **Help article** and **Learn lesson** appear once the clip is ready. Hover a clip to preview it. You can rename a clip, copy its link, switch sharing, or delete it. Delete removes the video, the transcript, the screen text and every comment for good, and the link stops working.

Each person has 10 GB for clips. The top of your list shows how much you have used. Past 80%, it lists your oldest clips that nobody has watched, with a Delete button on each. At 10 GB a new recording waits until you delete something.

FavorOS admins can open an All clips view that lists every person's clips and how much space each person uses. An admin can open a clip and delete it to free space, and cannot edit it. What was said and what was on screen are not searched there.
