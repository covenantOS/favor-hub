FavorOS has two test accounts, **FavorOS Tester** (tester@favorintl.org) and **FavorOS Tester Two** (tester2@favorintl.org). They exist so a change can be checked on the live site as a person other than you, and between two people, without a real staff member getting a message.

## What they are

Both are admins with HR access and no Google account behind them. Nobody signs in to them from the sign-in page. A check script signs in with a key the technology team holds, and every sign-in, allowed or refused, is written to the sign-in log.

## What they can do

- Open every page an admin can open, including the Work Center for every fundraiser.
- Use Mail against a stored sample mailbox. Gmail is never called.
- Chat with each other and post in their own private channel.
- Hold sample data set up for them: a reporting line between the two, an open evaluation, a first week for Tester Two and two upcoming meetings.

## What they cannot do

They cannot write to Blackbaud, send email, notify staff, make a request, sign an expense, enter gifts or print receipts. Those buttons answer "This is the FavorOS test user." Steps that only read or preview still work, so a screen can be checked up to the point of saving.

## Where you will not see them

The testers are left out of Admin People, the Chat and Meetings directories, pickers, tour counts and notices. If one shows up in a list of staff, tell the technology team through Feedback.
