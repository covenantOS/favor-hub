When an answer has more rows than fit on screen, it shows the first few. On the Favor Brain page, press **Show all** (or the expand button beside Copy) and the whole list opens in a side panel where you can sort by any column, filter with the buttons above it, and search. **Copy** puts the rows on your clipboard to paste into any sheet, and **CSV** downloads them. In Claude or ChatGPT, open the download link in the answer. A CSV link works for **24 hours**, and anyone who has it can open it in that time, so keep it to yourself. A list on the Favor Brain page says "Expired. Ask again" after 24 hours.

## Open in Google Sheets

**Open in Google Sheets** sits beside Copy and CSV on every list in the hub: Favor Brain tables, Foundation prospects, Thank-you receipts, the Request board, the Expense log, Feedback and Brain admin. It makes a sheet in a folder called Favor exports in your own Drive, with a header row, a line saying where the rows came from, and an About tab. Only you can open it until you share it.

The first time, Google asks once whether Favor may make sheets in your Drive. Press **Allow** and the sheet is built when you come back, without pressing the button again. A sheet holds what you could already copy from the screen, so the same rule applies: it holds partner information, keep it inside Favor, and delete it when you are done. From Claude or ChatGPT, ask "put that in a Google Sheet".

## Asking for a list

Say what each row is and what columns you want: "List active partners in Texas with their last gift date, largest gift and assigned RDD, as a spreadsheet."

## Contact details

Phone numbers, emails and mailing addresses in lists are a separate access package. Today only the hub admin has it by default. If your work needs contact details for partners beyond your own, ask for it on the Favor Brain page and say why.

## Using lists well

- Keep lists inside Favor. Don't forward them or upload them to other tools without your director's okay.
- A list is a snapshot. Blackbaud is the record; check a partner's record before you call.
- If a count in a list doesn't match the dashboard, send feedback with the question you asked.
