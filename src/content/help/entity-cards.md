A name in FavorOS is more than text. A coworker, a partner, a fund, an appeal, a task, a request, a meeting, a clip and a course each have one card, and it is the same card on every page.

## Opening a card

On a computer, point at the name and the card appears beside it. On a phone, tap the name and the card rises from the bottom. Press the name itself, or **Open** on the card, to go to the full page.

What the card shows depends on the thing:

- **A partner**: the Lookup ID, lifetime giving and where it came from (their own gifts, or through a donor-advised fund or foundation), the household, the last gift, whether they are active, LYBUNT or lapsed, and who holds the partner. Lifetime giving counts the way the KPI dashboard does: a gift sent through a DAF counts for the partner it names, and spouses count once. Point at or tap an underlined label to see what it means.
- **A coworker**: team and email, with Message to start a chat.
- **A fund or an appeal**: its ID, and for an appeal its category, start date and whether it is active.
- **A task or a request**: its status, owner and due date.
- **A meeting**: whether it is scheduled, live or ended, and the host. **A clip**: who made it, its length and the date. **A course**: its lessons and who it is for.

A partner's card shows giving only to people who have the Work Center.

## What you can do from a card

- **Open** goes to the full page.
- **Make a task** starts a task in Projects with the card attached.
- **Ask Brain** asks Favor Brain about that partner, fund or task.
- **Send to** puts the card in a Chat channel or a direct message, adds it as a comment on a request, or copies a link. The person who gets it sees the same card, limited to what their access allows.

## Where names are cards

Today, the Work Center, the partner page, Mail, Chat, Projects, Meetings, Lists, the report tables in Reports and Standard reports, the rows behind a number and Gift entry. A task code typed in a comment or a Chat message, such as MKT-12, becomes a card too. If you find a partner name that is plain text, tell the technology team through Feedback.
