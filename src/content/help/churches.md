## What is on the page

The numbers across the top are church giving this year against Church Engagement's goal for 2026, the churches that gave, the churches due for a thank-you or a call in the next three weeks, the churches nobody has spoken to in 90 days, and the churches with an email for the church newsletter.

The list shows every church that has given since January of last year, largest this year first. Filter by region, press **Due soon** for the churches whose next call falls within three weeks or is already late, or type a church or a pastor in the search box.

## The next call

Church Engagement thanks a church at once and calls every three months. The **Next** column adds 90 days to the last email, call or meeting logged on the church. A church with no contact logged is timed from its last gift.

## One church

Press a church to see how many gifts are waiting for a thank-you, its city, who holds it, its giving by year, the pastors and staff linked to it, and its latest actions. **Open the record** takes you to the partner page, where you log a visit or thank a gift.

This page is in beta and open to admins and testers for now.
