Lists is under Reporting. Choose **Partners** or **Gifts**, then set criteria from the menus on the left. The count at the top changes as you go.

## Criteria

- **Partner status** uses the KPI dashboard definitions: active is a gift in the last 12 months, LYBUNT a last gift 12 to 24 months back, lapsed older than that. Major is one gift of $1,000 or more.
- **Held by** takes a person, a team or a role. **Place** takes states, cities and ZIP starts.
- **Giving** takes lifetime and largest gift ranges, giving in up to three periods, and first and last gift dates.
- **Gifts** takes date, amount, fund, appeal, appeal category, gift type, payment method and fundraiser credit.
- **Records** takes solicit codes, constituent codes, attributes, relationships, events and last contact.

## Save, share and export

Save list keeps the criteria. Share it with only you, your team or everyone. A shared list always shows each person only the partners their Work Center access allows. **CSV** downloads the rows and **Open in Google Sheets** builds a sheet in your Drive.

A list that is only a status shows a line comparing its count with the KPI dashboard. The data is copied from Blackbaud several times a day, and the page shows when.
