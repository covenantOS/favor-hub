Recurring is in **Gifts**, beside Gift entry, Other gifts and Online gifts. It lists every recurring gift schedule in Blackbaud, the monthly partners and the ones who give every quarter or year. You can change a schedule here and Blackbaud is updated for you.

## The numbers across the top

Active schedules shows how many run now, split into card and bank draft. Scheduled each cycle is what one round of charges adds up to. Paid in the last 30 days counts the schedules that had a payment above $0 in that time. Declined since July counts active schedules with a declined charge since July 1, and Work the declines opens the Declines page. Stopped in 90 days counts schedules that ended recently after paying in the half year before, so Partner Care can call to win the partner back. Point at a number to read exactly how it is counted.

If a schedule is active and its next charge date has passed, or it is still active after its end date, a line above the list says so. Press the line to see those schedules.

## Find a schedule

The buttons above the list filter it: Active, Declined, Stopped, Held, Giving 2+ years, Card, Bank, Past due, Past end date and Stopped in 90 days. Type a partner name in Find a partner. Choose 15, 25 or 50 rows a page. Each row shows the amount, method, a dot for each of the last 12 months (filled when a payment came in), the last payment, how many payments, who holds the partner and the status.

Press a row to open the schedule beside the list. Press a partner name to open the partner.

## Change a schedule

Open a schedule that is Active or Held. Then:

- **Change the amount.** Type the new amount and press Save the new amount.
- **How often.** Pick monthly, quarterly or another cycle and press Save how often.
- **Skip one charge.** Moves the next charge date one cycle later. FavorOS shows both dates and asks you to confirm.
- **Hold the schedule** sets it to Held. **Resume the schedule** sets it back to Active.
- **Stop the schedule** ends it. Blackbaud does not restart a stopped schedule, so FavorOS asks you to confirm. If the partner starts again, set up a new schedule in Blackbaud.

Pick who asked for the change under Partner asked by, and add a note if you want one. FavorOS saves the change in Blackbaud, reads it back and only then says it saved. An action goes on the partner's record. Under Changes made in FavorOS, **Undo** stays open for 24 hours.

## Open in Google Sheets

Open in Google Sheets sends every row of the filter and search you are looking at to a sheet only you can open. Keep it inside Favor.

## Is there an easier way?

Instead of finding the schedule in Blackbaud and editing it there, change it here. The list shows the same schedules with their payments and declines, and the partner's record gets its action without extra steps.

The partner page shows the same schedules under Recurring gifts, with the next charge and a Missed a charge mark when the date has passed.
