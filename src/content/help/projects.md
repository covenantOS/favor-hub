Projects is where FavorOS keeps work that has an owner and a date. Admin > Projects changes who can use it.

## The pages

The Projects page has three tabs. **My tasks** counts what is assigned to you, with tiles for Overdue, Due today and Open, a search box, **Show completed**, and your tasks grouped under Today, Next 7 days, Later and No date. Each group has a line to add a task. **All projects** lists the projects, with Active, All and Archived filters. **Team workload** is described below. **Add task**, **Plan a project** and **New project** sit at the top right. **Requests**, beside Projects in the page tabs, is the [request board](/help/request-board/).

A project opens with its own page: a project list in the left column and, across the top, **List**, **Board**, **Calendar**, **Timeline** and **Workload**, with **Filter**, **Select**, **Add task**, Assignee, Due, Status, Tag and **Show completed** under them.

## Asana

Projects shows **Synced with Asana** beside its title. Tasks in the projects Favor has switched on are read from Asana and changes made here are written back, so the two stay the same. A person asked to connect Asana sees **Connect your Asana account** before Projects opens. Press **Connect Asana** so changes you make go to Asana under your name. On a project, **Connected as** your name shows with **Disconnect**. An admin chooses which projects sync, and who must connect, in Admin > Projects.

## Projects and tasks

A project has a lead, members, sections, and tasks with an owner, dates, a priority and optional Blackbaud codes. Open a task to comment, @mention someone, add subtasks, set what it waits on and attach files. A task with no project is a personal task on My tasks.

## Plan a project from a sentence

Press **Plan a project**, write what you are doing (for example, "Banquet in Houston on December 6"), and press **Draft plan**. FavorOS picks the closest template, counts dates back from the date in your sentence and gives each task to the person whose title fits. The templates are weekly newsletter, monthly appeal letter, banquet or hosted dinner, grant application, print order and church newsletter. They follow how the Marketing team ran those jobs in Asana. Change any title, owner, date or priority, add or remove tasks and sections, then press **Create project**. Nothing is saved before that. Each owner gets one notice for all of their tasks.

A yellow box lists people who would be above their usual week with the new tasks.

## Team workload

**Team workload** on the Projects page shows every person with open tasks and the weeks ahead. A usual week is that person's own median over the last 12 weeks. A week turns amber above one and a half times it and red above twice it. Urgent tasks count extra. Five or more past-due tasks marks a person as behind. Press a cell to see its tasks.

## Friday update

A project's lead or an admin presses **Friday update** on the project page. The page shows what the update would say now: tasks finished, started, pushed back, late, waiting and due in the next seven days. Turn the switch on and the update posts to the project channel every Friday at 3 PM Eastern, written by the project lead. It stays off until an owner turns it on.

## Chat

Press **Open the project channel** on a project to make its Chat channel. The lead and members join it. Type `/task Send the report @Dana due friday` in any conversation to make a task and post its card. Add a project key first, for example `/task FALL Cut the video`, to file it in that project. Hover a message and press **Make a task from this message** (on a phone, open the message and press the same row). A task card opens the task right in Chat. A reply under the card is a comment on the task, and a comment written in FavorOS is copied under the card.

## Meetings

When a meeting's notes are ready, each action item with a named owner becomes a task for that person. The task says where in the transcript it came from. Items with no owner get a **Make a task** button on the notes page. Ticking the item or the task ticks the other.

## Clips and Drive

On a task, **Record the brief** or **Record proof** opens the recorder and attaches the clip when you save. **Attach a clip** picks one you already made. **Attach from Drive** searches Drive, and **Project folder files** lists the project's folder in US Team Files.

## Calendar and email

Press the bell, then **Project notices**. Choose one email per notice, a daily summary or no email, and whether tasks you own go on your Google Calendar on their due date. Google asks once for permission to add events to your calendar.

## Expense requests

On a task, **Link one of mine** attaches one of your expense requests. The task shows when it is waiting for approval, approved and signed, or declined.
