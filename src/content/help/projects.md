Projects is where FavorOS keeps work that has an owner and a date. It is open to FavorOS admins for now, and Admin > Projects changes who can use it.

## Projects and tasks

A project has a lead, members, sections, and tasks with an owner, dates, a priority and optional Blackbaud codes. Open a task to comment, @mention someone, add subtasks, set what it waits on and attach files. A task with no project is a personal task on My tasks.

## Chat

Press **Open the project channel** on a project to make its Chat channel. The lead and members join it. Type `/task Send the report @Dana due friday` in any conversation to make a task and post its card. Add a project key first, for example `/task FALL Cut the video`, to file it in that project. Hover a message and press **Make a task from this message** (on a phone, open the message and press the same row). A task card opens the task right in Chat. A reply under the card is a comment on the task, and a comment written in FavorOS is copied under the card.

## Meetings

When a meeting's notes are ready, each action item with a named owner becomes a task for that person. The task says where in the transcript it came from. Items with no owner get a **Make a task** button on the notes page. Ticking the item or the task ticks the other.

## Clips and Drive

On a task, **Record the brief** or **Record proof** opens the recorder and attaches the clip when you save. **Attach a clip** picks one you already made. **Attach from Drive** searches Drive, and **Project folder files** lists the project's folder in US Team Files.

## Calendar and email

Press the bell, then **Project notices**. Choose one email per notice, a daily summary or no email, and whether tasks you own go on your Google Calendar on their due date. Google asks once for permission to add events to your calendar.

## Expense requests

On a task, **Link one of mine** attaches one of your expense requests. The task shows when it is waiting for approval, approved and signed, or declined.
