Onboarding is in Staff (it moved from Admin in October 2026) for FavorOS admins and for people an admin marked as HR in Admin, People and roles. A step owner or a supervisor sees the runs they are part of.

## Start a new hire

Press **Start onboarding**. Enter the name, the Favor email, the role, the team, the supervisor and the start date, and say whether the person works at headquarters. The Favor email has to exist first, so Michael creates it in Google Admin before you start. An optional note appears on the person's first-day page.

FavorOS builds the checklist from the HR list: the employee file, the paperwork site, the staff directory, WhatsApp, Google Groups, the shared drive, the Blackbaud user, Paycom, equipment, the first-day welcome, the calendar events, training and a 30-day check-in. Each step has an owner by duty and a due date counted from the start date. A weekend date moves to the Friday before or the Monday after.

Every step a person does is also a task in the Onboarding project in Projects, so it shows on that person's Today and in their notices. Marking the step or the task done does the same thing in both places.

## Owners

The **Owners** tab names one person for each duty: the HR file and shared drive, the staff directory and groups, WhatsApp, training and technical access. A duty with no owner sends its steps to whoever starts the run, and the run page says so.

## The HR file

The employee file stays where HR keeps it. The step **Start the employee file** has a list of documents with a box to tick when each one arrives. FavorOS keeps the ticks and nothing from the documents. The new hire fills in the forms on onboard.favorintl.org.

## Add to FavorOS

On the step **Add to FavorOS**, press the button and read what it will do before you confirm. It creates the FavorOS account, puts the person on the staff list, sets the supervisor and start date, joins the default Chat channels, assigns the two New hire paths in Learn with due dates, and emails the person a link to their first-day page. Each line of the result says done, skipped or failed.

## The first week, by role

The first-day page shows five days built for the new hire's role. FavorOS reads the role from the team and title on the run (Regional Development Director, Partner Care, Church Engagement, Grants, Marketing, Admin desk or Support Team) and falls back to a general first week when the team matches none of them.

Day 1 is the FavorOS tour, FavorOS basics and a first conversation with the supervisor. Day 2 is Favor: who we are and requests, expenses and Clips. Day 3 is Blackbaud: your first week and the partner page. Day 4 is the role's own courses and the practice record. Day 5 is Favor Brain and a check-in with the supervisor. Each course ticks when the new hire finishes it in Learn, and the page shows how many of the courses and exercises are done.

**Add to FavorOS** also assigns the Learn path for the role, with a due date seven days after the start date. It adds it to the two New hire paths, and a role with no path of its own gets only those.

## Offboarding

Press **Start offboarding**, pick the person, the supervisor and the last day. If the person stays on as a volunteer, tick that box: their accounts stay open and the closing steps are left out. The checklist covers the exit conversation, handing over open work, equipment, FavorOS, Google, Paycom, Blackbaud, the KPI dashboard, Favor Brain, the website, GoHighLevel, the directory and WhatsApp, shared logins, files and the roster.

The side panel lists what the person still owns: open tasks, request cards, direct reports and Learn assignments. **Hand over** moves tasks, request cards and the people who report to them to the person you pick. Nothing is deleted. Credit for gifts dated in their time stays with them.

**Close FavorOS access** blocks the account, ends open sign-ins, removes the Google link, takes the person off the staff list and closes their Learn assignments. It asks you to confirm. Google Workspace suspension needs a Workspace admin and stays a step on the list.

## Change the checklist

The **Checklist** tab holds the built-in list for onboarding and offboarding. Edit a title, a detail, the owner, the number of days from the start date and the phase, switch a step off, or add one. Changes apply to runs you start afterwards. **Back to the built-in list** removes your edits.

## Reminders

Each step owner gets one email when a run starts. On weekday mornings after 8:00 each owner gets one email for steps due tomorrow, due today or late, at most once a day. These follow the same switch as request notices, so nothing is sent while notices are off.
