Gift entry is for the mail day. You photograph each check and reply slip, the page reads it, and every gift is checked against the adding machine tape before anything goes to Blackbaud. It runs in a phone browser and on a desktop. Admins can use it for now.

## Set up the deposit

Press New mail deposit. Pick Regular, Acquisition or Grant mail, set the deposit date, and enter the tape total and the number of items on the tape. Regular and acquisition mail are separate deposits. The page builds the name, for example Regular Mail 2026-10-09.

## Take the photos

Press Take photos on a phone to open the camera inside the page. Fill the frame with one check, hold still, and press the shutter. A blurry photo is turned away so you can take it again. If the camera will not open, use Camera app or Choose photos. Cash has no photo, so press Add cash row and type the amount.

Photos are stored in a private bucket that only admins can open. A card number is never kept: if one shows in a photo, the photo is thrown away and the row tells you to use Phone gift.

## Check each gift

Two readers read every photo. A gold mark on an amount, check number, date or name means the two readers disagree, the figures do not match the written words, or the check date is more than 45 days old. Point at the mark to see why.

The page matches the name on the check to a partner and offers a short list when more than one record fits. Pick the right one, or search by name, street, phone or email. It fills the fund as Where Needed Most and the appeal from the partner's last gift, the fallback the Admin Desk guide uses. Change either one.

A designated fund, a gift of $5,000 or more, and a giving fund or foundation also copy their photo to the gift in Blackbaud later. A giving fund asks who recommended the gift.

Every row needs one glance from you. Open a row, check the photo against the fields, and press Looks right. Changing a field takes the glance back so you look again. The button Looks right for clean rows confirms the rows with no highlights at once.

## Duplicates

The page warns when the same check number from the same partner is already in another deposit, when the same photo was filed before, and when Blackbaud already has the same amount from that partner within three weeks. Remove the row or keep it. Nothing is skipped without your decision.

## The tape

The bar at the top shows the tape, what you have photographed, and the difference. The count and the total must both equal the tape. A partner nobody can find can be marked Enter by hand in Blackbaud, which keeps the row on the tape and leaves it out of the batch.

## Create the batch in Blackbaud

When the total and the count equal the tape and every row says Looks right, press Create batch in Blackbaud. The hub makes an unapproved batch and adds every gift to it with the check number, check date, fund, appeal and a Reference such as "[Channel: Mail check] Regular Mail 2026-10-09, check 4410, hub gg_...". The soft credit goes on a gift when you named who recommended it. Blackbaud numbers the batch itself, and the batch description names the deposit and who entered it. The 6 AM gift run still sets constituency and fundraiser credit, the same as for every gift today.

The hub never approves a batch. Jennifer approves it in Blackbaud, in Gifts, then Gift batch entry, the way she does now. Blackbaud cannot tell who photographed the checks, because every batch the hub makes shows the hub's login as the creator. The batch description and the hub's log carry the real name.

## Watch the status

The status view shows each step. The batch reads Waiting for approval in Blackbaud until it turns approved, and the page checks every 20 seconds while it is open. If Blackbaud holds a different number of gifts or dollars than the hub sent, the page says so.

If Blackbaud does not answer, the hub waits 1, 5, 15 and then 60 minutes and tries again, and it asks Blackbaud what the batch holds before it sends anything twice. A gift Blackbaud stores with an error shows the reason on its row. Fix it in Blackbaud's batch grid and press Entered by hand. Press Try again for a deposit that Blackbaud turned down whole.

Gift entry has its own lane of 400 Blackbaud calls a day, shown at the top of the page. It never uses the website's share or the Work Center's. At 400 it waits for the reset at 8 PM Eastern and sends after.
