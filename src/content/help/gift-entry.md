Gift entry sits in **Gifts**, the area for the gift day, beside Online gifts, Receipts, Thank-you letters, Statements and Declines. It is for the admin desk and Partner Care. A photo of a check becomes a gift to approve. Nothing goes to Blackbaud until a person presses Approve on every gift and another step sends the batch.

## Gifts > Overview

The Overview puts the gift day on one screen. Six numbers run across the top: deposits started today, online gifts to approve, gifts waiting for a second look, deposits in Blackbaud this month, receipts marked thanked, and gifts of $5,000 and up this month for thank-you letters. Each one opens its page. Below them, a chart shows the gifts Blackbaud took in each weekday, split into checks and cash, card and online, and other. A stock or DAF gift can be far larger than a day of checks, so the chart caps each bar at $25,000 and says so. The month's mail deposits, the online gifts waiting for approval and the newest thank-you letters sit beside it.

## The Gift entry page

The page opens on **Today's deposit**. On a Monday, Wednesday or Friday it says the day is a pickup day. Pick Regular, Acquisition or Grant mail, check the date, type the tape total and the number of items, and press **Start photographing**. The page builds the name, for example Regular Mail 2026-10-09. The five steps under it show where a deposit goes: count and tape, photograph, review, send to Blackbaud, approved in Blackbaud.

**Deposits** lists every mail deposit of the month, whoever entered it. A batch typed straight into Blackbaud shows with **Typed in Blackbaud** and the day it was entered; a deposit made here shows **FavorOS** and opens when you press it. The list reads the Blackbaud copy, which can be up to 12 hours behind, so a batch typed this morning shows after the next copy. **Waiting after entry** links Receipts, Thank-you letters and the gifts missing a check or letter image. **Needs coding** lists White Mail gifts that still need an appeal.

## Start a deposit

Use the Today's deposit card at the top of the page, or press Start a deposit in search (Ctrl K).

## Capture

On a phone, press Take photos, fill the frame with one check, and press the shutter. Photograph the reply slip right after its check and it joins that gift. On a desktop, press Choose photos or drop scanned images on the capture card. Cash has no photo: press Cash row and type the amount. A card number is never kept. If one shows in a photo, the photo is thrown away.

## Review

An open deposit has a band across the top: the tape, what is entered and whether it ties to the tape, how many gifts are approved, how many need a look, and **Send to Blackbaud**, which opens once every gift is approved. The gifts are listed on the left with a dot for each one's state: green approved, amber to review, red a problem, grey held. The chips over the list show all of them, the ones to review, the held ones or the approved ones. The photo sits in the middle with the partner's giving history under it (gifts before this one, the lifetime total and the last gift), and the fields on the right. A gift of $5,000 and up says it goes to the thank-you letters list after approval, a personal contact appeal names whose it is, and a partner's first gift says so. On a phone the gift comes first and the list follows.

Each gift shows beside its photo: the partner, amount, check number and date, name on the check, fund, appeal and memo. A dot beside a field shows how sure the page is. Green means both readers agree, amber means one reader or no second check, red means the readers disagree or nothing was read. Under the appeal and fund, a small line says where the value came from: the reply slip, the memo, the last gift, or the White Mail table.

A check never carries an appeal code, so the page does not read one from the check. The appeal comes from what came with it: a reply slip or business reply envelope first, then a letter or note in the envelope. Photograph each one with the buttons under What each reader saw (Reply slip, Reply envelope, Letter). A slip taken right after its check joins that gift. When nothing came with the check, the gift is coded White Mail by the decision table (church, foundation, how often the partner gives, amount) and goes on the Needs coding list. Under the appeal, one line says why, for example Reply slip L26B-AD or No slip or note: White Mail. Suggestions from a code in the memo, an expected gift someone logged for the partner, or the partner's last gift show as buttons with their reasons. They are not decided until a person presses one or picks another appeal.

The fund comes from the slip, then the letter, then an expected gift, then the memo, and is Where Needed Most when none names one.

## A gift someone said was coming

When a check matches a partner, the page reads that partner's record for a gift someone was told to expect: an opportunity with an expected date within six weeks, or a call, meeting or note from the last two months with a sentence such as "a gift for the Chad wells is on its way" or "he plans to send $5,000". Emails and mailings are not read. The amount has to fit: no more than a fifth over what the note said.

The fund the note describes goes in the Fund box with **Suggested** and the reason under it, for example "Sep 28 call: She said a gift for the Chad wells is on its way". The words are matched to the fund list, so "the Chad wells" finds the Chad wells fund. Under the appeal, a button offers the personal contact appeal of the person who made the call (Gift due to personal contact), with the same reason. The appeal stays White Mail until you press the button or pick another one, and you can change the fund like any other field. The page never creates a partner. When no record fits, search for one or enter the gift by hand in Blackbaud.

Press **Approve** or A. The page moves to the next gift that needs a look. **Hold** (H) and **Reject** (R) ask for a reason. J and K move between gifts, and E jumps to the amount. A rejected gift keeps its photo and leaves the count. A held gift stops the batch until someone takes it off hold.

An admin can set an amount from which a second person must also approve a gift. The second person is never the first. The setting is off until an admin saves an amount.

## Send

The batch can be sent when the gifts equal the tape in count and dollars and every gift is approved. Posting is off for everyone until an admin switches a person on under People. Without it, the page says Posting is not switched on and everything above still works. Sending creates an unapproved batch in Blackbaud. Jennifer approves it there, as she does now.

## Take it back

On the day a batch was sent, and while Blackbaud still shows it unapproved, Take batch back deletes it and reopens the deposit. Approvals stay, so fix the one gift and send again. After Blackbaud approves a batch, a gift is voided in Blackbaud.

## Day summary

The Day summary on the front page lists each deposit for a day: the tape, what was entered and approved, who approved, what is held or rejected, and whether the day ties to the tape.

## After the batch is approved

Once Jennifer approves the batch in Blackbaud, each gift joins the thank-you receipts queue on the Receipts page, which prints a receipt for every partner who gives. A gift at the level that earns a handwritten thank-you letter appears on the HQ thank-you letters list in the Work Center when the Work Center rule says so. The receipts run never prints those letters.

## The daily revenue report

When the batch is in Blackbaud, the deposit page shows **Daily revenue report**, built from the gifts in this batch: the total for the day, the number of gifts, the gifts of $1,000 and up, and the totals by post block. It does not wait for Blackbaud's copy to refresh. **Copy for WhatsApp** puts the post on the clipboard, and **The post** shows it. Held and rejected gifts are left out. A gift with no partner picked is left out and counted under the totals. The same report for any day is under Reports, Daily Revenue Report.
