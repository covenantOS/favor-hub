Mail shows your own Gmail inside FavorOS. Nobody else sees your mail. To make the list fast, FavorOS saves for each conversation the sender, the subject, a one-line preview, the labels and the results of sorting it (its category and whether it waits on you). It never saves message bodies or attachments; those are read from Gmail when you open them, and a copy stays on your own device for seven days. Disconnecting Google or removing a mailbox deletes the saved rows for it. If Mail is turned on for you, it shows in the menu. Admin > Settings > Chat > Mail sets who has it: admins, all staff, chosen people and teams, or nobody.

## Allowing it

Google asks once, the first time you sign in to FavorOS. That one screen covers your mail, calendar, Drive and contacts for every tool here, Mail included.

If you signed in before that screen covered Mail, Google asks one more time when you open Mail. Leave the mail boxes unticked and Mail stays read only. **Open in Gmail** is always in the **More** menu.

## The screen

A computer shows three columns: the mailbox list, the conversation list and the open conversation. The search box at the top of the list takes the key **/** (shown at its right edge). Start a search with **?** to ask in plain words.

### The mailbox list

**Compose** starts a new message. **Add a mailbox** connects another Favor Google account (see below). Then, in order:

- **Inbox**, with the number of unread conversations. Hover it to see how many are in Primary and how many in the other tabs. It is the same number as on the Mail icon in the menu and on Today, it changes the moment you read or archive here, and it catches up within a minute of reading somewhere else.
- Under Inbox, the sorted views: **Needs you**, **Partners**, **Staff**, **People**, **Receipts and gifts**, **Newsletters**, **Notifications**, **Calendar**, **Not logged**, any category you made, and **Waiting on reply** when sent mail has had no answer. A view shows only when it has conversations in it, and each shows a count.
- **New category** (see Categories in your words).
- **Starred**, **Snoozed**, **Sent**, **Drafts** (with how many), **Scheduled**, **Assigned**, **All mail**, **Spam** and **Trash**.
- **Labels**, your own Gmail labels, each with a colored dot.

**Scheduled** lists messages waiting to go, with **Cancel** on each. **Assigned** lists the emails you handed to a teammate, who has each one and your note, with **Done** on each.

### The conversation list

The header row has a **Select all** box, the name of the list, **Filter**, **Sort** and **Refresh**.

- **Filter** offers **Unread**, **Starred**, **Has attachments** and, in the Inbox, **From a partner**. Ticked filters show as a chip with an x that clears them. Snoozed has no filter.
- **Sort** offers **Newest first**, **Oldest first** and **Most important first** (partner and major partner mail on top). A tick shows the current choice.
- **Refresh** reads the list again from Gmail.

Each row shows the sender (several names with a count when there is a thread), the time, the subject, a one-line preview and small tags: **Needs you** when it asks you something, **Partner** when the sender is a partner, **Sent 3 days ago** on sent mail with no answer, and a paper clip when it has a file. Bold means unread. The star on the right stars the conversation.

Hold the pointer over a row for quick buttons: **Archive**, **Delete**, **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Snooze**, and **Follow up** on sent mail still waiting for an answer. **Follow up** opens a reply that starts "I am following up on my last note" for you to edit. **Show more** at the bottom of the list reads older conversations.

At the top of the inbox three cards can appear. **This morning** names the conversations to start with and why (its arrows button refreshes it, the x closes it). A yellow card counts partner email not logged in Blackbaud, with **Review** and an x (**Not this week**). A card about newsletters and notices you never open offers **Archive all** and an x (**Not now**).

### Reading a conversation

Press a conversation to read it. The toolbar above it, left to right:

- **Archive** (or **Move to Inbox** when it is already out of the inbox or in Trash), **Delete**, **Mark as unread**, **Snooze**, **Labels**.
- **Hand off** gives it to a teammate with a note. See [Inbox, Waiting on and Hand off](/help/inbox-and-hand-off/).
- **Find a time** lists free times for you and the staff on the thread.
- **Reply with a clip** records a clip in place and puts its link in a reply.
- **More** opens the list under Acting on a conversation.
- **Newer conversation** and **Older conversation** (the up and down arrows).

Under the subject sit the sender's name and, when it applies, **Waiting on you**. Earlier messages fold to one line; press one to open it. Each message has its own **Star**, **Reply** and **More for this message** (**Reply**, **Reply all** when there are several recipients, **Forward** and **Reply in a new window**). The three dots under a message show the quoted text. A file opens in the viewer without leaving the page, and the arrow beside it downloads it. **Reply**, **Reply all** and **Forward** also sit under the last message.

When the conversation is between staff, a line asks **This is a team conversation. Continue in Chat?** **Move to Chat** posts the email history, pinned, in a Chat conversation with the same people and offers **Post a note in the email thread that this moved to Chat**. The x hides the suggestion. Once moved, the line reads **This conversation moved to Chat** with **Open in Chat**.

Search takes Gmail's own words: `from:name`, `to:name`, `has:attachment`, `is:unread`, `before:2026/10/01`, `label:trips`. An empty result says so and repeats those examples.

## More than one mailbox

Under **Mailboxes** on the left, **Add a mailbox** connects another Favor Google account, such as an accounting or admin inbox or a second account of yours. Google asks you to pick the account and allows Gmail only. Only favorintl.org accounts can be added, up to five, and only you can see a mailbox you add.

**All mailboxes** merges them into one list. A colored dot beside each sender shows which mailbox the conversation came to, and each mailbox has its own unread count. A reply goes out from the mailbox the conversation came to. The **From** line in a message lists each mailbox and the addresses Gmail lets it send as. **Manage mailboxes** removes one; FavorOS forgets the connection and nothing is deleted in Gmail. The number on the Mail icon counts every mailbox.

## Mail knows Favor

**This morning** at the top of the inbox names the few conversations to start with and why: a major partner (one gift of $1,000 or more), a partner, mail that waits on you, or a star. **Most important first** in Sort puts partner and major partner mail on top.

A card above each message says who the sender is to Favor: staff, a partner in Blackbaud, or someone outside, with how many conversations you have had. For someone outside, **Add as prospect** makes a task for Support. For a partner, the card shows giving, who holds them and the last contact, **Log as an action** logs the email in one press, and **Timeline** lists their gifts, calls, meetings and mail together.

An appeal code, a Lookup ID or a fund name in a message is underlined. Hover for a card, press for the details. A partner who writes about a gift shows a card with two buttons, one that notes the expected gift in Blackbaud and one that tells Partner Care in Chat. Each asks you first. Promises in mail you sent ("I will send the itinerary by Friday") show under **You promised** with a Make a task button.

**Not logged** lists partner email from the last 7 days that is not logged in Blackbaud, and a note at the top of the inbox reminds you once a week.

**New category** under the inbox views opens **Categories in your words**. Give it a name and a sentence ("Anything about a mission trip"). Mail reads each new email against your words and adds the category as a view under Inbox. Your categories are listed in the same window. **Move to category** still teaches it by sender.

**Ask about this email** (the box under the subject, with **Ask**) answers a question from the conversation alone, and a line under it says Favor Brain answered from this email. **Ask Favor Brain** beside it opens Favor Brain in a new tab with the email as context. **Find a time** lists free times for you and the staff on the thread, puts them in a reply or books a FavorOS meeting after you confirm. A file shows what it is (a check, receipt, W-9, grant letter or photo) and the next step. Start a search with **?** to ask in plain words, like "?major partners who wrote this month".

**Before this goes** checks a message for the word "donor", a greeting for someone other than the partner, an amount that differs from the earlier mail, "attached" with no file, and no subject. It opens **Send anyway?** with **Fix it** and **Send now**. Send now counts down from 5 and sends on its own unless you tick **Wait for me to press Send**. **Z** undoes the last archive, delete or snooze. **Unsubscribe** is in More on a newsletter.

## Tabs, filters and sorting

Above the inbox, tabs sort your newest 100 conversations. **Needs you** holds what asks you a question or waits on your answer, oldest first. **Partners** are senders whose address is on a partner in Blackbaud. **Staff**, **People**, **Receipts and gifts**, **Newsletters**, **Notifications** and **Calendar** hold the rest, each with a count. **Waiting on reply** lists mail you sent that has had no answer for three days.

**Filter** and **Sort** at the top of the list work in every tab, as described above.

To change where a sender belongs, select a conversation and press **Move to category**. Every later email from that address goes there.

When a handful of newsletters and notices from the same senders sit unread, a card at the top of the inbox offers **Archive all**, with Undo.

## Choosing several

Press the circle beside a sender and it turns into a check. Press more circles to add to the choice, or Shift click to take a range. **Select all** at the top of the list takes every loaded conversation. The bar above the list shows how many are selected and has **Clear selection**, **Archive**, **Delete**, **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Label**, **Snooze** and **Move to category**. **x** selects the highlighted conversation and **Esc** clears the choice.

## From a partner

When the sender is a partner, a card above the message shows lifetime giving, the last gift, who holds the partner and the last contact. **Log as an action** enters the email on the partner in Blackbaud. Long threads start with a one line summary, and **Suggested replies** offers three short replies. Pressing one opens the message window with that text for you to edit. Nothing sends until you press Send.

## Acting on a conversation

Archive, delete and snooze show **Undo** for a few seconds.

**Snooze** takes the conversation out of the inbox until the time you pick: **Later today**, **Tomorrow**, **This weekend**, **Next week**, or **Pick a date and time** and **Snooze**. On one conversation it also offers **Until they reply**, and **Until their next gift** when the sender is a partner. It comes back unread the next time you open Mail or Today after that time. Snoozed lists what is waiting.

**Labels** lists your Gmail labels with a box for each. Tick the ones that apply, type a name under **New label** and press **Add** to make one, then press **Apply**. On several selected conversations it applies to all of them.

**More** links a conversation to the rest of FavorOS:

- **Make a task** adds a task for you with the subject as its title.
- **Send to Chat** posts the subject, the sender and the text to a channel or a person.
- **Assign to a teammate** hands the email to one person on staff. Type a name, pick the person, write what you need from them and press Assign. They get a notice in Chat with your note and the email's subject. **Assigned to teammates** in the same menu lists what you handed off and to whom, with Done on each.
- **Mention a coworker** tells one or more people about the email without handing it off. Pick the names, write a note and press Tell them. Each gets a notice in Chat.
- **Make a request** puts a request on the request board in your name, with the email's subject, sender and first lines. Pick where the change lands (FavorOS, Website, Portal or App) and write what needs to change.
- **Ask Favor Brain** opens Favor Brain in a new tab with a question about this email: the subject, who sent it and when. Type your own question in the Ask box first, or press it empty to ask what to do about the email.
- **Find a time** is the same as the calendar button in the toolbar.
- **Open partner** shows when the sender's address is on a partner in Blackbaud.
- **Log as an action in Blackbaud** enters the email as a contact on that partner through the Work Center's entry, with the date and a one line summary you can edit. It shows for people who have the Work Center.
- **Unsubscribe** shows on a newsletter. It opens the sender's page or sends the sender an email.
- **Open in Gmail** opens the conversation in Gmail.

Nothing goes to anyone until you press the button in the sheet. On a phone, More also holds Mark as unread, Snooze and Star.

## Writing

**Compose**, **Reply**, **Reply all** and **Forward** open the message window. A new message floats at the bottom right over any page and stays open while you move between pages. A reply opens under the conversation so the two scroll together. Three windows can be open at once.

The window's buttons:

- **Minimize** (or press the title), **Full screen** and **Close**. Close keeps the draft. On a reply under the conversation, **Pop out** moves it into a floating window (Shift and click **Reply** opens it popped out), and a popped window has **Dock at the bottom** to put it back.
- **To**, with **Cc Bcc** to add those rows, and **Subject**, at the top. Type a name in To and pick from staff first, then your Google contacts. With more than one mailbox, **From** picks the one to send as. The title line says whether this is a new email, a reply or a forward and what it is about.
- On a computer, **Side by side with the page** opens an email you started from a page next to that page instead of over it, and **Dock at the bottom** puts it back. Minimized drafts stack in the corner.
- The message box holds only what you write. Your signature sits under it as one small line, **Signature**. Press it to read the signature, and use the off switch in the open line to leave it off this message. On a reply, **Show the earlier message** shows the quoted text.

The row under the message box, left to right:

- **Send** and, beside it, **Send later**. The time list is **This afternoon**, **Tomorrow morning**, **Tomorrow afternoon**, **Monday morning**, or **Pick a date and time** and **Schedule**. A box asks FavorOS to remind you if nobody replies in 3 days. A message with files cannot wait. **Scheduled** in the mailbox list shows and cancels them.
- **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Link**, **Bulleted list**, **Numbered list**.
- **Attach files** (or drop them on the window), up to 24 MB together. **Insert** holds the rest, without a download. **A Drive file** searches your Drive and puts a link in the message, **A clip** adds a link to one of your Clips, **Photos from Drive** searches Favor's photo library by what is in them and puts the ones you pick in the message, and **A template** opens your saved messages.
- **Templates** (**A template** under **Insert**) lists your saved messages. Fields fill from the person on the To line: {first_name}, {name}, {last_gift}, {last_gift_date}, {total_giving} and {my_name}. Type a name under **Save this message as a template** and press **Save** to add one.
- **Discard draft**.

A draft saves a few seconds after you stop typing and shows under Drafts in Gmail too. Press Ctrl Enter to send. **Send** runs **Before this goes**, then holds the message for 15 seconds with **Undo**, and the hold carries on if you change pages.

## Put something from FavorOS into an email

You can move an item into an email from the page where you found it. Two ways work everywhere.

- **Send to email** starts a new email with the item in it. If you already have drafts open, it asks which draft gets the item, with **New email** first.
- **Drag** the item, or its **Drag** handle, onto an open email, onto the **Drop here to start an email** box that appears while you drag, or into any text box in FavorOS.

Send to email is on a Favor Brain answer, a clip (in My clips), a task (in its menu) and meeting notes. A Favor Brain answer arrives as the question for a title, the main figure, the table and a Source line with the time the numbers are from. Meeting notes arrive as the summary, decisions and action items, with the meeting's people on the To line. Partner details in an answer go only to people who may share them.

## Is there an easier way?

Instead of copying a Favor Brain table or a task into a blank email by hand, press **Send to email** on it, or drag it in. The title, figure, table and source line arrive already laid out.

The same keys as Gmail. Press **?** for **Keyboard shortcuts**, the list below.

| e | Archive |
| # | Delete |
| s | Star |
| x | Select the highlighted conversation |

## On a phone

The list, a conversation and a new message are each a full screen. **Back** at the top left returns to the list, and the mailbox list opens from the menu button. The bar under a conversation has **Archive**, **Delete**, **Labels**, **Reply** and **New message**. Hand off, Find a time and the other actions are under **More**.
