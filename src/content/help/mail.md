Mail shows your own Gmail inside FavorOS. Nobody else sees your mail, and FavorOS keeps no copy of it. It is open to admins for now, and Admin > Settings > Chat > Mail changes who can use it.

## Allowing it

Google asks once, the first time you sign in to FavorOS. That one screen covers your mail, calendar, Drive and contacts for every tool here, Mail included.

If you signed in before that screen covered Mail, Google asks one more time when you open Mail. Leave the mail boxes unticked and Mail stays read only. **Open in Gmail** is always in the **More** menu.

## Reading

Mailboxes are on the left: Inbox, Starred, Snoozed, Sent, Drafts, All mail, Spam, Trash, then your labels. The number beside Inbox is unread conversations in your Primary tab, the number Gmail shows beside Inbox and the same number as on the Mail icon in the menu. Hover it to see the count across every tab. It changes the moment you read or archive here, and a minute after you read somewhere else.

Press a conversation to read it. Earlier messages fold to one line; press one to open it. The three dots under a message show the quoted text. A file opens in the viewer without leaving the page, and the arrow beside it downloads it.

Search takes Gmail's own words: `from:name`, `to:name`, `has:attachment`, `is:unread`, `before:2026/10/01`, `label:trips`.

## More than one mailbox

Under **Mailboxes** on the left, **Add a mailbox** connects another Favor Google account, such as an accounting or admin inbox or a second account of yours. Google asks you to pick the account and allows Gmail only. Only favorintl.org accounts can be added, up to five, and only you can see a mailbox you add.

**All mailboxes** merges them into one list. A colored dot beside each sender shows which mailbox the conversation came to, and each mailbox has its own unread count. A reply goes out from the mailbox the conversation came to. The **From** line in a message lists each mailbox and the addresses Gmail lets it send as. **Manage mailboxes** removes one; FavorOS forgets the connection and nothing is deleted in Gmail. The number on the Mail icon counts every mailbox.

## Tabs, filters and sorting

Above the inbox, tabs sort your newest 100 conversations. **Needs you** holds what asks you a question or waits on your answer, oldest first. **Partners** are senders whose address is on a partner in Blackbaud. **Staff**, **People**, **Receipts and gifts**, **Newsletters**, **Notifications** and **Calendar** hold the rest, each with a count. **Waiting on reply** lists mail you sent that has had no answer for three days.

**Unread**, **Starred**, **Has attachments** and **From a partner** narrow whatever list you are in. **Newest first** switches to **Oldest first**.

To change where a sender belongs, select a conversation and press **Move to category**. Every later email from that address goes there.

When a handful of newsletters and notices from the same senders sit unread, a card at the top of the inbox offers **Archive all**, with Undo.

## Choosing several

Press the circle beside a sender and it turns into a check. Press more circles to add to the choice, or Shift click to take a range. **Select all** at the top of the list takes every loaded conversation. The bar above the list then archives, deletes, marks read or unread, labels, snoozes or moves them to a category. **x** selects the highlighted conversation and **Esc** clears the choice.

## From a partner

When the sender is a partner, a card above the message shows lifetime giving, the last gift, who holds the partner and the last contact. **Log as an action** enters the email on the partner in Blackbaud. Long threads start with a one line summary, and **Suggested replies** offers three short replies. Pressing one opens the message window with that text for you to edit. Nothing sends until you press Send.

## Acting on a conversation

The buttons above a conversation archive it, delete it, mark it unread, snooze it and change its labels. Archive, delete and snooze show **Undo** for a few seconds.

Snooze takes the conversation out of the inbox until the time you pick. It comes back unread the next time you open Mail or Today after that time. Snoozed lists what is waiting.

**More** has four links to the rest of FavorOS:

- **Make a task** adds a task for you with the subject as its title.
- **Send to Chat** posts the subject, the sender and the text to a channel or a person.
- **Open partner** shows when the sender's address is on a partner in Blackbaud.
- **Log as an action in Blackbaud** enters the email as a contact on that partner through the Work Center's entry, with the date and a one line summary you can edit.

## Writing

**Compose**, **Reply**, **Reply all** and **Forward** open the message window. Type a name in To and pick from staff first, then your Google contacts. The paper clip attaches files up to 24 MB together; the Drive and clip buttons put a link in the message. A draft saves a few seconds after you stop typing and shows under Drafts in Gmail too. **Send** waits five seconds with **Undo**.

## Keys

The same keys as Gmail. Press **?** for the list.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| j and k | Older and newer conversation |
| o or Enter | Open |
| u or Esc | Back to the list |
| e | Archive |
| # | Delete |
| s | Star |
| x | Select the highlighted conversation |
| Shift U, Shift I | Mark unread, mark read |
| b | Snooze |
| l | Labels |
| r, a, f | Reply, reply all, forward |
| c | Compose |
| / | Search |
| Ctrl Enter | Send |

## On a phone

The list, a conversation and a new message are each a full screen. **Back** at the top left returns to the list. The bar under a conversation archives, deletes, labels, replies and starts a new message.
