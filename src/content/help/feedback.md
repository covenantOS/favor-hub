## Send a note

1. Press **Feedback** at the top right of any page.
2. Pick what kind of note it is: something's wrong, confusing, an idea, or works well.
3. Write a few words. Say which button, number or page if it helps.
4. Press **Send**.

The note goes with the page you were on, so you don't have to explain where you were.

## Other ways to send one

- **Ask Favor answers**: press **Right** or **Not right** beside a question on the Favor Brain page, or tell your AI what was off.
- **Help articles**: answer **Was this helpful?** at the bottom of any article.
- **The tour**: say whether it helped when it ends.

## Getting the answer

Every note is read. When Will answers, the answer shows on your **Feedback** page, a number appears beside Feedback in the menu, and Today shows a card until you read it. Each note also shows where it stands: waiting, read, fixed, or not now.

## For the hub admin

The Feedback page lists every note, newest first, with who sent it, where from, and for Ask Favor notes, the exact question and how it was read. Mark each one **Mark read**, **Fixed** or **Not now**, and write an answer when it needs one. New notes count beside Feedback in the menu and show on Today.
