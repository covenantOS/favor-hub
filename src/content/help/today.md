Today opens every time you sign in. It answers two questions on one screen: what needs you right now, and how Favor is doing.

## The line under the greeting

Under your name is the date, how many things need you (and how many are late), and how many meetings are left today with the time of the next one. Press the count to jump to Needs you.

After a day or more away, a second line shows what changed while you were gone: new gifts from your partners, updates on tasks you follow and new Chat messages. Each one opens its page. Close the line with the X.

## Needs you

One list of everything waiting on you, the most urgent first. The chip at the start of each row says what it is:

- **Reply**: someone asked you a question and is waiting on your answer.
- **Approval**: an expense waiting for your signature.
- **Thank**: a gift on the 24-hour clock. Each gift is thanked within 24 hours of the time it was entered in Blackbaud, counting weekdays only. Press Thank to say how you thanked the partner. More than three gifts fold into one row. Press it to open the list in place.
- **Task**, **Request**, **Hand-off**, **Evaluation**: work given to you in FavorOS. A hand-off opens with Pick up.
- **Actions**: your Blackbaud actions due today or late, in one row. Press it to see each action and open it in the Work Center.
- **Chat** and **Mail**: mentions, messages and email that need you. Mark read or Done clears the row, with Undo on the message that follows.
- **Call**: partners worth a call (a first gift, a largest gift, a giving anniversary, or a partner who has gone quiet). Not now hides one for two weeks.
- **Waiting**: the last row folds together what you are waiting on from others, each with Nudge, which sends a short reminder the way the thread started (a reply on the email, a Chat message or a comment on the task).

The tabs narrow the list to Messages, Work, Partners or Waiting on others. The first eight rows show; Show all opens the rest. The clock on a row hides it until tomorrow, next week or a day you pick. Partner directors and admins can pick whose partners the gift rows cover at the foot of the list.

Log a contact and New task sit at the foot of the list for people with the Work Center. Make a request is the button at the top right of every page, and Ctrl K starts anything else.

## Schedule

Every event on your Google Calendar for today, whether it is held in FavorOS, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or on the phone. A FavorOS meeting opens its room. Any other meeting opens a sheet with **Join**, **Move to FavorOS** and **Open in Google Calendar**. Meetings that already ended fold into one Earlier row. When there is room, Coming up shows your next meetings this week.

Press **Move to FavorOS** on a meeting held somewhere else. FavorOS makes a meeting at the same time with the same people, not recorded. A box in the sheet, checked at the start, emails each person on the meeting (never you) the FavorOS link. Once it is moved, Today shows the meeting as a FavorOS meeting.

If you have not connected Google, the card has **Connect my Google**.

## How Favor is doing

If you can see the KPI dashboard, this card shows money raised this year against the goal, the change from this day last year, where the year ends at this pace, and each team's progress. Press the amount raised or a team's amount to see its Favor definition and the gifts behind it.

Everyone who holds partners also sees the new gifts since the last workday and how many gifts are waiting for a thank-you.

## Cards for your job

Under How Favor is doing come the cards that fit your job:

- **Your week** shows a director's goals or Partner Care's cadence. The Support Team and admins see the **Entry queue**, the actions waiting to be entered for each person.
- **Gift day** shows the gift desk, Partner Care, the Support Team and admins the six numbers from Gifts > Overview, each opening its page.
- **Team** (admins) lists open and overdue actions for each person, the most overdue first. Show all lists everyone.

## What changed, your recent changes and shared files

The last card lists what happened across FavorOS in the last two weeks, the changes you made in the last 24 hours (each with Undo), and Drive files shared with you this week. A file opens in the FavorOS file viewer, the same one Chat uses.
