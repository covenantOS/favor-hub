Today opens every time you sign in. It greets you and lays out your day.

## Your briefing

The briefing at the top of Today is written for you each time you open it.

- **New gifts** lists gifts dated since the last workday from the partners you hold. An admin sees every partner, and a Support person sees the directors they cover. Thanked marks a gift that already has a thank-you.
- **To thank** lists the gifts on the 24-hour clock. Every gift is thanked within 24 hours of the time it was entered in Blackbaud (the admin desk enters the mail on Monday, Wednesday and Friday), counting weekdays only, so a gift entered at 2 PM on a Friday is due at 2 PM on Monday. Each row shows the time left (Due in 6 h, Due today, Due tomorrow), the soonest due first and the largest gift first within a day. Once the time runs out the row turns red and reads Overdue. Gifts more than a day past it fold under **Older**. Press Thank to choose how you said it. The same list is under Gifts to thank in the Work Center.
- **Late** lists your Blackbaud actions past their due date. **Today's meetings** lists your calendar with one line on each partner in the room: what they give, when anyone last spoke to them and how many gifts are not thanked.
- **Since you were away** shows after a day or more off. It counts what changed in your partners, requests, projects and Chat. Close it with the X.
- **Moments** suggests a call for a first gift, the largest gift a partner has given, a giving anniversary this week, or a partner who gave steadily and has gone quiet. Not now hides a moment for two weeks.
- **Focus** hides the rest of Today and shows your next three things: a meeting starting within the hour, late actions, the thank-yous due soonest, then actions due today. Skip moves to the next one. Show everything brings Today back.

People who do not work with partners see the late actions and their meetings, and no partner information.

## What needs you

The cards on the left are things waiting on you in FavorOS. Each card has a number, a line about the oldest item, and a button that takes you straight to the work. You only see cards that apply to you, for example:

- **Expenses**: expense requests waiting for your signature, if you approve expenses.
- **Receipts**: a print file you made that still needs printing or marking.
- **Feedback**: an answer to a note you sent.

When nothing is waiting, the card says so.

## Your work

Your work sits under What needs you. It is the Work Overview, which moved onto Today in October 2026.

- **Today's work** lists your own Blackbaud actions due today and overdue, oldest first. Press the circle to complete one, Snooze to move it, or the partner name to open the partner. With this card on the page, the Blackbaud actions card in Your day steps aside.
- **Gifts to thank** lists the gifts waiting for your thank-you, with a Thank button on each.
- **Your week** shows a director's goals, the Support Team's entry backlog or Partner Care's cadence.
- **Gift day** shows the admin desk, Partner Care, the Support Team and admins the six numbers from Gifts > Overview.
- **Your recent changes** lists the changes you made in the last 24 hours, each with Undo. Changes other people made are not listed here.
- **Team** (admins) lists open and overdue actions for each person.

Start something at the foot of the page also has **Log a contact** and **New task** for people with the Work Center, and **Start a deposit** for the gift desk.

## Snooze

Each card under What needs you and each Blackbaud action has a clock button. Press it and choose Tomorrow, Next week, or Pick a date.

- **A card** hides until that day. Its Undo brings it back at once.
- **A Blackbaud action** gets a new due date in Blackbaud, the same change the Work Center makes. It leaves the list when its date moves past the next seven days, and it shows its new date until the next full load. Undo puts the old date back for 24 hours.

## Your day

- **Blackbaud actions**: your open actions due in the next seven days, with overdue ones marked. The overdue number and the "more in Blackbaud" line cover the whole list, including the rows past the first twelve. Press one to open it in the Work Center, where the panel has **Open in Blackbaud** if you want the record there. If your FavorOS account is not linked to a fundraiser record in Blackbaud, the card says so.
- **Today's meetings**: every event on your Google Calendar today, whether it is held in FavorOS, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, on the phone or has no link. A FavorOS meeting opens its room. Any other meeting opens a sheet with **Join**, **Move to FavorOS** and **Open in Google Calendar**.
- **Shared with you**: Drive files shared with you or changed this week. A file opens in the FavorOS file viewer, the same one Chat uses, with **Open in Google Drive** inside it.
- **Unread mail**: unread mail from the last three days (sender and subject only), from your Google account.

## Move to FavorOS

Press **Move to FavorOS** on a meeting held somewhere else. FavorOS makes a meeting at the same time with the same people, not recorded. A box in the sheet, checked at the start, emails each person on the meeting (never you) the FavorOS link, the day and time in Eastern, and a file that adds it to their calendar. Staff open the room; people outside Favor get a guest link. Replies go to you, and nobody is emailed twice for one meeting. Uncheck the box and FavorOS sends no email. Your calendar event stays as it is. A box in the sheet adds one line with the FavorOS link to the notes of that event on your own Google Calendar. It shows only when you organize the event or guests may edit it, and the first time Google asks you to allow calendar edits.

## Where the year stands

If you can see the KPI dashboard, Today shows money raised against the year's goal and each team's progress, from the dashboard's own numbers. Press the amount raised or a team's amount to open a side panel with its Favor definition and the gifts behind it, with Open in Google Sheets and Download CSV.

## Recent activity and your requests

The activity list shows the last two weeks across FavorOS: requests made and finished, print files, foundation contacts. **Your requests** lists what you asked for and where each one stands.

## What changed this week

Press **Raised so far this year** on the year card. The panel opens on **This week**: the gifts dated Monday through today against the same days last week (how many and how much), the largest gifts this week and the new partners, whose first gift ever came this week. Press a name for the partner card, or the amount to open that gift on the partner page. The same panel opens from the year number in Reporting. Below it are the reasons the number moved this month, the Favor definition and every gift behind the year.
