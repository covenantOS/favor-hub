Open **Favor brain** in the menu and press **Copy** beside the address. The address is:

`https://mcp.favorintl.org/mcp`

## Claude

1. Open **claude.ai** (or the Claude app) and go to **Settings**, then **Connectors**.
2. Press **Add custom connector**. Name it **Favor** and paste the address.
3. Press **Connect**. Choose **Continue with Google** and pick your Favor account.
4. In a new chat, open the tools menu and turn **Favor** on.

## ChatGPT

1. Open **chatgpt.com**, then **Settings**, then **Apps and connectors**.
2. Under **Advanced**, turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Press **Create**. Name it **Favor**, paste the address and choose **OAuth**.
4. Sign in with your Favor Google account. In a chat, pick **Favor** from the plus menu.

## The first time

A page asks you to let the app use the Favor brain as you, then Google asks you to sign in. Use your **@favorintl.org** account. One connection per app; if it ever asks again, sign in again the same way.

## Check it worked

Ask: "Who am I in the Favor brain?" It answers with your name, your role and what you can ask about.
