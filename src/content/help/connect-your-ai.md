Open **Favor Brain** in the menu and press **Copy** beside the address. The address is:

`https://mcp.favorintl.org/mcp`

## Claude (any plan, free included)

1. Open **claude.ai** or the Claude app, sign in, then press **Customize** and **Connectors**.
2. If you use Claude through the Favor organization, **Favor** is already in your list under **Yours**. Press **Connect** beside it and go to step 4.
3. If you use your own Claude account, press **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**. Name it **Favor**, paste the address and press **Continue**.
4. A Favor page opens. Press **Continue with Google** and sign in with your **@favorintl.org** account. Claude shows "Connected to Favor."
5. Start a chat and ask a question. The first time, Claude asks to use Favor: press **Allow once**, or **Always allow** so it stops asking.

Claude's free plan allows one custom connector, which is all you need.

## ChatGPT (Business, Enterprise, Edu or Pro only)

ChatGPT lets you add a connector like Favor only on a Business, Enterprise or Edu workspace, or on a personal Pro plan. On the free and Plus plans the option is not there; use Claude instead.

1. Turn on **Developer mode** under **Settings**, **Apps**, **Advanced settings**.
2. Under **Apps**, press **Create**.
3. Name it **Favor**, paste the address and choose **OAuth** for authentication.
4. Press **Scan Tools** and sign in with your Favor Google account when it asks, then press **Create**.
5. In a chat, pick **Favor** from the tools menu.

## The first time

A page asks you to let the app use the Favor Brain as you, then Google asks you to sign in. Use your **@favorintl.org** account. One connection per app; if it ever asks again, sign in again the same way.

## Check it worked

Ask: "Who am I in the Favor Brain?" It answers with your name, your role and what you can ask about.
