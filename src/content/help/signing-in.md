## Sign in

1. Go to **dash.favorintl.org**.
2. Press **Sign in with Google** and pick your **@favorintl.org** account.
3. Google asks to share your calendar, Drive and inbox with the hub. Allow it. No page opens until Google is connected.

The old codes for receipts, foundations and the expense log are gone. Your Google sign-in opens everything your job needs.

## What the hub reads from Google

The hub reads **today's calendar**, the **names of files shared with you**, and **who emailed you**, so Today can show your day. It never opens an email or a file, and it never sends anything as you.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pick your Favor account"**: Google signed you in with a personal account. Sign in again and choose the one that ends in @favorintl.org.
- **"That took too long"**: the sign-in page sat open too long. Start again.
- **It keeps asking you to sign in**: your browser may be blocking cookies for dash.favorintl.org. Allow them, or try another browser.
- **A page says your account has no access**: send feedback or make a request with the page name, and Will looks at it.

## Signing out

Press the arrow beside your name at the bottom of the menu. On a shared computer, always sign out.
