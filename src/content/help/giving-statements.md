Giving statements replace Gifts, then Giving statements, in Blackbaud. The page reads the Blackbaud copy FavorOS keeps, so a gift entered this morning shows after the next sync. Every statement is a PDF on Favor's letterhead layout, ready to print or save.

## One statement, on request

1. Open **Giving statements** and type a name or a Blackbaud ID under **Find a partner**. Pick the partner.
2. Choose a year, or **Between two dates**. The page lists every gift that belongs on the statement and what it left off, with the reason.
3. Check the totals and any note in the yellow box. Press **Make the PDF**, then **Open the PDF** or **Download**.

FavorOS keeps a copy under **Print files**. If an admin has set a copy folder in US Team Files, the PDF is saved there too.

## What counts

- **Gifts the partner gave.** One-time gifts, monthly payments and other gift types on the partner's own record. These make the tax total.
- **Monthly gift schedules** are not money. Their payments are listed instead.
- **Declined cards.** A payment recorded at $0 stays off. A declined card recorded with an amount also stays off and shows a note, because a person has to settle it in Blackbaud first.
- **Refunds** show on their own lines and reduce the net total.
- **Not tax-deductible.** A gift to a fund named Non Tax Deductible, or a book purchase, is listed apart and not counted.
- **Stock and in-kind gifts** count, carry a marker and print the non-cash note.
- **Gifts through a fund or foundation.** When a fund, brokerage or foundation gave the money and the partner is soft-credited, the fund is the donor of record. The partner sees those gifts in a separate section that never counts toward the total. Untick **List gifts made through a fund or foundation** to leave the section off.

## The January year-end run

1. Open the **Year-end run** tab and choose the gift year. It opens on last year.
2. The totals show the Mail, Email and Other lists from the annual tax receipt lists, and how many partners already have a statement in a print file.
3. The lines under them compare the year's giving with the Blackbaud copy and, for the current year, with the KPI dashboard.
4. Pick a list and press **Make the print file**. A file holds up to the number set in Admin (100 to begin with). Mail goes in ZIP order.
5. Download and print the file, then press **Mark the statements printed**. The next file cannot be made until this one is marked, and no partner is ever put in two files.
6. Repeat until the list says every partner has a statement.

**Throw this print file away** puts its partners back on the list. A second run makes nothing new for a partner already in a file.

## Emailing

Emailing a statement to a partner is built and switched off. An admin turns it on under Admin, Giving statements, and FavorOS asks to confirm because it contacts partners.

## The wording

The **Wording** tab shows every line that prints. **Open a sample statement** shows it on a made-up partner. A FavorOS admin changes any line in Admin, Giving statements.
