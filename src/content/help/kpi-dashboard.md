## Where it is

Open **Reporting** in the menu, then **Executive**. The tab row also has **Highlights**, **Reports** and **Definitions**. Your team's page (RDD team, Partner Care, Church Engagement, Grants or Marketing) opens from the same row. Leadership sees every team. **Definitions** explains how each number is counted. **Print / PDF** at the top right of the Executive page prints the page.

## How fresh it is

Every number rebuilds from Raiser's Edge at **5 a.m. and 5 p.m. Eastern**. The date the numbers are as of shows under the page title. A gift entered at noon shows up after the 5 p.m. rebuild.

## The Executive page

Raised so far this year against the goal, partners at a glance, major partners by largest single gift, revenue by source, partner counts (active, LYBUNT, lapsed, major) and monthly totals, each compared with the same day last year.

## A team tab

- **The team goal** and how much of it is raised, by quarter. The annual goal is spread across the quarters at 22%, 22%, 20% and 36%. From the second quarter on, each quarter's goal is its share of what is still left of the annual goal, so it rises after a slow quarter and falls after a strong one.
- **Revenue so far**, with the part given on the fundraiser's own appeal codes (influenced) and the part that came as monthly payments (recurring).
- **New partners**: what partners gave in the month of their first gift ever.
- On RDD pages, **the portfolio**: the partner records assigned to the RDD in Raiser's Edge today, and upgrades from Partner Care.
- On the Marketing tab, the newsletter results.

## If a number looks wrong

Check Definitions first; most surprises come from how households, soft credits and dates are counted. If it still looks wrong, press the feedback button at the top right of the page and say which number and what you expected. A screenshot helps.
