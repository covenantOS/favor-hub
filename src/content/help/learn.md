Learn is where courses live in FavorOS. A course is a short list of lessons. A learning path puts courses in order for a role or a purpose, such as New hire: Favor, New hire: FavorOS, RDD, Partner Care, Support Team, Grants, Marketing and Admin desk.

## My learning

**My learning** opens first. **Assigned to you** lists courses and paths with their due dates, soonest first, and a late one is marked. Each card shows how many lessons you have finished and a **Start** or **Continue** button. **In progress** lists courses you began on your own, and **Completed** lists what you have finished, with the certificate.

The **Catalog** lists every published course and path. Search by a word in the title or summary, or filter to the ones you have not started, the ones in progress or the ones you finished.

## Taking a course

Press a course to see its lessons in order. **Start** opens the first lesson, and **Continue** opens the lesson where you left off. A video resumes at the second where you stopped, even on another computer or your phone.

A lesson is one of these:

- **Video**: a training video with captions. It counts as done when it ends, or press **Mark complete**.
- **Article**: a help article read right in the lesson.
- **Document**: a Google Doc, Sheet, Slides file or PDF shown in the lesson, with **Open in Google Drive** under it.
- **Reading**: a page written for the course.
- **Clip**: a recorded clip that plays in the lesson.
- **Link**: a web address that opens in a new tab.
- **Quiz**: multiple choice and true or false questions with a pass mark.

Press **Mark complete and continue** on a lesson to move on. On a phone, **Lessons** at the top opens the outline, and Back returns to the course.

## Quizzes

Answer every question and press **Submit answers**. The page shows your score, the pass mark and the questions you missed, each with a short reason. Press **Retake the quiz** as many times as you like. The right answers show once you pass, and your best score is kept. A quiz is finished only by passing it.

## Certificate

When every lesson in a course is done, the course page shows **Course complete** with your certificate. Open it from **Completed** any time and press **Print**. Your supervisor and HR can open it too.

## Assigned learning

When someone assigns you a course or path, it shows on Today under **Learning**, in the menu beside **Learn**, on the bell, and as a task in **Projects** with the due date and a link back to the course. Finishing the course closes the task. You get a notice and an email when it is assigned, three days before it is due, on the day, and weekly while it is late, in the way you chose under **Request email**.

## For admins and HR

**Manage** (in the Learn tabs) is open to hub admins and to people marked HR in Admin > People.

1. **Courses**: **New course**, then add lessons. The add box searches the help library, your clips and Drive together, and also adds a written page, a quiz or a link. **Record a clip** opens the recorder, and the clip becomes a lesson when you stop. Drag lessons into order or use the arrows. A course is a draft until its status is **Published**. A draft previews for authors and records nothing.
2. **Paths**: put published courses in order and name who the path is for.
3. **Assign**: pick a course or a path, then people, a team or everyone, with a due date and an optional note. Giving the same learning again moves its due date and never tells a person twice.
4. **Reports**: who is assigned what, who is done, in progress, not started or late, and quiz scores. Filter by course, path, team or status, and download the list as a CSV. Remove an assignment here.

Admins also choose **Who can use Learn** at the bottom of the Courses tab: admins and HR only, all staff, or off.
