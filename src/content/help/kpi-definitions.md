These match the **Definitions** tab of the KPI dashboard. Favor Brain uses the same definitions, so its answers agree with the dashboard.

## Who counts as a partner

- **Each household counts once**, so spouses linked in Raiser's Edge are one partner.
- **Gifts through a DAF or foundation** count for the partner soft-credited on them.
- **Recurring gifts** count each payment as it comes in.

## Active, LYBUNT and lapsed

The windows run over the 12 months up to the date at the top of the page.

- **Active**: gave in the last 12 months.
- **LYBUNT**: last gave 12 to 24 months ago.
- **Lapsed**: last gave more than 24 months ago.

Households where everyone is deceased or inactive are left out of the LYBUNT, lapsed and major partner counts. Each comparison uses the same count as of this date last year.

## Major partners

- **A major partner** has given $1,000 or more in a single gift. That gift also moves a partner in the United States from Partner Care to an RDD.
- **Partner Care keeps** international partners, with a few exceptions, and partners whose largest single gift is under $1,000, whatever they give monthly.
- **Tiers** go by the largest single gift: $1K to 4,999, $5K to 9,999, $10K to 24,999, $25K to 49,999, $50K to 99,999, $100K to 499,999, $500K to 999,999 and $1M and up.
- **Major partner retention**: of the major partners who gave 12 to 24 months ago, the share who gave again in the last 12 months.
- **Average major partner gift**: the average of all gifts major partners made in the last 12 months. The median sits next to it because a few very large gifts pull the average up.
- **High capacity, not yet major**: an active partner who has never given $1,000 at once and whom iWave rates at $25,000 or more in giving capacity. Only partners with an iWave rating in Raiser's Edge can be counted.

## Revenue by source

- **Non-portfolio major gifts**: gifts coded Z200-NPMG, whoever is credited on them.
- **RDDs**: gifts credited to an RDD, including RDDs who have since left.
- **Grants Team, Partner Care, Church Engagement**: gifts credited to each team's lead. Church Engagement counts gifts from the church partners assigned to the team.
- **Marketing**: gifts on marketing appeals (letters, newsletters, acquisition mailings, media, thank-you packets and similar) that no fundraiser is credited on.
- **Other**: gifts to mission trip appeals and reimbursements.
- **No team credit**: gifts no one is credited on that no line above covers, such as most website and white mail gifts.

A gift shared with another fundraiser counts for the share credited to each. Revenue stays with the RDD credited on each gift, so the year's total includes RDDs who have left, and the RDD team goal adds up each RDD's annual goal for the part of this year they were on the team.
