## The top bar

The top bar has five controls, left to right.

- **Search** (the magnifier) opens search. Ctrl K does the same.
- **The bell** holds your notices, reminders, Quiet hours, Sounds and What's new.
- **Record a clip** (the camera) starts a recording. It shows only if your account can record clips.
- **The ? menu** holds **Help for this page**, **Help home**, **Tour and what is new**, **Feedback** and **Keyboard shortcuts**.
- **Your picture** opens your menu: Expenses, My learning, Evaluations, My clips, Record a clip, Blackbaud, Paycom, Data saver, Sounds and Sign out. My learning and Evaluations show only if you can open them.

Point at any of them for its name. Make a request is in Projects.

## Search from anywhere

Press **Ctrl** and **K** together on any page (**Command** and **K** on a Mac), press **/** when no box is selected, or press the magnifier at the top of the page. Type a few letters: "receipts", "meeting", "LYBUNT", "connect Claude". Use the arrow keys to move and Enter to open. Esc closes it.

With nothing typed, the list shows your recent pages and your pages. After two letters it also searches the records behind them. Results sit in groups:

- **Your pages** and **Other pages**: every FavorOS page, with its area ("Partners › My week"). A page that moved shows where it used to be ("Was Work > Work Center").
- **Actions**: things you can do, such as Log a contact or Make a request.
- **Partners**: partners by name, with city and Blackbaud ID. A row opens the partner page.
- **Requests** and **Projects**: request cards and tasks by title, with status and owner.
- **Reports**, **Tools** and **Menu**: standard reports, tools and menu items.
- **Meetings**: meetings and their notes. **Chat**: messages you can read. **Clips**: your clips by title and by what was said.
- **Favor Brain**: your own Brain chats from the last 30 days.
- **Learn**: courses and learning paths.
- **Help**: help articles.
- **Drive**: files in Favor's shared Drive your Google account can open. A Drive row opens in a new tab.

You see only what your own account may open.

## Cards on every name

Point at a colleague, a partner, a task code like SB-0003 or a request and a card shows its key facts. A partner card shows lifetime giving, last gift and who holds the partner, to people who may open the Work Center. **Send to** on a card puts it in Chat, makes it a task, links it in a request, asks Favor Brain about it or copies the link. On a phone, tap the name.

## Searching the help docs

The search box at the top of **Help docs** searches the full text of every article.

## Keys during the tour

While the tour runs, the right arrow goes forward, the left arrow goes back, and Esc ends it.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** anywhere outside a text box to see every shortcut for FavorOS and for the page you are on. The same sheet opens from **?** in the top bar, then **Keyboard shortcuts**. Mail and Favor Brain keep their own lists, and **?** on those pages opens them.

These work on every page.

| Keys | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac) | Search |
| ? | The shortcut sheet |
| Ctrl Z (Cmd Z) | Undo the last change that offered Undo |
| G then a letter | Go to an area: G T Today, G P Partners, G G Gifts, G J Projects, G S Staff, and so on for the areas in your menu |
| [ or ] | Previous or next tab |
| \ | Narrow or widen the side menu |
| Alt T | Go to the latest message at the bottom of the page |
| Esc | Close a menu or sheet |

In Projects, J and K move through tasks, Enter opens one, Space marks it done, N adds a task, X selects it, D sets the due date, A assigns it and F2 renames it. In Chat, Enter sends and Shift Enter starts a new line.

## Undo

When a message at the bottom of the page has an **Undo** button, press Ctrl Z (Cmd Z on a Mac) to press it. It works while the message is on screen. Inside a text box, Ctrl Z undoes your typing as it always did.

## Select a range

In a list of checkboxes, tick one box, hold **Shift** and tick another. Every box between the two takes the same state. This works in Receipts, My partners, Evaluations, Lists and the Projects admin pages.

## Right-click menus

Right-click a thing to see what you can do with it, where you clicked.

- **A task in Projects**, in a list or on the board: Open, Mark done or Reopen, Due date, Assign, Priority, Rename and Select.
- **A link in the side menu, the tab row or the phone bar**: Open, Open in a new tab, Copy link, and Pin where the link can be pinned.
- **A Chat message**: the message's own menu opens at the message, with Reply, Copy link, Pin, Edit and Delete as they apply to you.

On a phone, press and hold a link in the menu or a Chat message for the same menu. A task in Projects has no hold menu because holding a task picks it up to move it. From a keyboard, put focus on the thing and press the Menu key or Shift F10.

Hold **Shift** while you right-click to get your browser's own menu. A right-click inside a text box or on selected text also keeps the browser's menu.

## Sounds

FavorOS has one set of short, quiet sounds. Open **Sounds** from the bell or from your picture menu.

- **On** plays every sound, including a soft click when you press a main button, flip a switch or close something.
- **Subtle** is the starting setting. It plays only what reaches you and what you finish: a call or huddle ringing, a mention, a direct message, a new notice, someone joining or leaving your meeting, someone waiting to be let in, a recording starting or stopping, a message sent, a task or step done, and an error. It leaves out button and switch clicks and plays at 70 percent of the volume.
- **Off** plays nothing. A ringing call still shows its card, and a phone still vibrates.

The **Volume** slider sets the loudness. Under **Listen**, the play button beside each sound lets you hear it. On a phone that can vibrate, **Vibration** turns the light tap on or off.

**Quiet hours** in the bell silences the sounds that come from other people (calls, mentions, direct messages and notices). On an iPhone the ring and silent switch mutes the sounds. The choice is kept on that device and browser, so set it again on a second computer.

If your computer is set to reduce motion, FavorOS drops its animations too.
