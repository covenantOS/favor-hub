## Search from anywhere

Press **Ctrl** and **K** together on any page (**Command** and **K** on a Mac), or press **Search** at the top of the menu. Type a few letters: "receipts", "mileage", "LYBUNT", "connect Claude". Use the arrow keys to move and Enter to open. Esc closes it.

Search covers every hub tool, the outside tools (Blackbaud and Paycom), every help topic and every training video.

## Searching the help docs

The search box at the top of **Help docs** searches the full text of every article and every video's captions, so a word spoken in a video finds that video.

## Keys during the tour

While the tour runs, the right arrow goes forward, the left arrow goes back, and Esc ends it.

## Sounds

The hub makes a soft sound when you press a main button or when something finishes, like a note sending. It never plays on its own or when you move the mouse. To turn sounds off, press the **speaker** beside your name at the bottom of the menu. Press it again to turn them back on. The choice is kept on that computer.

If your computer is set to reduce motion, the hub drops its animations too.
