## Search from anywhere

Press **Ctrl** and **K** together on any page (**Command** and **K** on a Mac), or press **Search** at the top of the menu. Type a few letters: "receipts", "mileage", "LYBUNT", "connect Claude". Use the arrow keys to move and Enter to open. Esc closes it.

Search covers every FavorOS tool, the outside tools (Blackbaud and Paycom), and every help topic.

## Cards on every name

Point at a colleague, a partner, a task code like SB-0003 or a request and a card shows its key facts. A partner card shows lifetime giving, last gift and who holds the partner, to people who may open the Work Center. **Send to** on a card puts it in Chat, makes it a task, links it in a request, asks Favor Brain about it or copies the link. On a phone, tap the name.

## Searching the help docs

The search box at the top of **Help docs** searches the full text of every article.

## Keys during the tour

While the tour runs, the right arrow goes forward, the left arrow goes back, and Esc ends it.

## Sounds

FavorOS makes a soft sound when you press a main button or when something finishes, like a note sending. It never plays on its own or when you move the mouse. To turn sounds off, press the **speaker** beside your name at the bottom of the menu. Press it again to turn them back on. The choice is kept on that computer.

If your computer is set to reduce motion, FavorOS drops its animations too.
