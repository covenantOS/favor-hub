## Search from anywhere

Press **Ctrl** and **K** together on any page (**Command** and **K** on a Mac), press **/** when no box is selected, or press the magnifier at the top of the page. Type a few letters: "receipts", "meeting", "LYBUNT", "connect Claude". Use the arrow keys to move and Enter to open. Esc closes it.

With nothing typed, the list shows your recent pages and your pages. After two letters it also searches the records behind them. Results sit in groups:

- **Your pages** and **Other pages**: every FavorOS page, with its area ("Partners › My week"). A page that moved shows where it used to be ("Was Work > Work Center").
- **Actions**: things you can do, such as Log a contact or Make a request.
- **Partners**: partners by name, with city and Blackbaud ID. A row opens the partner page.
- **Requests** and **Projects**: request cards and tasks by title, with status and owner.
- **Reports**, **Tools** and **Menu**: standard reports, tools and menu items.
- **Meetings**: meetings and their notes. **Chat**: messages you can read. **Clips**: your clips by title and by what was said.
- **Favor Brain**: your own Brain chats from the last 30 days.
- **Learn**: courses and learning paths.
- **Help**: help articles.
- **Drive**: files in Favor's shared Drive your Google account can open. A Drive row opens in a new tab.

You see only what your own account may open.

## Cards on every name

Point at a colleague, a partner, a task code like SB-0003 or a request and a card shows its key facts. A partner card shows lifetime giving, last gift and who holds the partner, to people who may open the Work Center. **Send to** on a card puts it in Chat, makes it a task, links it in a request, asks Favor Brain about it or copies the link. On a phone, tap the name.

## Searching the help docs

The search box at the top of **Help docs** searches the full text of every article.

## Keys during the tour

While the tour runs, the right arrow goes forward, the left arrow goes back, and Esc ends it.

## Sounds

FavorOS makes a soft sound when you press a main button or when something finishes, like a note sending. It never plays on its own or when you move the mouse. To turn sounds off, open the menu under your initials at the bottom of the left menu and press **Sounds**. Press it again to turn them back on. The choice is kept on that computer.

If your computer is set to reduce motion, FavorOS drops its animations too.
