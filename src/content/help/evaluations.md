Evaluations run twice a year. The purpose is growth, accountability and encouragement. The form says so in its introduction: it is not designed primarily to decide compensation or advancement.

## The three weeks

**Week 1.** Complete your self-assessment (Form A). It has 74 questions in seven sections, and every answer saves as you go, so you can stop and come back. Every rating is required. About 17 of the written answers are required and the rest are optional. A rating of 1 or 2 needs an example. Then name 3 to 5 people who work with you, from the staff list. They are asked by email as soon as you save.

**Week 2.** Each person you named writes their own feedback (Form B, 50 questions). Your supervisor reads your self-assessment and the feedback side by side, with your average and the others' average for each competency. There is no overall score. They look for themes that repeat and write a summary with a six-month development plan (Form C).

**Week 3.** You and your supervisor meet. After the meeting you read the summary and the plan, add a note if you want one recorded, and acknowledge. HR signs. The signed evaluation is filed as a Google Doc in your folder in the US Executive Drive and you can download it as a PDF. Your goals become tasks in Projects, and your supervisor reviews them at the next evaluation.

## The scale

5 Exceptional. 4 Strong. 3 Meets Expectations. 2 Developing. 1 Needs Significant Improvement. N/O Not Observed, for a question you have not seen enough to judge. Choose N/O instead of guessing.

## Who can read what

- Your self-assessment: you, your supervisor, HR and FavorOS administrators.
- A reviewer's feedback: not anonymous. The reviewer's name is on it. Your supervisor, HR and FavorOS administrators read it. You do not read it word for word. Your supervisor summarizes the themes for you.
- A reviewer reads only the form they wrote.
- Your summary and plan: you, once your supervisor shares it, plus your supervisor, HR and FavorOS administrators.
- None of this appears in Chat, search, Favor Brain or the dashboards.

## If you are asked for feedback

Open Evaluations, then **Feedback I owe**. Write about what you have seen yourself, with an example when you can. Choose **Not Observed** for anything you have not seen. If you cannot give feedback, tell the employee or their supervisor so they can ask someone else.

## For supervisors

My team lists each person who reports to you with the next step and its due date. Open a person to see Feedback, Compare and Summary and plan. Compare shows self against others for each competency and flags the ones that differ by a point or more. If fewer than three feedback forms arrive, you can go ahead with what you have after you confirm.

## For HR and administrators

Evaluations, then **Manage terms** opens a term: the date week 1 starts, and the people. People who started January to June are picked first. Each person's supervisor and start date come from Admin, People and roles. The same page shows who is behind, lets you change a supervisor by drop-down, and names the folder where signed evaluations are filed.
