Evaluations run twice a year. The purpose is growth, accountability and encouragement. The form says so in its introduction: it is not designed primarily to decide compensation or advancement.

## The two weeks

**Week 1.** HR opens a cycle and everyone is emailed at once. You complete your self-evaluation (Form A). It has 74 questions in seven sections, and every answer saves as you go, so you can stop and come back. Every rating is required. About 17 of the written answers are required and the rest are optional. A rating of 1 or 2 needs an example. When you submit, you name two or three people who work with you, and your supervisor is always one of them. Each person gets an email with their own link to the feedback form (Form B, 50 questions). If someone cannot give feedback they decline, with a note if they like, and you pick a replacement.

**Week 2.** Review meetings. HR schedules them ahead of time, with the supervisor and one leadership member joining you on Zoom. Your supervisor reads every answer in Evaluations as the forms come in and exports your self-evaluation and each reviewer's answers to separate Google Sheets, with the reviewer's name beside every answer. Leadership combines them. FavorOS does not write the evaluation. After the meeting your supervisor fills in the Employee Evaluation and Development Summary, shares it with you in Google Docs, and attaches the link in Evaluations. You read it, add a note if you want one recorded, and acknowledge. HR signs.

## The scale

5 Exceptional. 4 Strong. 3 Meets Expectations. 2 Developing. 1 Needs Significant Improvement. N/O Not Observed, for a question you have not seen enough to judge. Choose N/O instead of guessing.

## Who can read what

- Your self-evaluation: you, your supervisor, HR and FavorOS administrators.
- A reviewer's feedback: not anonymous. The reviewer's name is on it. Your supervisor, HR and FavorOS administrators read it. You do not read it word for word.
- A reviewer reads only the form they wrote.
- The leadership member in your meeting sees the date, time and Zoom link. Your supervisor shares the answers with them.
- Your final evaluation: you, once your supervisor shares it, plus your supervisor, HR and FavorOS administrators.
- None of this appears in Chat, search, Favor Brain or the dashboards.

## If you are asked for feedback

Open Evaluations, then **Feedback I owe**. Write about what you have seen yourself, with an example when you can. Choose **Not Observed** for anything you have not seen. If you cannot give feedback, open the form and choose **Decline to give feedback**.

## For supervisors

My team lists each person who reports to you with the next step and its due date. Open a person to see Feedback, Compare and Meeting and final evaluation. Each form that arrives sends you an email with a link. **Export the answers** makes a Google Sheet in your own Drive for the self-evaluation and one for each reviewer. The first time, Google asks you to allow FavorOS to make Sheets for you.

On the Meeting and final evaluation tab you find the supervisor instructions, the final evaluation form (it opens as a copy), and the box where you paste the link to the finished document.

## For HR and administrators

Every evaluation needs a supervisor, and that comes from **Reports to** in Admin, People and roles. When someone has nobody there, Admin, Settings, People, Evaluations holds a fallback list of person and supervisor. A line in People and roles always wins. FavorOS never picks a manager for you.

Evaluations, then **Manage terms** opens a term: the date week 1 starts, how many days each week lasts, and the people. Everyone is emailed at once. The term page shows who has gone through each step, lets you change a supervisor by drop-down, and opens each person's page to schedule the review meeting: the day, the time, the leadership member and the Zoom link. The same settings hold the links to the supervisor instructions and the final evaluation form.
