All partners is a tab under **Partners**. It holds every partner record in Blackbaud, including people who have not given yet.

## Find partners

Type a name, email, phone number, lookup ID, street, city or ZIP in the search box. The buttons under it narrow the list by kind: Individuals, Households, Organizations, Churches and Foundations, each with its count. A second row filters by status (Active, LYBUNT, Lapsed or Never gave), by holder, by state and by lifetime giving. Filters opens more: codes, solicit codes and tags.

Point at a status badge, a Household or a Passes on figure to read what it means. Lifetime giving counts the way the KPI dashboard does, so a gift sent through a DAF counts for the partner it names, and a partner and spouse are one household.

Sort by any column heading. Columns lets you pick which columns show. The list pages through 25, 50 or 100 rows at a time. Press a partner's name to open the partner page.

## Save a view

Once you have filters you use often, open Views and save them. Save a view for yourself, or for everyone. A saved view comes back the way you left it.

## Star, tag and export

Star a partner to find them again. Select one or many partners and the bar at the bottom adds a tag to all of them. Tags are FavorOS labels that everyone can see and filter by here. They do not go to Blackbaud.

Export downloads the partners that match as a CSV file. Sheets sends the list to a Google Sheet only you can open.

## Recently opened

The Recently opened tab lists the partners you opened lately. It follows you to any device.

## Duplicates and merges

The Duplicates and merges tab shows how many pairs of records look like one partner. Open the queue takes you to Data care, where you line the two records up and merge them. The Data care article explains each step.
