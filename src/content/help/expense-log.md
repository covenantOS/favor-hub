## When a request comes in

You get an email with a link, and the request shows on Today and beside **Expense log** in the menu.

1. Open the link or press the request in the **Expense log**.
2. Read the trip details, each expense, the total and the reason.
3. Sign by typing or drawing your name.
4. Press **Approve & sign** to send the signed copy to the requester and the distribution list, or **Decline** with a short reason.

Requests that have waited a week or more show in gold on Today.

## The log

The expense log lists every request with its status, document number, requester, amount, approver and the signed PDF. Use the filter chips and the search box to find a name or an EXP number.

## Log settings (hub admin)

Under **Log settings**: the default approver and the distribution list, the mileage rate and the per-trip deduction, out-of-office substitutes with their dates, and who else can open the log.
