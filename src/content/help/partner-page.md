## Open a partner

Press any partner's name in FavorOS: a Work Center row, a Favor Brain table, a household member. The partner opens beside your work. Hover a name for a quick look first. Find a partner at the top of every page searches by name, email, phone, street address or lookup id; press Enter to open the first match. Open full page shows the same view at full width, at its own address you can bookmark or send.

## The top

The name, the household, who holds the partner, and the iWave rating sit at the top, with any do-not-call, do-not-email or do-not-mail marks. Call, Text and Email open your phone or mail app. Log a contact records a call, email, meeting or mailing for the partner's holder, with Thanked or another tag if you pick one. Task adds something to do with its due date. Note adds a note to the partner record in Blackbaud.

The four figures below are lifetime giving, this year (with the last 12 months), the last gift and the largest gift. They count the way the KPI dashboard and Favor Brain count:

- A gift sent through a donor-advised fund (DAF), a foundation or a business and soft-credited to the partner counts for the partner. A partner who gives only through National Christian Foundation shows every one of those gifts, and the line under the figures reads "All through National Christian Foundation (DAF)".
- The DAF or foundation does not count those gifts as its own. Its page shows them as gifts it passed on for others.
- A partner and the spouse linked to them in Blackbaud are one household, so either page shows the gifts of both, once. Household giving, under the figures, lists each person's lifetime, this year, last 12 months and last gift. When a gift is soft-credited to both spouses, the line "Credited to both, counted once" shows the amount taken off so the household total counts that gift one time. The table below it gives each year for each person and for the household.
- Active means a gift in the last 12 months. LYBUNT means the last gift was 12 to 24 months ago. Lapsed means more than 24 months. A major partner has given one gift of $1,000 or more.

A word with a dotted underline, such as Soft credit, DAF or LYBUNT, shows what it means when you point at it or tap it. In Giving by year, the shaded part of a bar is giving that came through a DAF, foundation or business.

## Notes

The Notes section sits under Due and open. It lists every note on the partner record in Blackbaud, newest first, with its topic (General, Prayer, Giving or Family), who wrote it and the date. A long note shows its first lines, and Read all opens the rest.

Add a note, or the Note button at the top, opens one form. Pick the topic, write a summary and the note, and press Save note. The note saves to the partner record in Blackbaud and shows In Blackbaud with Undo for 24 hours. Tick Show at the top as an instruction for a do-not-contact request or any rule the next caller must see. The newest instruction sits in red under the partner's name and on the Call prep screen. Topics other than General lead the note's summary, so Prayer: Pray for the surgery reads the same in Blackbaud.

## Due and open, then the timeline

Due and open lists the partner's open actions. Tick one to complete it, or press its date to move it. Press its name to open it in the Work Center with every field.

The Giving pattern card at the top of the page shows how the partner gives, the date of the last gift, when the next one is expected, their health and the next step. Health is Growing, Steady, Slipping, Lapsing, At risk or New. It comes from the last two years of gifts: how often they give, whether a gift is overdue, how the last 12 months compare with the 12 before and whether anyone has called. Press Call mode on the next step, or in the buttons under the name, to open Call mode.

Call mode puts the script, the giving and the last notes on one screen with a Log it button. Press Speak it, or type, to say what happened. Write it up turns that into the summary line and notes, and a follow-up task if you said you would do something later. Press Log it to save a completed phone call on the partner. Undo stays for 24 hours.

The timeline mixes gifts, calls, emails, meetings, notes and events, newest first. Chat messages that name the partner, FavorOS meetings about them and your own clips appear in it too. All, Gifts, Contacts and Chat and meetings narrow it.

## Communication preferences

A partner with a do-not-contact mark shows a red strip under the name. It lists each channel that is off (Do not call, Do not email, Do not mail, Do not text) and the reason: a solicit code such as Do Not Call or Do Not Contact, the Requests no email or No valid address flag on the Record tab, a Do not mail mark on the preferred address, or a Do not call or Do not email mark on every phone or email. A narrower code, such as Do Not Solicit or Do Not Mail Thank You, shows as a line in plain words. Deceased shows alone. The same strip appears in Call mode, on Call prep, and inside Log a contact when you pick Phone call, Email or Mailing.

The Communication preferences section lower on the page lists Call, Email, Mail and Text with the reason beside each. Admins and the Support Team have a switch on each row. Turning one on adds the matching solicit code (Do Not Call, Do Not Email, Do Not Mail or Do Not Text) with today's date; turning one off ends the codes that cause it. A row caused by a mark on a phone, email or address says Edit on Contact and opens the Contact tab. Every change saves to Blackbaud and shows Undo for 24 hours. Email codes reach GoHighLevel's Do Not Disturb at the next morning run, the same run that applies Blackbaud's Do Not Email codes.

## Further down

Giving by year shows the last eight years; this year is gold. Opportunities lists each ask with its status; press the pencil to change it, or New opportunity to add one. Contact details, codes and assignments are folded at the bottom as a summary; Edit beside a phone, email or the address opens the Contact tab.

## Gifts, Actions and Relationships

Seven tabs sit under the Call, Text and Email buttons: Overview, Gifts, Actions, Relationships, Contact, Codes and Record. The first four read the record's history.

**Gifts** lists every gift page by page, newest first. Date, Amount and Type sort when you press the heading. The columns are Date, Amount, Type, Fund, Appeal, Counts as, Method and Thanked. Counts as says how each gift counts. Own means the gift is on this record. Soft credit means another record sent the gift and Favor's rule credits it here, and the line beside it names that record. Passed on means this record sent the gift for other partners, and it names them. Point at Soft credit or Passed on to read the meaning. Own and soft credit gifts count toward the partner's giving. Recurring gifts lists each schedule with how often it runs, the next charge and a Missed a charge mark when the next charge date has passed. Change a schedule on the Recurring page under Gifts. When Blackbaud has an ask suggestion for the partner, it shows above the list.

A gift that honors or remembers someone shows "In honor of" or "In memory of" and the name under its fund, and the Tributes list names each tribute with its gift date and who to tell. FavorOS keeps these, and they show to the people who can open Other gifts. A tribute you enter on Other gifts appears here once the gift reaches Blackbaud, and tributes written in a gift comment in Blackbaud are copied in.

**Actions** lists every action on the record, open ones first. All, Open and Contacts narrow it. Each row has the date, status, category, type, summary and fundraiser.

**Relationships** lists the people and organizations linked to the partner, with the relationship, what they are to the partner, their position and the date it started. Under it are the contact details on file, the solicit codes, constituent codes, attributes and ratings, and the tags FavorOS holds for the partner. Add a tag here. Tags are FavorOS labels that everyone can see and filter by in All partners. They do not go to Blackbaud.

## Contact, Codes and Record

Contact, Codes and Record read Blackbaud live each time you open them, so they show what is in Blackbaud now, including ended addresses and ended codes.

**Contact** lists every address, phone and email. An address shows its type, a Preferred mark, Do not mail, the dates it was in use and, for a seasonal address, the window ("Every year May 1 to Oct 15"). Edit opens the row in place. Add an address, Add a phone and Add an email open a blank form. A new preferred address or phone takes the mark from the old one, and the tab checks that exactly one row holds it. End this address records the last day it was in use. Mark inactive keeps a phone or email on the record and stops it counting. A seasonal address keeps its type; end it and add the new address instead. The three record flags (Gives anonymously, Requests no email, No valid address) sit at the bottom. If someone changed the same field in Blackbaud since you opened the form, the form stops and shows their value with Use theirs and Keep mine.

**Codes** lists the constituent codes and the solicit codes. Partner and Prospect carry a Morning run mark. The morning run owns them, so they are not added or ended here, and choosing Partner in Add a code shows the gifts check first: a record whose gifts are all soft-credited to someone else stays a Prospect. Church and the other codes are added with a start date and ended with End. Add a solicit code picks from Favor's list, such as Do Not Call or Do Not Solicit, and End stamps today. Ended codes fold under "ended", and Reopen puts one back.

**Record** marks a partner Active, Inactive or Deceased. Deceased and Inactive show what goes with it: set the flag (always), end the Partner code and the assignments on that date, add the Do Not Solicit solicit code, and cancel open actions you pick. A household shows Who, and the other person stays active; a note can go on their record. An organization is marked inactive only. Mark active reverses the flag and reopens the codes that ended with it. The assignments stay ended for the morning run to settle the next day.

Marking deceased also reaches the places outside Blackbaud that would still contact the person. The FavorOS copy of the record updates at once. The GoHighLevel contact, found by the record's emails, gets Do Not Disturb on every channel, the tag deceased and a removal from the published workflows. The giving portal stops emailing the record and refuses its sign-in. Reminders FavorOS holds for the partner close, and the partner's open asks leave Asks. Mark active turns off the Do Not Disturb FavorOS set, removes the tag and lifts the portal block; an unsubscribe the person made earlier stays. Each of these writes a row to the Audit log under Records.

Admins change contact details, codes and record status. The Support Team changes codes and record status, and contact details for any partner. Directors and Partner Care change contact details for partners their team holds. Everyone else reads.

## Add a partner

Add partner sits in the Work Center's tool row, and on Find a partner. It is for admins and the Support Team. The form takes three kinds of record.

**Individual** takes a title, first, middle and last name, a suffix, a phone with its type, an email and an address. **Household** adds a second person with their own title, names, phone and email, and joins the two as spouses; both get the address. **Organization** takes the name, a type (Church, DAF Provider, Foundation and the like), and a main contact. The contact is a person with their own record, joined to the organization with what they are to it (Employee, Staff Member, Pastor, Board Member or Member) and their position.

Records already in Blackbaud show beside the form as you type: FavorOS's copy at once, then Blackbaud's own search a moment after you stop. Each shows why it matches, such as the same phone, the same email, the same name and city. An organization never matches on its name alone. Open this record opens it in a new tab. Add as new partner stays locked until you tick **None of these is the same person**, and changing a name, phone, email or city clears the tick. The server checks again when you press Add.

Add a relationship links the new partner to someone already in Blackbaud: pick the partner, then what they are to the new one (Parent, Child, Sibling, Friend, Grandparent, Grandchild, Mentor or Mentee). Code is Prospect or Partner. Held by comes from the state on the address through the Regions table; with no state, or a state no region lists, it shows Partner Care, and you can pick anyone. The source code, addressee and salutation come from the record review the next weekday, as they do now. A step that does not save, such as a link, comes back by name so you can finish it in Blackbaud.

## Copy and share

Press a phone number, an email or the address to copy it. A Copied note confirms it. Copy link in the top row copies the address of this partner page. The link opens for signed-in staff who already have access to the partner. For anyone else the page shows an error.

Click into Find a partner with nothing typed and it lists Recent partners, the last eight you opened. That list stays in this browser only.

## Saving and Undo

Each change shows at once and saves to Blackbaud in the background. After a save, the Contact, Codes and Record tabs read Blackbaud again and show what it kept. If Blackbaud answers yes and keeps something different, the row says so in plain words. The message at the bottom has Undo for 24 hours. Gifts come from the copy of Blackbaud that refreshes at 5 AM and 5 PM.
