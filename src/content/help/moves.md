## What is on the page

Each column is a stage: Identify, Qualify, Cultivate, Ask, Close and Steward. A card is a major partner, which means a household with one gift of $1,000 or more at any time, or any partner with an open ask. The card shows the amount asked, the last contact, the fundraiser who holds the partner and the next step with its due date.

The first column, **Not staged**, holds major partners nobody has placed yet. Drag one into a stage, or press the card and pick the stage in the side panel. A partner with an open ask on the Asks board starts in Ask until someone moves them.

The numbers across the top add up to the Asks board. Open asks here is the same total as Open there, because the board reads the same asks for the same portfolio.

## Moving a partner and setting the next step

Drag a card to another column, or open the card and press a stage. The next step is a sentence and a date. Saving it puts a reminder on the owner's list in Actions, so it shows up on the day it is due. Changing the step replaces the old reminder.

Every move and every next step is kept in the card's history.

## Who sees what

Directors see the partners they hold. The Support Team sees the partners of the directors it supports. Admins see every partner and can filter by fundraiser. Admins also use **Edit stages** to rename, reorder, add or remove stages and to choose which stage takes partners with an open ask. A stage with partners in it cannot be removed until they are moved.

## List

**List** shows the same partners as a table, 25 to a page, with the stage in a column. Use it to find one partner among hundreds or to page through the Not staged column.

## Relationship map

The side panel and the **Relationships** tab on a partner record show who is linked to the partner: spouse and family, pastor and church, employer, board members and foundation contacts. Each person shows what they have given by the same rule as every other page, and a major partner is marked. Press a name to open that record.

**Add a relationship** finds the other partner, asks what they are to this one and sends it to Blackbaud. If Blackbaud turns it down, the relationship stays in FavorOS and the map labels it FavorOS only.
