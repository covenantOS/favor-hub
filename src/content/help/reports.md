Reports lists every report your team runs. You see the reports your role uses today.

## Open a report

Press a report's name. The name sits once, at the top of the page. Use the filters to choose dates, categories or states. The tiles and the table change with them, and the address keeps your filters so you can bookmark a view.

Press a column heading to sort. Type in **Search these rows** to narrow the table. The totals row follows what you search. Press a total to open a side panel with its Favor definition and the rows it adds up. It opens when the whole list is on the page and nothing is searched.

## Check against the KPI dashboard

The line under the tiles names the KPI dashboard figure the report overlaps and shows both numbers. **Matches** means they are equal to the cent. **Differs by** shows the gap. **Not on the KPI dashboard** means the dashboard has no figure for this report.

The two address lists, Appeal mailing list and Annual tax receipt lists, add a second line that names the saved Blackbaud query they replace (795 and 1066) and compares partners and dollars with that query's own output on the day it was run. It shows only when your filters are the ones the query was run with. A difference is listed with its count.

## Get the rows out

- **Export CSV** downloads the rows and the totals row exactly as the page shows them.
- **Open in Google Sheets** makes a private sheet in your Drive. The first time, Google asks once for permission.
- **Copy for WhatsApp** appears on reports someone posts by hand, such as the Daily Revenue Report. It copies the post text in the order and wording of the post you make today.

Reports do not send email. Whoever posts or sends a report today keeps doing it, with these buttons to prepare it.

## Mail files

Appeal mailing list and Annual tax receipt lists (Mail list) have **Mail file CSV** and **Mail file to Sheets**. The columns, their names and the way values are written match the Blackbaud export: Primary Salutation and Primary Addressee as Blackbaud prints them, the state spelled out, the last gift date as 10/1/2026 12:00:00 AM, and QRECID. The sheet is made in US Team Files, in the Hub mail files folder, and the button turns into a link to it. Everyone with access to US Team Files can open it.

For a tax receipt list, a partner whose only address is marked do not mail stays on the Mail list, because a receipt is not a solicitation. Only a partner Blackbaud marks as having no valid address is held back, and that partner shows under No receipt with the reason. The appeal list leaves a do-not-mail address out.

## Typed-in numbers

A few reports hold a number Blackbaud does not have, such as Africa income from QuickBooks or the mailed count for an appeal. Those cells are text boxes. Type the number, press Tab, and the totals update.

## Lists that replace saved queries

- **Yesterday's gifts** replaces the Constituents for Actions queries Partner Care runs each morning. It lists every gift since the last list (Monday reaches back over the weekend) with who holds the partner and the partner's last action. Set the amount, the gift type and who holds the partner, or pick dates to see any week. The $5,000 and up gifts by director list comes from the same page with the amount set to $5,000 and up and Held by set to A director.
- **Top partners by total giving** replaces the Top Givers queries. Pick the cut-off and the years it counts.
- **RDD and church engagement actions** replaces RDD Action Reporting. One row for each completed action with the tags typed on it, for any dates.
- **Gifts with no fundraiser credit** replaces the three credit checks: gifts with no credit, major gifts with no credit and Partner Care's personal contact gifts.

Each page says on its tie-out line how its count compares with the gifts table, and the query map names the saved query each one was checked against.

## Where each Blackbaud query went

The lookup at the bottom of Reports lists the Blackbaud queries run in 2026 with the query number and what replaced each: a report here, the Work Center, Favor Brain, or the morning run. Pick **Run in the last 120 days** to see the queries staff still run. Type a query name to find it.
